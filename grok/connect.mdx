Ryze AI is listed in Grok Bot's app directory. Search for it, click **Add**, and sign in. You don't need to add a custom MCP server or paste a URL.

Once connected, you can ask Grok Bot about your Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, Search Console and store data in plain language.

## Add Ryze AI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connect Apps">
    In Grok Bot, open **Connect Apps**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/grok-step-1.png" alt="The Connect Apps window in Grok Bot" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for Ryze AI and click Add">
    Type `Ryze AI` in **Search across apps and skills**, open **Ryze AI** (developer: Ryze AI), and click **Add**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/grok-step-2.png" alt="The Ryze AI connector page in Grok Bot with the Add button" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and pick workspaces">
    Your browser opens Ryze. Sign in if asked, choose which workspaces Grok Bot can access (or **All workspaces**), then click **Allow connection**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/grok-step-3.png" alt="The Ryze authorization page: pick workspaces and allow the connection" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Grok Bot">
    You'll see **Authorization complete!** Click **Open Grok Bot** in the browser prompt, or **Return to Grok Bot**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/grok-step-4.png" alt="Authorization complete, with the prompt to open Grok Bot" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The Ryze AI page in Grok Bot now shows your account as **Connected** and lists the Ryze tools Grok Bot can call.
    <Frame><img src="/images/grok-step-5.png" alt="Ryze AI connected in Grok Bot, with its tools enabled" /></Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try it

Start a **new chat** in Grok Bot and ask for what you want:

> Pull the last 7 days of Google Ads spend and break it down by campaign.

Grok Bot uses Ryze tools to answer.

<Tip>
Start a **new chat** after connecting or reconnecting. Existing chats may not pick up the connection.
</Tip>

## Added Ryze as a custom MCP server before?

You don't need it anymore. Uninstall the custom server and add **Ryze AI** from **Connect Apps** instead, so you don't have two copies of the same tools.
