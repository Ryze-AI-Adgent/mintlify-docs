Open **AI Marketer → Templates** in the sidebar. Each template is a ready prompt for the agent, built on your workspace's live data.

## Find a template

- Tabs across the top: **All**, **Monitor**, **Create**, **Optimize**, **Automate**, **Report**.
- Platform filters on the right — Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Search Console and others — narrow the list to templates for that platform. Click a filter again to clear it.

## Three kinds of templates

**Task templates** — click the card (or its arrow) and the agent panel opens and starts working on that prompt right away. This uses credits like any message (see [Credits & Usage](/agent/credits)). To change the prompt first, click the copy icon, paste the prompt into **New chat**, edit it, then send.

**Deck templates** — presentation-style reports in the **Report** section: **Monthly Client Report**, **Marketing Audit**, **Quarterly Business Review**, **Creative Performance Review**, **SEO Audit**, **Post-Click Teardown**, **Competitor Landscape**, **Media Plan & Forecast** and **New Client Proposal**. Open one and click **Build with agent**: the agent pulls this workspace's live data and builds your version in [Reports](/agent/reports). **Copy prompt** copies the brief so you can adjust it.

**Dashboards** (the **Monitor** tab) — for example **Organic Traffic Overview**, **AI Traffic Overview**, **Paid Ads Overview**, **All Accounts Overview**. Click **Use template** to create the dashboard in Reports once the platforms it needs are connected. If a required source isn't connected yet, the button says **Connect …** and takes you to [Integrations](/integrations/overview).

## Not sure where to start?

Open a **New chat**. In the empty chat, pick an action chip — **Launch a campaign**, **Make creatives**, **Build a deck**, **Design a dashboard** or **Automate routine** — to see matching templates. For campaigns, decks, dashboards and automations, the first card (**Plan a campaign with me**, **Plan a deck with me**, **Design a dashboard with me**, **Plan automations with me**) makes the agent interview you first — your goal, budget and audience, who the deck is for, what you check every week — and then build it with you. See [Chat with the AI Marketer](/agent/chat).

Want a template to run on its own every week? Copy its prompt into a [scheduled task](/agent/schedules).
