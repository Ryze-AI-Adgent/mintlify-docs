Reports are write-ups your AI marketer builds from your live data — spend by channel, ROAS, conversions, trends, an executive summary. Instead of a wall of chat, you get a structured page you can share.

Find them under **Reports** in the sidebar, in the **AI Marketer** group.

## Two kinds of reports

- **Deck** — a slide-style report with a fixed snapshot of the numbers. Download it as a PDF, email it, or share it by link.
- **Dashboard** — a live view that pulls fresh data from your connected accounts. Click **Refresh data** to update it. Dashboards are shared by link only — they can't be exported to PDF.

## Generate a report

Click **Generate Report** and you land in a chat with the agent. It tells you what kinds of reports it can build, asks what to cover, then pulls the data and builds it.

Want a starting point? Click **View templates** to pick a ready-made deck or dashboard. See [Templates](/agent/templates).

You don't have to use the button — just ask the agent for a report in any chat ("put together a May performance report"). When it has the numbers, it builds the report and gives you a link to open it.

<Note>
The agent only builds the report once it has real data from your connected accounts — it won't invent metrics. Connect your ad accounts and analytics first so it has something to pull.
</Note>

## Work with a report

From the top of a report you can:

- **Download PDF** (decks) — render the report to a clean, branded PDF.
- **Refresh data** (dashboards) — pull the latest numbers.
- **Share** — see below.
- **Update Report** — opens a chat where the agent sums up the report's sections and asks what to update: fresh numbers, specific sections, or something else.

To rename or delete a report, use the menu on its row in the Reports list.

## Share a report

Click **Share**. Decks offer two options; dashboards go straight to the link.

### Share via Email (decks)

Enter one or more addresses, separated by commas, and click **Send**. Each recipient gets the report as a PDF attachment.

- **Also add recipients to this workspace** — adds each recipient as a member of this workspace so they can sign in and see your reports directly. Leave it off to just send the PDF without giving access.

<Note>
Adding someone to the workspace gives them access to everything in it, not just this report.
</Note>

### Share by Link

- **Anyone with the link** — turn it on and anyone with the link can open the report, no sign-in needed. With it off, only workspace members can open it.
- **Copy link** — copy the link to send it anywhere.
- **Reset link** — create a new link; the old one stops working.
- You can also invite people by email from the same dialog — they join this workspace with full access.

<Tip>
Share a dashboard once it has finished loading — the link shares the views that were open at that moment.
</Tip>
