Reports are write-ups your AI marketer builds from your live data — spend by channel, ROAS, conversions, a trend chart, an executive summary. Instead of a wall of chat, you get a structured document with its own page that you can download or send on.

Find them under **Reports** in the sidebar.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/reports-page.png" alt="The Reports page listing reports built by the agent" />
</Frame>

## Generate a report

Click **Generate Report** and you land in a chat with the agent. It asks what kind of report you want and what to cover, then pulls the data and writes it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/report-generate-chat.png" alt="Starting a report by chatting with the AI marketer" />
</Frame>

You don't have to use the button — just ask the agent for a report in any chat ("put together a May performance report"). When it has the numbers, it builds the report and gives you a link to open it.

<Note>
The agent only writes the report once it has real data from your connected accounts — it won't invent metrics. Connect your ad accounts first so it has something to pull.
</Note>

## Read a report

Each report opens on its own page: a title, an executive summary with an overall score, then sections of metrics, tables, and charts.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/report-view.png" alt="A performance report with summary, score, and channel breakdown" />
</Frame>

From the top of any report you can:

- **Download PDF** — render the report to a clean, branded PDF.
- **Send via Email** — email it as a PDF attachment without leaving the page.

## Send a report by email

Click **Send via Email**, enter one or more addresses, and send. Each recipient gets the report as a PDF attachment.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/report-send-email.png" alt="The Send report via email dialog with recipients and the add-to-workspace switch" />
</Frame>

- **Recipients** — type one email or several, separated by commas.
- **Also add recipients to this workspace** — on by default. Each recipient is added as a member of this workspace so they can sign in and see your reports directly. Turn it off to just send the PDF without giving access.

<Note>
Adding someone to the workspace gives them access to everything in it, not just this report. Turn the switch off if you only want to share the single PDF.
</Note>
