You pay for the AI's thinking, not its plumbing. Every time the model reasons — reads your question, looks at tool results, writes an answer — that's one AI step, and steps are what cost credits.

## How a message is charged

Every AI step costs **0.5 credits**.

There is no model to pick. Ryze chooses the right model for each reply, and the step price is the same whichever one it uses.

A simple reply is one step. When the AI pulls data or edits a campaign, it takes an extra step per round of tool results it reads — the tool calls themselves are free.

A few tools carry a fixed cost on top, because they do heavy lifting:

| Tool | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| **Ad creative** (built and rendered from a brief, or re-rendered after an edit) | 2.5 credits per creative |
| **Generated photo** (an image the agent generates to use inside a creative) | 0.5 credits per image |
| **UGC video** | 10 credits per video |
| **SEO data lookup** (rankings, backlinks, keyword data) | 0.5 credits each |
| **Competitor ad search** (Ad Library) | 0.5 credits each |
| **Web search** and **web page fetch** | 0.1 credits each |

Everything else the agent calls is free: Google Ads, Meta, GA4, Search Console, your store and your CMS.

### Examples

- **"Hey agent!"** → one step. **0.5 credits.**
- **"What's my Google Ads spend this week?"** → pull data, then answer: two steps. **1 credit.**
- **"Compare ROAS across my Meta and Google campaigns."** → a few rounds of reports, then the comparison: ~4 steps. **~2 credits.**
- **"Generate 3 ad creatives for our spring launch."** → three creatives (7.5), a few steps, and 0.5 for each photo the agent generates. **Usually 9–12 credits.**

## Agent runs

Work the agent does on its own — [scheduled tasks](/agent/schedules) and applying [Approvals](/agent/approvals) — is billed the same way: 0.5 credits per AI step, plus the fixed-cost tools above.

**Background research for Approvals is free.** The agent scanning your accounts for opportunities costs nothing — you only pay when you approve a fix and the agent goes to apply it.

## Through Claude or ChatGPT

When you use Ryze through the [Claude](/claude/connect) or ChatGPT connector, the thinking happens in your assistant, on your Claude or ChatGPT plan. Ryze charges **no AI steps** there. Pulling data and making changes in your ad accounts through the connector is free; only the fixed-cost tools above (an SEO data lookup, a competitor ad search) use credits.

Ad creatives and UGC videos are generated inside the Ryze app, not through the connector.

## What you're not charged for

These don't use credits:

- **Tool calls** — pulling data, editing campaigns, publishing content: free. You pay for the AI steps around them, plus the fixed-cost tools above.
- **Approvals research** — the background scanning that files proposals is on us.
- **Managed Campaigns** — the agents that set up and manage a [Managed Campaign](/paid-ads/managed-campaigns) don't use credits. Those are priced on ad spend.
- **Chat on your own Anthropic key** — if you connect your own [Anthropic key](/integrations/anthropic), AI steps cost nothing — only the fixed-cost tools do.

## Track your usage

Open **Organization Settings → Usage** to see consumption across your whole organization.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/nav-organization-settings.png" alt="Opening Organization Settings" />
</Frame>

The Usage page shows:

- **Credit balance** — what you have left.
- **Spent this period** — used since your billing period started.
- **Credits by day** — daily consumption trend.
- **By workspace and by user** — who and what is spending.
- **Credit caps** — a monthly credit limit per workspace, counted from your billing day. Leave it empty for no limit.

## Good to know

- A reply that pulls data does more thinking, so it costs more — hover the credit icon to see the breakdown.
- Unused plan credits roll over — each billing period's allowance is added on top of your remaining balance, and top-up credits stay too.
- The Usage page breaks consumption down by workspace and by user, so you can see who's spending.
