Ryze reports on everything the agent does and everything it measures, across ads, SEO, and CRO in one place.

## The reports

| report | covers |
|---|---|

## Delivery

- **Custom dashboards** — live view of fixes, tests, and results
- **Weekly AI summaries** — every Wednesday
- **Slack and email alerts** on significant changes
- **Agent Activity** — every action the agent has taken, timestamped

See [Scheduling](/reports/scheduling) and [Exporting](/reports/exporting).

## Dashboards

Dashboards are live views the agent builds from your connected accounts — you don't configure widgets by hand.

- **Templates** — start from a ready-made one: **Organic Traffic Overview**, **AI Traffic Overview**, **Product Winners & Losers**, **Paid Ads Overview**, or **All Accounts Overview**.
- **Ask for one** — click **Design a dashboard** in the chat shelf, or just describe it ("a dashboard with spend, ROAS and conversions per platform for the last 30 days"). The agent pulls the data, lays out the charts and tables, and hands you the page.
- **Platforms** — the paid-ads dashboard covers Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Ads, TikTok, Snapchat (including conversion value), LinkedIn, Reddit and OpenAI Ads, depending on what is connected. When you have several accounts per platform, the accounts from your **starred** connection are selected first.


## Unified attribution

Reports combine ads, SEO, and CRO rather than treating them separately. This matters because the channels interact: CRO improvements raise the return on both ads and organic, and catalogue work improves Shopping and organic together.

<Note>
Platform-reported numbers will not match each other. Google Ads, Meta, GA4, and Shopify all use different attribution windows and models. Ryze reports each rather than forcing a false reconciliation.
</Note>

## Reading reports honestly

**Different channels have different latency.** Paid moves in 24 hours, technical fixes in days, organic in 2–6 weeks, AI citations in weeks to months. A monthly report will show paid results and very little organic movement — that is expected, not underperformance.

**Volatility is normal in AI visibility.** Judge by rate across many prompts over a quarter, never by a single week. See [monitoring interval](/ai-visibility/monitoring-interval).

**Record a baseline.** Every report is only meaningful against where you started. This matters particularly for the plan guarantees, which are measured against your own baseline.

## Per workspace

Reports are generated per workspace, so agencies get per-client reporting without manual separation. Alerts can be routed per workspace. See [agency and multi-workspace](/plans/agency-multi-workspace).
