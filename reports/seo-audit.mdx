The SEO audit report lists what Ryze found wrong on your site, ordered by expected impact rather than by severity label or count.

## How findings are grouped

**Crawling and indexation** — pages that cannot be reached or are not eligible to appear. These block everything else.

**On-page** — titles, meta descriptions, headings, thin content, duplicate descriptions.

**Structured data** — missing, invalid, or contradicting the visible page.

**Performance** — Core Web Vitals against field data.

**Structure** — hierarchy depth, orphan pages, internal linking, pagination.

**Catalogue** — GTINs, categories, imagery, out-of-stock handling.

## Reading the priority order

The order is not arbitrary. Findings are ranked by expected traffic impact:

1. **Blocking issues** — pages that should be indexed but cannot be. Nothing else matters until these are fixed.
2. **Scale issues** — duplicate or thin content across many pages, crawl traps. Individually small, collectively large.
3. **High-value page issues** — problems on pages that already earn impressions.
4. **Everything else** — alt text, heading levels, meta length.

A store fixing alt text while half its catalogue is unindexable is working the wrong end of the list.

## The most useful section

**Pages with impressions but poor rankings.** These are proven demand against an underperforming page — the highest return available, because you do not need to earn new demand or wait for new pages to be indexed.

Ryze surfaces these from your Search Console data. If you read one section, read that one.

## Acting on it

Approved fixes ship within hours. Configure what needs sign-off in [Permissions & approvals](/claude/permissions).

Ask the assistant directly:

> Show me the highest-impact SEO issues on my store.

> Which pages have impressions but rank below position 10?

## What the report does not cover

- **Commercial factors** — pricing, product quality, brand strength. These frequently matter more than any technical finding.
- **Off-page authority** — link profile strength is reported separately.
- **Content quality judgement** beyond structural signals like thinness and duplication.

A clean audit does not mean you will rank. It means nothing technical is stopping you.

## Cadence

Full audit at onboarding, then continuous monitoring. Issues appear when themes change, apps are installed, and products are removed — periodic re-audits miss weeks of damage.
