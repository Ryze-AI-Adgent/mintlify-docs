Ryze works inside Claude. Once connected, you can ask Claude about your Google Ads and Meta data in plain language — no dashboards, no exports.

## Before you start

- **Any Claude plan works** — Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise all support custom connectors. On the Free plan you can add one custom connector, so remove another custom connector first if you already have one.
- **Team or Enterprise?** An organization Owner adds Ryze once in Claude's **Organization settings → Connectors → Add → Custom → Web** with the URL below. After that, each member opens **Customize → Connectors**, finds **Ryze AI** and clicks **Connect** — skip to step 6.
- **You need an active Ryze plan or free trial** (Paid Ads, Agency or an SEO plan). Without one, Claude can list your workspaces and connect platforms, but every other Ryze tool replies that a subscription is required, with a link to start your trial or fix your payment.

## Your connector URL

You'll need this when adding Ryze to Claude:

```
https://connector.get-ryze.ai/mcp
```

## Add Ryze as a connector

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings in Claude">
    Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai), click your profile avatar at the **bottom-left**, then choose **Settings**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/claude-step-1.webp" alt="Open Settings in Claude" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Connectors → Customize">
    In Settings, open the **Connectors** tab. Claude shows that connectors have moved to **Customize** — click the link to open **Customize → Connectors**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/claude-step-2.webp" alt="Open the Customize panel" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a custom connector">
    On the Customize page, click the **+** button next to *Connectors*.
    <Frame><img src="/images/claude-step-3.webp" alt="Click the plus button to add a connector" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    - **Name** — enter `Ryze AI`
    - **Remote MCP server URL** — enter `https://connector.get-ryze.ai/mcp`
    <Frame><img src="/images/claude-step-4.webp" alt="The empty connector modal" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hit Add">
    After filling both fields, click **Add** at the bottom-right of the modal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect** on the new Ryze AI connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick workspaces and allow">
    Claude opens the Ryze page **Connect your data sources to AI**. Sign in if asked. Under **Workspaces**, choose the workspaces Claude can access — all are selected by default (**All workspaces**), up to 20 per connection. Then click **Allow connection**.

    When exactly one workspace is selected, the page also shows **Connect your accounts**, so you can connect Google, Meta and other ad accounts right there before you allow access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    You'll see a green **Connected to Ryze AI** toast. The connector now lists the Ryze tools for the platforms connected in the workspaces you picked, plus Ryze's own tools (workspaces, connections, creatives and more). Platforms you haven't connected yet don't show tools until you connect them and reload the connector — see the [FAQ](/claude/faq).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Shortcut: open [claude.ai/customize/connectors](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors) directly and start at step 3. Or, in Ryze, open **Integrations → Claude MCP** and click **Open Claude MCP** — it copies the connector URL and opens Claude's add-connector dialog.
</Tip>

## New to Ryze?

If you don't have a Ryze account yet, click **Sign up** on the sign-in page that opens in step 7 (*Create your account to connect Ryze AI to your assistant*). Enter your email and your website, click **Create account**, then type the code we email you. Ryze creates your workspace and takes you back to **Connect your data sources to AI**, where **Connect your accounts** lets you link Google, Meta and other ad platforms. Then click **Allow connection**.

To use the tools, start your free trial in **Organization Settings → Billing**, or open the link Claude shows when it says a subscription is required.

## Try it

Start a **new chat** in Claude and ask for what you want:

> Pull the last 7 days of Google Ads spend and break it down by campaign.

Claude uses Ryze tools to answer.

<Tip>
Always start a **new chat** after connecting or reconnecting. Existing chats won't pick up the connection.
</Tip>
