---
title: "Connect your Shopify store"
description: "Connect a Shopify store to Ryze with only your .myshopify.com domain — no app creation, API keys, or credentials."
---

Connecting Shopify to Ryze needs only your store domain. There is no app to create, no API key to generate, and no credentials to copy.

## Before you start

- You need a Ryze workspace. See [Create a workspace](/workspaces/create-workspace).
- You need permission to approve app access on the Shopify store.
- Have your `.myshopify.com` domain ready — not your custom domain.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations → Shopify">
    In Ryze, go to **Workspace Settings → Integrations** and pick **Shopify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your store domain">
    Type your `.myshopify.com` domain — for example `yourstore.myshopify.com` — and continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve in Shopify">
    A Shopify window opens. Approve the access prompt and the connection completes automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Shopify card then shows a **Connected** badge.

<Tip>
After connecting, start a **new chat** in Claude or ChatGPT so the assistant picks up the change. Existing chats will not see it.
</Tip>

## What Ryze uses the connection for

- **Product and revenue data** — identifying your best-selling products, which anchors keyword and topic selection
- **Publishing** — pushing generated articles live to your store
- **On-page fixes** — shipping technical and GEO changes to store pages
- **Store auditing** — finding on-page SEO issues across products and collections

## Troubleshooting

**The approval window reports an error.** Retry from the Integrations page. If it persists, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) with your store domain.

**Nothing happens after approval.** Check for a blocked popup, then retry.

**The store shows as disconnected later.** Click **Reconnect** on the Shopify card and re-authorize, then start a new chat.

**You entered your custom domain.** Use the `.myshopify.com` domain instead — the custom domain will not resolve.

## Upgrading to a custom app (theme access, redirects)

The standard connection covers products, publishing, and content edits. Theme file edits, URL redirects, and navigation changes need your own **custom app**: Shopify allows theme file writes only with a separate exemption the standard Ryze app does not have, and redirects need a scope the standard app does not request. You create the custom app in the Shopify **Dev Dashboard** — follow [Connect Shopify Custom App](/integrations/shopify-custom-app) for the full walkthrough.

<Warning>
Installing the custom app in Shopify is **not enough by itself**. Ryze keeps using the token from your original connection, with the original permissions — reconnecting on top does not refresh it either. You must **delete the existing connection and connect again through the Custom app tab**.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the custom app in Shopify">
    Shopify no longer creates custom apps inside the admin — new ones are built in the **Dev Dashboard**. Follow [Steps 1–3 of the custom app guide](/integrations/shopify-custom-app): create the app, add the scopes (include `write_themes`, plus `write_online_store_navigation` for URL redirects), **Release** the version, and **Install** it on your store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the current connection in Ryze">
    In **Workspace Settings → Integrations → Shopify**, delete the existing connection. Do not use Reconnect — the old token would survive with the old permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect again via the Custom app tab">
    Pick **Shopify** again, switch to the **Custom app** tab, and enter your store domain plus the app's **Client ID** and **Client secret** (Dev Dashboard → your app → **Settings**).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Blocked tasks (redirects, schema template deployments, theme edits) resume automatically on the next run once the custom-app connection is live.

## Connecting more than one store

A single workspace can hold multiple accounts for the same platform. For separate brands or clients, use a separate workspace per store. See [Connect multiple accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts) and [Managing multiple accounts](/integrations/managing-multiple-accounts).
