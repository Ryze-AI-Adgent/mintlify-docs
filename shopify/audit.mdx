---

When you [connect a Shopify store](/connect-shopify), Ryze audits it against your Search Console data, DataForSEO rankings, and your catalogue. This is the check list.

## Technical

- Crawlability — `robots.txt` rules blocking pages that should be indexable
- Indexation — pages carrying `noindex`, and pages excluded in Search Console
- Canonical tags — missing, self-referencing incorrectly, or pointing to the wrong URL
- Redirect chains and loops
- Broken internal links and 404s that still receive traffic or links
- Pagination — broken or blocking access to deep catalogue pages
- Sitemap accuracy — non-canonical or non-indexable URLs included
- Duplicate URLs across collection paths and variants

## On-page

- Missing, duplicated, or truncated title tags
- Missing or duplicated meta descriptions
- Heading structure — multiple H1s, skipped levels
- Thin product descriptions, and descriptions copied from the supplier
- Thin or empty collection pages
- Image alt text
- Internal linking — orphan pages, and key pages with too few inbound internal links

## Structured data

- Presence of `Product` schema on product pages
- Completeness of the `offers` block — `price`, `priceCurrency`, `availability`
- Markup that contradicts the visible page, particularly price and availability
- Invalid or partial markup that will be ignored
- `BreadcrumbList` and `Organization` presence
- Review markup validity where reviews exist

## Catalogue and feed

- Missing GTINs
- Incorrect product categories
- Product titles that will not perform in shopping surfaces
- Low-resolution imagery
- Out-of-stock products returning soft 404s or removed without redirects
- Variant handling — near-duplicate pages without canonicals

## Performance

- Core Web Vitals — LCP, INP, CLS against field data
- Unoptimised and oversized images
- Render-blocking scripts, including accumulated app scripts
- Excess JavaScript
- Missing lazy-loading

## What happens after the audit

Findings are prioritised by expected impact rather than by count. Approved fixes ship within hours — see [permissions and approvals](/claude/permissions) to control what needs sign-off.

Ask the assistant directly:

> Audit my store and show me the biggest on-page SEO issues.

> Which of my collection pages are thin, and what should they say?

## Limits

The audit reflects what Ryze can read through the Shopify connection and your connected analytics. It does not evaluate commercial factors — pricing position, product quality, or brand strength — which frequently matter more than any technical finding.
