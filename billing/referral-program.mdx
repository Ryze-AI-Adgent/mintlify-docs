Every Ryze user has a personal referral link and code. When someone you refer becomes a paying customer, you earn **30% of every payment they make during their first 12 months** — automatically, on every invoice.

## Share your link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the referral dialog">
    Click the **gift icon** in the top navigation bar of your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your invite link">
    Your personal link looks like `reports.get-ryze.ai/login?ref=yourcode`. Share it
    anywhere — DMs, posts, communities. You can also send invites by email straight
    from the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They sign up and subscribe">
    Anyone who opens your link is tracked for 30 days. Once they subscribe, every
    payment they make earns you 30% for their first 12 months.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How tracking works

- **By link** — opening your invite link marks the visitor's browser for 30 days. If they sign up and later subscribe, the referral is yours, even if they pay weeks after the click.
- **By code** — if someone signed up without your link, they can still credit you: **gift icon → Apply referral** and enter your code. This only works before their first payment.

## When you earn

- Commissions accrue on **real payments only**. Free trials don't generate anything until they convert to a paid subscription.
- You earn on **every payment** — monthly renewals included — for the referral's first 12 months.
- If a referred customer is refunded or cancels, the related commission is adjusted automatically.

## Getting paid

Payouts are handled through our partner portal:

1. Go to [ryze.tolt.io](https://ryze.tolt.io) and sign in with the same email you use for Ryze.
2. Add your **PayPal** account under payout settings.
3. Earnings are paid on **NET-15 terms** — commissions from one month are paid out around the 15th of the following month. There is no minimum payout threshold.

The portal also shows your full history: clicks, sign-ups, and every commission with its status.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I apply my own referral code?">
    No — self-referrals are blocked. The code is for people you invite.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My friend signed up but I don't see them in Past referrals">
    Make sure they used your link before signing up, or applied your code before
    their first payment. If they did and you still don't see them,
    [contact support](/help/contact) with their sign-up email and we'll check the attribution.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do trials count?">
    A trial shows up as a sign-up, but commissions only start once the trial converts
    into a paid subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a limit on how many people I can refer?">
    No limit on referrals. Email invites from the dialog are capped at 20 per day to
    prevent abuse — your link itself has no limits.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
