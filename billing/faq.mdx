---

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Where do I find and download my invoices?">
Open **Billing** in your organization settings and click **Manage** on a subscription. This opens the billing portal, where you can view and download every invoice and receipt.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I add my company name, VAT or tax ID to invoices?">
In the billing portal (**Billing → Manage**), update your billing details. Company name, address, and VAT/tax ID appear on all future invoices.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I change my payment card?">
Go to **Billing → Manage** and update your payment method in the billing portal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get invoices automatically every month?">
Invoices are emailed to your billing address after each successful payment.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My billing email is different from my login email. Is that a problem?">
No. Your billing details in the portal can use a different email from your account login.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do errors or failed runs cost credits?">
No. You're only charged for work that lands — failed runs, system errors, and internal tool calls don't cost credits. See [Credits & Usage](/paid-ads/credits).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I want a refund.">
Email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) and we'll help.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
