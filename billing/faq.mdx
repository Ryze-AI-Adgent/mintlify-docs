Everything here follows our [Refund and Cancellation Policy](https://www.get-ryze.ai/refund-policy). If anything below seems to disagree with it, the policy wins — tell us at [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) so we can fix the page.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
Open **Organization Settings → Billing**, click **Manage** on the subscription and choose cancel. Access stays until the end of the paid period, and the page shows **Cancels {date}**. Cancellation is self-serve only — for security, support doesn't cancel by email or chat on your behalf. If you can't reach the Billing page (for example a login problem), email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) from the address the subscription is billed under and we'll restore your access so you can cancel. See [Manage your subscription](/billing/manage-subscription).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will I be charged automatically when my trial ends?">
Yes. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, the plan you selected starts automatically and the card on file is charged for the first billing period. The trial end date is shown on **Organization Settings → Billing**. To avoid the charge, cancel before that date — auto-renew turns off and the trial simply ends. A trial requires a valid payment method and is available once per organization.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the small charge at signup?">
Some plans charge a small one-time setup or verification fee when the trial starts (the amount is shown at checkout). It confirms your payment method and funds a starter credit allowance so the agent can begin work immediately. It is earned when charged and is not refunded, including if you cancel during the trial.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
Paid Ads and Agency plans include a 7-day free trial. SEO Starter can be trialled for 3 days from $1; some SEO offers show a 7-day trial instead. The exact length is displayed before you confirm. See [plans overview](/plans/overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I want a refund.">
Subscription charges are non-refundable once processed — including partial periods, unused time, or a trial that converted because it wasn't cancelled. To stop future charges, cancel from **Billing → Manage**; you keep access until the period ends.

We do correct duplicate charges and billing errors: email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) within 30 days with the account email, charge date, amount and a short description. Please contact us before opening a bank dispute — most billing questions are resolved within one business day. Full terms: [Refund and Cancellation Policy](https://www.get-ryze.ai/refund-policy).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I think I was charged twice.">
Two charges in one month usually mean two different products (for example Paid Ads at $89 and SEO Starter at $129), or a second subscription created under another email. Failed card attempts can also show on some statements but don't collect money. Check **Billing → Manage** for the subscriptions on this organization; if you see a duplicate you didn't intend, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) with the two dates and amounts within 30 days and we'll correct it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I pause instead of cancelling?">
Monthly subscribers can pause once, for 1, 2 or 3 months, from the cancel flow (**Pause my plan for**). While paused you aren't billed and the agent doesn't run; your articles, backlinks, integrations and settings stay saved. Billing resumes automatically when the pause ends unless you cancel first. Trials and annual plans can't be paused.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do my credits expire?">
Not while your subscription is active: unused plan credits and top-up credits carry over from period to period, and each period's allowance is added on top of your balance. All credits — including carried-over and top-up credits — expire when the subscription ends, whether by cancellation, non-payment or termination, and aren't refunded or reinstated. See [Credits & Usage](/agent/credits).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How am I billed?">
Monthly or annually, on the date you subscribed, and automatically renewed for periods of the same length until you cancel. Prices are in USD and may not include tax. Your billing date also anchors the SEO engine's monthly content cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change plans mid-cycle?">
Yes, from **Billing → Manage**. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the current period. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal. Switching from annual to monthly, or cancelling an annual plan mid-term, doesn't refund the unused part of the annual charge.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if a payment fails?">
We retry the card and notify you. If payment still isn't received, the subscription is suspended and then cancelled, and remaining credits expire. Update your card from **Billing → Manage** to keep things running; reactivating after non-payment may require settling the outstanding balance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is the Billing page? I don't see it in Workspace Settings.">
Billing lives at the **organization** level, not the workspace: open **Organization Settings → Billing**. Only the organization owner sees it — if you were invited as a member, ask the owner.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I find and download my invoices?">
Open **Billing** in your organization settings and click **Manage** on a subscription. This opens the billing portal, where you can view and download every invoice and receipt. Invoices are also emailed to your billing address after each successful payment.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I cancelled — can I still download my invoices?">
Yes. Invoices stay available after cancellation; open **Billing → Manage** as long as you can sign in. If you no longer have access, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) with the email you paid under and we'll send the links.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I add my company name, VAT or tax ID to invoices?">
In the billing portal (**Billing → Manage**), update your billing details. Company name, address, and VAT/tax ID appear on all future invoices. Past invoices aren't reissued automatically — contact support if you need one corrected.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I change my payment card?">
Go to **Billing → Manage** and update your payment method in the billing portal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My billing email is different from my login email. Is that a problem?">
No. Your billing details in the portal can use a different email from your account login.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My bank statement shows more than the invoice amount (e.g. £68.97 or $94 for an $89 plan).">
We bill a fixed amount in USD. The difference is your card issuer's currency conversion plus any foreign-transaction fee — not an extra charge from Ryze. The USD invoice in the billing portal is the accurate record.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do errors or failed runs cost credits?">
No. You're only charged for work that lands — failed runs, system errors, and internal tool calls don't cost credits. Credits used by schedules, agent runs and actions you configured are normal usage, not an error. See [Credits & Usage](/agent/credits).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my content and workspace if I cancel?">
Articles published to your own site or CMS stay yours, and backlinks we placed on third-party sites aren't taken down. Ryze stops producing new work. After the subscription ends, the workspaces it covered are scheduled for deletion along with their generated drafts, connections and history; resubscribe before then and the data can be restored (expired credits can't). See [SEO FAQ](/seo/faq).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete my account and data?">
Cancel your subscription first from **Billing → Manage**, then email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) from your account email asking for deletion. We remove your organization, workspaces, connected-account tokens and card details, and confirm in writing. Backups are purged within 30 days — see [Data storage](/security/data-storage).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I reach support, and where can I see my ticket?">
Email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) — every message gets a reply by email, including the ones you send from the in-app feedback button. There's no ticket portal; the email thread is your ticket, so reply in the same thread to keep the history together.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
