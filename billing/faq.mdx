Everything here follows our [Refund and Cancellation Policy](https://www.get-ryze.ai/refund-policy). If anything below seems to disagree with it, the policy wins — tell us at [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) so we can fix the page.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
Open **Organization Settings → Billing** and click **Cancel plan** under the plan. Ryze shows a few screens first — click **Continue cancellation**, **Cancel anyway** or **I still want to cancel** to go on, pick a reason, then click **Confirm cancellation** on **Are you sure?**. Access stays until the end of the paid period, and the Billing page shows the date it ends. On an SEO plan with several websites, cancelling stops SEO for all of them — to drop only one website, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai). Changed your mind? Click **Resume subscription** before the end date. See [Manage your subscription](/billing/manage-subscription#cancel-your-subscription).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will I be charged automatically when my trial ends?">
Yes. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, the plan you selected starts automatically and the card on file is charged for the first billing period. **Do not rely on a reminder email before that charge — note the date when you start.** The trial end date is shown on **Organization Settings → Billing**. To avoid the charge, cancel before that date — auto-renew turns off and the trial simply ends. A trial requires a valid payment method. If your organization already had a subscription to that product, a new one starts without a trial.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the $1 charge at signup?">
Plans with a free trial charge a one-time $1 setup fee when the trial starts. It adds 250 starter credits to your balance so the agent can begin work immediately. It isn't refunded, including if you cancel during the trial.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
Yes. Paid Ads and Agency come with a 7-day free trial, SEO Starter with a 3-day free trial — each with a one-time $1 setup fee. The exact trial length is shown before you confirm.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a discount for annual billing?">
Yes. Paying annually saves 30% in the first year; after that, the annual plan renews at the regular price. The exact amount is shown before you confirm.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I want a refund.">
Subscription charges aren't refunded once processed — including partial periods, unused time, or a trial that converted because it wasn't cancelled. To stop future charges, cancel from **Billing → Cancel plan**; you keep access until the period ends.

We do correct duplicate charges and billing errors: email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) within 30 days with the account email, charge date, amount and a short description. Please contact us before opening a bank dispute. Full terms: [Refund and Cancellation Policy](https://www.get-ryze.ai/refund-policy).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I think I was charged twice.">
Two charges in one month usually mean two different products (for example Paid Ads at $89 and SEO Starter at $129), or a second subscription created under another email. Failed card attempts can also show on some statements but don't collect money. Check **Organization Settings → Billing** for the subscriptions on this organization; if you see a duplicate you didn't intend, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) with the two dates and amounts within 30 days and we'll correct it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do unused credits carry over?">
Yes. Credits never expire: unused plan credits and top-up credits carry over from month to month, and each period's allowance is added on top of your balance. Annual plans get the whole year's credits at the start of the year. See [how many credits each plan includes](/plans/overview#credits) and [Credits & Usage](/agent/credits).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How am I billed?">
Monthly or annually, on the date you subscribed, and automatically renewed for periods of the same length until you cancel. Prices are in USD and may not include tax. Your billing date also anchors the SEO engine's monthly content cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change plans mid-cycle?">
It depends on the plan:

- **Paid Ads ↔ Agency, monthly ↔ yearly:** on **Organization Settings → Billing**, click **Switch → Switch now** on the plan you want. The switch is immediate: the prorated difference is charged to your card now and the plan renews at the new price. If you're on a free trial, switching ends the trial and starts billing now. You need a card saved under **Invoices & payment method**.
- **SEO Starter → Traffic Printer:** click **Unlock Mentions** on the Traffic Printer card. Your card is charged today for the rest of the current period; it renews with your SEO subscription and covers all your websites.
- **Other SEO changes** (Traffic Printer → SEO Starter, monthly ↔ yearly): email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) and we'll change it for you.

Switching from annual to monthly, or cancelling an annual plan mid-term, doesn't refund the unused part of the annual charge. See [Switch your plan](/billing/manage-subscription#switch-your-plan).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if a payment fails?">
We retry the card and notify you. The plan shows *Payment failed — update your card to keep access.* and your workspaces show *Your last payment failed*. Click **Update payment method** on the plan (or in that message) to update the card and keep things running. If payment still isn't received, the subscription is suspended and then cancelled; reactivating after non-payment may require settling the outstanding balance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is the Billing page? I don't see it in Workspace Settings.">
Billing lives at the **organization** level, not the workspace: open **Organization Settings → Billing**. Only the organization owner sees it — if you were invited as a member, ask the owner.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I find and download my invoices?">
Open **Billing** in your organization settings and click **Invoices & payment method** on a subscription. This opens the billing portal, where you can view and download every invoice and receipt. Invoices are also emailed to your billing address after each successful payment.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I cancelled — can I still download my invoices?">
Yes. Invoices stay available after cancellation; open **Billing → Invoices & payment method** as long as you can sign in. If you no longer have access, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) with the email you paid under and we'll send the links.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I add my company name, VAT or tax ID to invoices?">
In the billing portal (**Billing → Invoices & payment method**), update your billing details. Company name, address, and VAT/tax ID appear on all future invoices. Past invoices aren't reissued automatically — contact support if you need one corrected.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I change my payment card?">
Go to **Billing → Invoices & payment method** and update your payment method in the billing portal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My billing email is different from my login email. Is that a problem?">
No. Your billing details in the portal can use a different email from your account login.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My bank statement shows more than the invoice amount (e.g. £68.97 or $94 for an $89 plan).">
We bill a fixed amount in USD. The difference is your card issuer's currency conversion plus any foreign-transaction fee — not an extra charge from Ryze. The USD invoice in the billing portal is the accurate record.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my content and workspace if I cancel?">
Articles published to your own site or CMS stay yours, and backlinks we placed on third-party sites aren't taken down. Ryze stops producing new work when the paid period ends. See [SEO FAQ](/seo/faq).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
Cancel your subscription first from **Billing → Cancel plan**, then open **Organization Settings → General** and click **Delete account**. See [Delete your account](/workspaces/delete-account).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I reach support, and where can I see my ticket?">
Email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai), or send a message from the app: click **Share Feedback** (the speech-bubble icon in the top bar, or in the account menu), pick a **Type**, write your message and click **Send**. Every message gets a reply by email. There's no ticket portal; the email thread is your ticket, so reply in the same thread to keep the history together.

To check whether Ryze is up, open the **Help & support** button at the bottom-right → **System status**, or [status.get-ryze.ai](https://status.get-ryze.ai). More ways to get help: [Get help and send feedback](/introduction#get-help-and-send-feedback).
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
