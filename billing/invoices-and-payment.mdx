Everything billing-related lives behind one button: **Manage**.

## Open billing

Go to [Billing](https://reports.get-ryze.ai/settings/billing) in your organization settings. You'll see a card for each subscription with its status and renewal date.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/billing-manage.png" alt="The Billing page with Manage buttons" />
</Frame>

## Download invoices

1. Click **Manage** on a subscription.
2. The billing portal opens.
3. View and download any invoice or receipt as a PDF.

<Tip>
Invoices are also emailed to your billing address after each successful payment.
</Tip>

## Update your payment card

Click **Manage**, then update your payment method in the billing portal. The new card applies to your next charge.

## Add company or VAT details

In the billing portal, edit your billing details to add a company name, address, or VAT / tax ID. These appear on all future invoices.
