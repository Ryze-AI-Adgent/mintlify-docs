Connecting Shopify usually needs only your store domain. When it fails, it is almost always one of the following.

## Two ways to connect

The **Shopify app** tab (the default) needs only your `.myshopify.com` domain and one approval in Shopify. The **Custom app** tab takes your store domain plus the **Client ID** and **Client secret** of a custom app you create in your store — see [Shopify Custom App](/integrations/shopify-custom-app). This page mostly covers the default **Shopify app**.

## You entered the wrong domain

Ryze needs the **`.myshopify.com`** domain, not your custom domain.

```
✓ yourstore.myshopify.com
✗ yourstore.com
```

Find it in your Shopify admin under **Settings → Domains**, listed as the store's permanent domain.

## Shopify didn't open

After you click **Continue**, Ryze sends you to your Shopify admin in the same tab to approve the app — there's no popup. If you land back in Ryze without the approval screen, make sure you're logged in to the right store in Shopify, then click **Connect** on the Shopify card again.

## You lack permission to approve

Approving app access requires sufficient permissions on the Shopify store. If you are a staff account without app installation rights, the store owner or an admin must complete the approval.

## You see an error message

| Message | What it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Must be a valid yourstore.myshopify.com domain** | You typed your custom domain or a URL with a path. | Enter only the `.myshopify.com` domain, e.g. `yourstore.myshopify.com`. |
| **This Shopify store is already connected to another Ryze workspace. Shopify keeps one live connection per store — disconnect it there first, then retry.** | Shopify allows one live Ryze connection per store, and this store is connected in another workspace. | Open that workspace → **Integrations** → Shopify, disconnect the store, then connect here. No access to that workspace? Email support with the store domain. |
| **Your Shopify connection expired. Please try again.** | More than 10 minutes passed between clicking **Continue** and approving in Shopify, or you switched to another workspace (for example in another tab) while connecting. | Click **Connect** again in the workspace where you want the store and approve right away. |
| **The Shopify install took too long to come back. Open the link again to retry.** | Shown on a connect link from your agent: the approval in Shopify didn't come back to Ryze in time. | Open the link again, or ask the agent for a new one. |
| **Shopify access expired — please reconnect your store.** | A custom-app connection lost its credentials. | Click **Reconnect** and enter the Client ID and Client secret again. |
| **The custom app exists, but it isn't installed on this store yet…** (custom app) | The custom app was created but never installed on the store, or was uninstalled later. | In the Dev Dashboard, open the app and click **Install app**, then connect or reconnect — see [custom app errors](/integrations/shopify-custom-app#errors-when-connecting). |
| **Shopify has no app with this Client ID on this store…** (custom app) | The Client ID belongs to another app, or the domain points to another store. | Check the `.myshopify.com` domain and copy the Client ID from this app's **Settings → Credentials** — see [custom app errors](/integrations/shopify-custom-app#errors-when-connecting). Or use the **Shopify app** tab. |
| **Shopify refuses custom-app credentials for this store: the app and the store must belong to the same organization in the Dev Dashboard…** (custom app) | The app was created in another Shopify organization, such as an agency's. | Create the app from the organization that owns the store — see [custom app errors](/integrations/shopify-custom-app#errors-when-connecting). Or use the **Shopify app** tab. |
| **Shopify has no store at …** (custom app) | No Shopify store exists at that domain. | Copy the `.myshopify.com` address from **Settings → Domains** and try again. |

Anything else: retry once from the **Integrations** page. If it persists, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) with your `.myshopify.com` domain and the exact message.

## It connected, then showed as disconnected

Click **Reconnect** on the Shopify card and re-authorize. Access can be revoked by:

- Uninstalling or reinstalling the app in Shopify
- Changes to staff permissions
- Store ownership transfer
- Store plan changes or pausing
- The store being closed, frozen or deleted — the card shows **Reconnect needed**. Reopen the store in Shopify, then connect it again.

When a connection drops, the workspace owner gets a **Reconnect Shopify** email. You can turn these emails off in **Settings → Notifications** (**Disconnected integrations**).

In Ryze, the agent sees the reconnected store from your next message. In Claude or ChatGPT, **start a new chat** after reconnecting.

## It shows connected but Ryze sees no data

Check, in order:

1. **Right store?** If you manage several, confirm which is connected to this workspace.
2. **Products published?** Products must be published to the Online Store sales channel to be visible.
3. **Store password-protected?** A store behind a password cannot be crawled or audited.
4. **Using Claude or ChatGPT?** Start a new chat there after connecting the store. If Shopify was connected after you added Ryze to Claude or ChatGPT and its tools still don't appear, refresh the Ryze connector — see the [Claude FAQ](/claude/faq) or [Connect Ryze to ChatGPT](/chatgpt/connect#connected-a-new-platform-after-installing).

## The agent says it can't make a change

With the standard **Shopify app**, the agent edits product, collection, page and blog content and SEO fields. Theme files (such as `robots.txt`, canonical tags or structured data in the theme) and URL redirects need a custom app. To switch:

1. Create a custom app with the access you need — see [Shopify Custom App](/integrations/shopify-custom-app). The quickest route is to [let AI Marketer set it up](/integrations/shopify-custom-app#fastest-way-let-ai-marketer-set-it-up): you only log in to Shopify and paste the keys.
2. In Ryze, remove the current Shopify connection (**View** → trash icon). **Reconnect** keeps the old access, so remove it first.
3. Connect again on the **Custom app** tab.

## Several stores

Each store is its own connection — use **View** → **Add one more account** on the Shopify card to add the next one.

**Copy integrations** in a new workspace doesn't carry over a store connected on the **Shopify app** tab. Disconnect the store in the old workspace, then connect it in the new one. A **Custom app** connection is copied.

## The assistant is reading the wrong store

When you connect Claude or ChatGPT, you choose which workspaces it can access. If you manage several stores, name the store in your message, and start a new chat when switching.

See [Connect Ryze to Claude](/claude/connect).

## Still stuck

Email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) with your `.myshopify.com` domain and what you saw. Include the error text if there was one.
