Ryze is a web app for running your marketing. At its center is the **AI Marketer** — an agent that reads your connected accounts, does the work (articles, technical fixes, campaigns, creatives, reports) and brings you anything that needs a decision. You can chat with it, schedule it, or let it run on its own and check in on **Home**.

## What's in the app

Everything lives in the left sidebar of your workspace:

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | The agent's input box over one **Needs you** list: setup steps, approvals, fixes and questions waiting for you. See [Home](/home). |
| **AI Marketer** | **New chat**, [Projects](/agent/projects) and **Chat History** (see [Chat with the AI Marketer](/agent/chat)), plus [Schedules](/agent/schedules), [Templates](/agent/templates) and [Reports](/agent/reports). The **AI Marketer** button in the top bar opens the same agent in a side panel on any page. |
| **Integrations** | Connect Search Console, Analytics, ad accounts, your CMS and more, and install the [Ryze Pixel](/paid-ads/ryze-pixel). See [Integrations](/integrations/overview). |
| **Brand** | Who you are, your voice and audience — the agent reads it before every task. See [Brand](/agent/context). |
| **Media** | Your product photos and videos for creatives and articles. See [Media library](/workspaces/media). |
| **Analytics** | Dashboards: the built-in **Ads Performance**, **Search Performance** and **Web Analytics**, plus dashboards you build yourself. Dashboards you pin show up under it. See [Analytics dashboards](/analytics/performance). |
| **Website** | The Website page with your visits and where they come from, plus **Conversions** — every conversion path on phone and desktop, and where people drop off (see [Conversions](/seo/conversions)) — and **Technical Audit** (see [Technical Audit](/seo/optimizations)). |
| **Ads** | The Ads page with spend and results, plus Managed Campaigns, My Creatives, Ad Templates, Competitor Ads. See [Paid Ads](/paid-ads/introduction). |
| **SEO** | The SEO page with what Google brings you, plus Content Plan, Keywords & Prompts, Backlinks & PR, [Preferences](/seo/preferences). See [SEO](/seo/introduction). |
| **Local** | The Local page with what your Google listing brings you, plus **Map Rankings**, **Reviews** and **Listing**. See [Local SEO](/seo/local-seo). |
| **Agentic** | [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility): visits from AI assistants, the prompts they answer and whether they name you. |
| **Social** | The Social page with what your posts bring you, plus **Calendar** and **Posts** for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and Threads. See [Social](/social/overview). |

Website, Ads, SEO, Local, Agentic and Social sit under **Channels** in the sidebar. Click one to open its page — see [Channel pages](/analytics/channel-pages). **Ads** and the ads analytics come with Paid Ads; **SEO**, **Technical Audit**, **Agentic** and Search Performance come with the SEO plans; **Local** comes with Traffic Printer; **Agentic**, **Local** and **Social** are also sold as [packages](/plans/overview#channel-packages). Sections your plan doesn't include show an **Unlock** label — click it to see the plan that adds them. **Conversions** is open on every plan; only its **A/B Tests** tab needs Shopify CRO.

## Start here

Open **Home** and work through the setup steps at the top of the **Needs you** list — a short setup chat, your brand profile, and the connections the agent needs. Once they're done, the agent takes over.

## Get help and send feedback

- **Help & support** — the button in the bottom-right corner of the app. **Ask a question** sends a message to the Ryze team; a real person replies. **Open knowledge base** opens this help center, and **System status** opens the live status page ([status.get-ryze.ai](https://status.get-ryze.ai)).
- **Share Feedback** — the speech-bubble icon in the top bar, or **Share Feedback** in the account menu at the bottom left. In **Send feedback**, pick a **Type** (**Bug report**, **Feature request**, **Question**, **Integration request** or **Other**), write your **Message** and click **Send**.
- **Changelog** in the account menu shows what's new.
- Or email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai).
