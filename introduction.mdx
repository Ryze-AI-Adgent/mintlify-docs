Ryze is a web app for running your marketing. At its center is the **AI Marketer** — an agent that reads your connected accounts, does the work (articles, technical fixes, campaigns, creatives, reports) and brings you anything that needs a decision. You can chat with it, schedule it, or let it run on its own and check in on **Home**.

## What's in the app

Everything lives in the left sidebar of your workspace:

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Setup steps, what needs your decision, and how results are moving. See [Home](/home). |
| **AI Marketer** | **New chat** and **Chat History** (see [Chat with the AI Marketer](/agent/chat)), plus [Schedules](/agent/schedules), [Templates](/agent/templates) and [Reports](/agent/reports). The **AI Marketer** button in the top bar opens the same agent in a side panel on any page. |
| **Integrations** | Connect Search Console, Analytics, ad accounts, your CMS and more. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview). |
| **Brand** | Who you are, your voice and audience — the agent reads it before every task. See [Brand](/agent/context). |
| **Media** | Your product photos and videos for creatives and articles. See [Media library](/workspaces/media). |
| **Analytics** | Web Analytics ([Ryze Pixel](/paid-ads/ryze-pixel)), [Ads and Search Performance](/analytics/performance), [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility). |
| **Ads** | Managed Campaigns, My Creatives, Ad Templates, Competitor Ads. See [Paid Ads](/paid-ads/introduction). |
| **SEO** | Technical Audit, Content Plan, Keywords & Prompts, Backlinks & PR, [Preferences](/seo/preferences). See [SEO](/seo/introduction). |
| **A/B Testing** | Store A/B tests designed, applied and measured by the Ryze team — part of Shopify CRO. See [Plans and pricing](/plans/overview). |

**Ads** and the ads analytics come with Paid Ads; **SEO**, Search Performance and AI Visibility come with the SEO plans. Sections your plan doesn't include show an **Unlock** label — click it to see the plan that adds them.

## Get help and send feedback

- **Help & support** — the button in the bottom-right corner of the app. **Ask a question** sends a message to the Ryze team; a real person replies. **Open knowledge base** opens this help center, and **System status** opens the live status page ([status.get-ryze.ai](https://status.get-ryze.ai)).
- **Share Feedback** — the speech-bubble icon in the top bar, or **Share Feedback** in the account menu at the bottom left. In **Send feedback**, pick a **Type** (**Bug report**, **Feature request**, **Question**, **Integration request** or **Other**), write your **Message** and click **Send**.
- **Changelog** in the account menu shows what's new.
- Or email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai).
