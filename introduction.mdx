Ryze is a web app for running your marketing. At its center is the **AI Marketer** — an agent that reads your connected accounts, does the work (articles, technical fixes, campaigns, creatives, reports) and brings you anything that needs a decision. You can chat with it, schedule it, or let it run on its own and check in on **Home**.

## What's in the app

Everything lives in the left sidebar of your workspace:

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Setup steps, what needs your decision, and how results are moving. See [Home](/home). |
| **AI Marketer** | **New chat** with the agent, plus [Schedules](/agent/schedules), [Templates](/agent/templates) and [Reports](/agent/reports). |
| **Integrations** | Connect Search Console, Analytics, ad accounts, your CMS and more. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview). |
| **Brand** | Who you are, your voice and audience — the agent reads it before every task. See [Context](/agent/context). |
| **Media** | Your product photos and videos for creatives and articles. See [Media library](/workspaces/media). |
| **Analytics** | Web Analytics, [Ads and Search Performance](/analytics/performance), [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility). |
| **Ads** | Managed Campaigns, My Creatives, Ad Templates, Competitor Ads. See [Paid Ads](/paid-ads/introduction). |
| **SEO** | Technical Audit, Content Plan, Keywords & Prompts, Backlinks & PR, [Preferences](/seo/preferences). See [SEO](/seo/introduction). |

**Ads** and the ads analytics come with Paid Ads; **SEO**, Search Performance and AI Visibility come with the SEO plans. Sections your plan doesn't include show an upgrade label instead.

## Start here

Open **Home** and work through the **Finish setup** cards — a short setup chat, your brand profile, and the connections the agent needs. Once they're done, the agent takes over.
