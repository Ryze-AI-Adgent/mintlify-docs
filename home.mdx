Open **Home** at the top of the sidebar (**AI Marketer → New chat** opens the same screen). The number on the **Home** item is how many things are waiting for you.

At the top, Home greets you and sums up where things stand:

- **"N things to review"**, with a line like **"2 to approve, 1 to fix, 1 setup step. Your agent is handling the rest."**
- **"You're all caught up"** — nothing waits on you. **"Your agent is handling everything. Ask it for what's next."**

Below it is the agent's input box. Type a task and the chat opens right there — see [Chat with the AI Marketer](/agent/chat). The shelf under the box offers ready prompts from [Templates](/agent/templates).

## Needs you

The **Needs you** list collects everything waiting on you, marked **Critical**, **Warning**, **Needs decision** or **Setup**:

- **Setup steps** — **Finish SEO setup** / **Finish Ads setup** (a short setup chat for that product), **Review your brand profile** (check what the agent wrote in [Brand](/agent/context)), **Connect Search Console & Analytics**, **Choose where to publish** (in [SEO → Preferences](/seo/preferences)), **Publish your first article** and **Connect an ad account**. You only see the steps for the products on your plan, and each one disappears once it's done.
- **Approvals** — agent proposals waiting for your OK. See [Inbox and approvals](/agent/approvals).
- **Fixes** — technical issues from the audit and articles that failed to publish or went live with warnings.
- **Questions** — campaigns where the agent stopped to ask you something. Reply right there and it picks back up.

Click an item to see the details and act on it, or open the page where it lives (**Open Technical Audit**, **Open Content Plan**, **Open campaign**). For a Technical Audit item you can:

- **Fix with agent** — starts an agent task that works through the fix on its own. Find it under **Chat History → Agent tasks**; it's billed like any agent run (see [Credits & Usage](/agent/credits)).
- **Fix** next to a page — the same agent task, for just that page.
- **Acknowledge** — dismiss the item.

Home shows the first few items; click **View all** to see the whole list, and **Back to chat** to return.

## Where the numbers went

Home doesn't show results. Click **Website**, **Ads**, **SEO**, **Local** or **Social** in the sidebar for that channel's page ([Channel pages](/analytics/channel-pages)), open **Analytics** for the **Search Performance**, **Ads Performance** and **Web Analytics** dashboards ([Analytics dashboards](/analytics/performance)), **Agentic** for [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility), and **Ads → Managed Campaigns** for your campaigns ([Managed campaigns](/paid-ads/managed-campaigns)).
