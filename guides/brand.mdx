## In this guide

- What the agent stores as brand memory
- How brand details change writing and creatives
- Updating your brand as the business changes

## Where to find it

Open **Brand** in the sidebar.

## Steps

1. Check what Ryze wrote. Ryze builds the first version of your brand profile by reading your site. If there is no profile yet, click **Generate brand profile** or **Set up manually**.
2. Edit the brand description at the top — what the company does, for whom, and what makes it different.
3. Fill in the tabs: **Identity** (what you sell, your industry and tone of voice), **Visual** (brand colors and fonts), **Context** (notes the agent should always remember, and blog examples that show your voice) and **Memory** (short facts the agent keeps — click **Add memory** to add one).
4. Click **Improve Brand Profile** if you want the agent to review the profile. It flags anything weak or missing and asks before changing anything.
5. Upload logos, product photos and videos in **Media** in the sidebar. The agent looks there first when it needs your product photos.

Writing rules that apply only to SEO articles are in **SEO → Preferences → Content**.
