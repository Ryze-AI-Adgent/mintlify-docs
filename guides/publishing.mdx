## In this guide

- Connecting the CMS you publish to
- Publishing a single article
- Spreading a batch across a schedule

## Where you can publish

Programmatic articles publish to any connected destination: **Shopify**, **WordPress**, **Webflow**, **Framer**, **Wix**, **GitHub**, **Ghost**, **HubSpot**, **HighLevel**, **Lightspeed eCom**, **Directus**, a **Custom Webhook** for any other CMS, or **Ryze Hosting** on your own domain. Connect one under **Workspace Settings → Integrations** and pick it as the publish target.

## When articles go out

With auto-publish on, anything due in your content calendar is drafted and published in a **daily publishing window that starts at 20:00 UTC**. Articles dated today go out in that window; nothing is published ahead of its scheduled date.

## After publishing

Ryze checks each article is live about 30 minutes after it publishes, and then **re-checks every published article three times a week**. If a page stops answering — for example a post was moved back to draft in your CMS — the article is flagged with a warning in your articles list and counted in your Monday/Wednesday/Friday digest. Ryze never unpublishes on its own; use **Republish** or **Unpublish** on the article to act on it.
