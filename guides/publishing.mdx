## In this guide

## Where you can publish

Content Plan articles publish to any connected destination: **Shopify**, **WordPress**, **Webflow**, **Framer**, **Wix**, **GitHub**, **Ghost**, **Squarespace**, **Duda**, **BigCommerce**, **Magento**, **Contentful**, **Sanity**, **Directus**, **Lovable**, **GoDaddy Airo**, **Base44**, **Replit**, **HubSpot**, **HighLevel**, **Lightspeed eCom**, a **Custom Webhook** for any other CMS, or **Ryze Hosting** (a free Ryze subdomain or your own domain). Connect one on the **Integrations** page and pick it in **Publish to** under **SEO → Preferences → Publishing**.

### Don't see your platform?

You can still publish with Ryze:

- **Ryze Hosting** — Ryze hosts your blog on a free Ryze subdomain right away, or on your own subdomain such as `blog.yoursite.com` after you add one DNS record. No CMS needed. See [Ryze Hosting](/integrations/ryze-hosting).
- **Custom-coded site** (Next.js, Vercel, Netlify, Hugo and similar) — publish articles as files committed to your repository with [GitHub](/integrations/github), or let your developer receive each article through a [Custom Webhook](/integrations/custom-webhook).
- **Someone else builds your site** — invite them to the workspace (**Workspace Settings → Team**) or send them this page.
- **Platforms without a publishing connection** (for example Weebly, Square Online, Pixieset or Google Sites) — use Ryze Hosting for the blog and link to it from your site's menu or footer.

## When articles go out

With **Publishing mode** set to **Automatic** (**SEO → Preferences → Publishing**), articles go out on their scheduled date in your content plan; nothing is published ahead of its scheduled date. In **Manual** mode, you publish drafts yourself.

To publish one article right away, open it in **SEO → Content Plan**, click **Publish now**, choose where it goes live (your default destination is preselected) and click **Publish**. To spread articles out, move their scheduled dates.

## After publishing

Ryze checks that each article is live shortly after it publishes, and keeps re-checking published articles regularly. If a page stops answering — for example a post was moved back to draft in your CMS — the article is flagged with a warning in your articles list and included in your articles digest email. Ryze never unpublishes on its own; use **Republish** or **Unpublish** on the article to act on it.
