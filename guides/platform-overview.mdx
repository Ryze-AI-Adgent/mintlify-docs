## In this guide

- Where paid ads and SEO live in the app
- What the agent can reach once your integrations are connected
- How dashboards, content and audits fit together

## The menu today

- **Home** — the agent's input box and everything that waits for you under **Needs you**, setup steps included.
- **AI Marketer** — **New chat**, **Projects**, **Chat History**, **Schedules**, **Templates** and **Reports**.
- **Integrations** — connect your ad accounts, analytics, CMS and other tools; the **Ryze Pixel** card is here too.
- **Brand** — who you are, what you sell and how you sound. The agent reads it on every turn.
- **Media** — your logos, product photos and videos.
- **Analytics** — the built-in **Ads Performance**, **Search Performance** and **Web Analytics** dashboards and your own dashboards.
- **Website** — **Conversions** and **Technical Audit**.
- **Ads** — **Managed Campaigns**, **My Creatives**, **Ad Templates** and **Competitor Ads**.
- **SEO** — **Content Plan**, **Keywords & Prompts**, **Backlinks & PR** and **Preferences**.
- **Local** — Google Maps rankings, reviews and posts.
- **Agentic** — **AI Visibility**.
