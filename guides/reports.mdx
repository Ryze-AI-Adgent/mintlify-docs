## In this guide

- Asking for a deck or a dashboard
- Updating a report you already made
- Sharing by link, PDF or email

## Where to find it

Open **AI Marketer → Reports**.

## Steps

1. Click **Generate Report** and tell the agent what to cover, or start from **View templates**. You can also ask for a report in any chat.
2. Choose the kind of report: a **Deck** (a snapshot you can download and email) or a **Dashboard** (a live view you share by link).
3. Open the report. Click **Download PDF** for a deck, or **Refresh data** for a dashboard.
4. Click **Update Report** to have the agent refresh the numbers or change sections.
5. Click **Share** to send a deck by email as a PDF, or to copy a link.
