## In this guide

- Browsing templates by category
- Selecting templates as reference
- Turning a template into your own creative

## Where to find it

Open **Ads → Ad Templates**.

## Steps

1. Browse the templates. Use **Category** to narrow them down.
2. Click the templates you like to select them. The bar at the bottom shows how many are selected; **Clear** starts over.
3. Click **Use as reference**. A chat with the agent opens with your templates attached, and the agent suggests angles and makes similar creatives for your brand.
4. Find the results in **Ads → My Creatives**.
