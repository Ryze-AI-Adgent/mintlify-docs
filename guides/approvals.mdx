- Where proposals show up: **Home → Needs you → View all**
- Reading a proposal: **Evidence**, **Proposed changes**, **Expected impact**, **Who can apply it**
- **Approve**, **Approve with a note** or **Reject**; **Copy** → **I applied it** for changes you make yourself
- **Notifications** at the top of the Inbox (Slack or email)

## Where to find it

Proposals show up on **Home** under **Needs you**. Click **View all** to open the **Inbox**, and filter by **SEO** or **Paid Ads**.

## Steps

1. Open a proposal and read it: **Evidence**, **Proposed changes** (before and after) and **Expected impact**. Opportunities — new things to create rather than fixes — show a draft and **Who can apply it** instead of a before-and-after.
2. Click **Approve** and the agent applies the change. Use the arrow next to the button to choose **Approve with a note** if you want something done differently.
3. Click **Reject** if the change isn't right — add an optional reason so the agent proposes better changes next time.
4. For a card marked **Can be applied by you**, **Copy** the draft, post it yourself, then click **I applied it** — or **Not for us**.
5. For a **Blocked** proposal, do what the blocker note says, then click **I fixed it**.
6. Click **Notifications** at the top of the Inbox to choose the Slack channels and email recipients that hear about new proposals.
