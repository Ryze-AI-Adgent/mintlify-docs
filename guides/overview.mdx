Every guide is a short walkthrough of one part of Ryze, recorded in the real app, with written steps under each video. Watch them in order or jump to what you need. The full playlist lives on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZChB6DhNgw4).

<Note>
  The videos show an earlier version of the app, and some menu names have changed since — for example, approvals now wait on **Home → Needs you**. Each guide notes where things are in the app today.
</Note>
