## In this guide

- Asking for creatives in your brand style
- Marking up an image with comments
- Finding your creatives in **My Creatives**

## Steps

1. Open **AI Marketer → New chat**, or click **Generate Creative** on **Ads → My Creatives**, and describe the creative you want: the product, the format, the angle and the style.
2. Use the **+** button in the message box to attach images from **Media**, add **Reference ads**, or fix the **Image aspect**.
3. Click a creative to open it fullscreen. Click **Add comments**, tap the spot you want changed, describe the change, and click **Send**. The agent regenerates with your notes.
4. Find every creative in **Ads → My Creatives**. From there you can **Download** it or **Iterate in chat**.
