## In this guide

- Reviewing a draft in the editor
- Asking for a rewrite on a specific passage
- Approving an article for publishing

## Where to find it

Open **SEO → Content Plan**.

## Steps

1. Find an article. Use the tabs — **All**, **Planned**, **Drafted**, **Published**, **Failed**, **Warnings** — and switch between **List view** and **Calendar view**.
2. Open an article to see its **Article score** (out of 100, based on nine checks such as internal links, FAQ section and meta tags).
3. Click **Edit** to change the title, slug, meta title and description, body, image or scheduled date yourself, then **Save**.
4. Click **Improve This Article** to open the agent next to the article. Tell it what to change — a passage, the title, the meta description — and it edits the article you are looking at.
5. In **Manual** publishing mode, drafts wait for you: Content Plan shows how many drafts there are to approve. Click **Review drafts**, open a draft and click **Publish now** when it's ready.

To publish less often, move the scheduled dates. To skip an article, delete it before its date.
