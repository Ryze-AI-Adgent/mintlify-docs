## In this guide

- Connecting your ad platforms (**Integrations → Advertising**)
- Reading **Analytics → Ads Performance**
- Handing a campaign to the agent with **Managed Campaigns**
- Asking where you're overspending — fixes arrive in **Home → Needs you**
- **Ad Templates** and **Competitor Ads** for inspiration

## Steps

1. Open **Integrations**, filter by **Advertising** and connect the ad accounts you use.
2. Open **Analytics → Ads Performance** to see spend, results and ROAS across platforms. Switch between the **Overview**, **Campaigns**, **Creatives** and **Portfolio View** tabs.
3. Ask the agent in **AI Marketer → New chat** — for example "Where am I overspending?". Fixes the agent finds on its own wait for your decision on **Home → Needs you**.
4. To hand a campaign to the agent, open **Ads → Managed Campaigns** and click **New Managed Campaign**. Pick the platforms and accounts, the daily budget and the goal, then click **Create campaign**.
5. For ideas, browse **Ads → Ad Templates** and **Ads → Competitor Ads**.
