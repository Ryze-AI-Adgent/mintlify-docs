## In this guide

- Running a scan and reading the Health Score
- Which issues Ryze can fix for you
- Approving fixes before they go live

## Where to find it

Open **SEO → Technical Audit**.

## Steps

1. Check when the last scan ran. The audit runs every week on its own and checks the 300 pages with the most impressions in your Search Console.
2. Click **Run Scan** to scan now. It takes a few minutes, and you can close the page while it works.
3. Read the **Health Score** (0–100) and the three scores under it: **Page Speed Score**, **LLM Optimization Score** and **SEO Optimization Score**. Click **What we check** for the full list of checks.
4. Go through **Issues Detected**. Each issue is **Critical**, **Warning** or **Notice**; switch between **Group by page** and **Group by issue**.
5. Click **Fix with agent** on a page or an issue. The agent gets the issue details and works on the fix in a chat with you.
6. If an issue is known and you accept it, open its actions menu and click **Acknowledge**.
