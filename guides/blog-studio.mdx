## In this guide

- Claiming a subdomain for your blog
- Styling the blog to match your brand
- Publishing without a CMS connection

## Where to find it

Open **SEO → Preferences** and click **Blog Studio ↗** — it opens the studio for your hosted blog. Blog Studio works with [Ryze Hosting](/integrations/ryze-hosting); if hosting isn't connected yet, the studio offers **Connect Ryze Hosting** first.

Inside, three modes:

- **Design** — favicon, site name and meta description, theme, colors, fonts, corners and layout, with a live preview. **Match my website style** asks the agent to pull the colors, fonts and header/footer from your live site.
- **Code** — custom code for your blog.
- **SEO** — the Search Console steps for your hosted blog, and the **Keep this blog out of Google** toggle if you also publish the same articles to your own CMS and don't want the hosted copy indexed. See [Ryze Hosting](/integrations/ryze-hosting#keeping-the-hosted-blog-out-of-google).

Your articles themselves live in **SEO → Content Plan**.
