## In this guide

- Setting the prompts Ryze monitors
- Reading visibility and share of voice
- Why a competitor gets cited and you do not

## Steps

1. Add prompts — the questions your buyers ask AI assistants. Open **SEO → Keywords & Prompts**, go to the **Prompts** tab and click **Add**. Enter one prompt per line or click **Suggest prompts**, then **Add prompts**.
2. Make sure Search Console and Google Analytics are connected. AI Visibility needs both.
3. Open **Analytics → AI Visibility**. Every prompt is asked on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity.
4. Read the results: **AI Share of Voice**, **Visibility Over Time**, **Visibility by Assistant**, **Rankings** of the brands that get named, **Top Domains** the assistants cite, and **Prompts** with the top brand for each.
5. Keep it fresh: click **Refresh** to ask the prompts again, or set **Auto-refresh** (**Settings** on **Keywords & Prompts**) to **Weekly** or **Monthly**.
