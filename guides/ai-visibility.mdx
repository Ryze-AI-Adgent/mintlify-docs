## In this guide

- Setting the prompts Ryze monitors
- Reading AI visits, named and not-named prompts
- Why a competitor gets cited and you do not

## Steps

1. Add prompts — the questions your buyers ask AI assistants. Open **SEO → Keywords & Prompts**, go to the **Prompts** tab and click **Add**. Enter one prompt per line or click **Suggest prompts**, then **Add prompts**.
2. Connect Google Analytics 4 or PostHog, or install the Ryze Pixel — the page counts visits from AI assistants through it.
3. Open **Agentic** in the sidebar. Every prompt is asked on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity.
4. Read the results: AI assistant visits and a card per assistant at the top, the **Fixes** that stop assistants from naming you, **Tracked prompts** (filter **Named** / **Not named**) and **AI readiness**. Click a prompt to see each answer, the pages it cites and the other brands it names.
5. Keep it fresh: click **Refresh** on **Keywords & Prompts** to ask the prompts again, or set **Auto-refresh** (**Settings** there) to **Weekly** or **Monthly**.
