## In this guide

- Exploring live ads in your industry
- Tracking specific competitors
- Using a competitor ad as reference

## Where to find it

Open **Ads → Competitor Ads**.

## Steps

1. On the **Explore** tab, browse live ads in your industry. Filter by platform (Google, Meta, LinkedIn), format, how long the ad has run and status, and sort by **Newest** or **Trending**.
2. On the **Tracked Competitors** tab, add up to 5 brands: click **Discover competitors** to pick from brands running ads in your space, or click **Add competitor**, enter the brand name and domain, and click **Track**. Their ads sync in 3–5 minutes.
3. Click the bell next to a tracked brand to get an email when it launches new ads.
4. Open an ad and click **Iterate with AI**. The agent creates your own version of it.
