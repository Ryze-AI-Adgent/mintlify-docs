## In this guide

- Adding Ryze from the Claude directory (**Add to Claude**) or as a custom connector with the link from **Home → Connect Claude**
- Choosing the workspaces and clicking **Allow connection**
- Asking your first question

## Steps

1. Add Ryze to Claude in one of two ways:
   - **From the setup chat** — on the **Talk to Ryze from Claude** step, click **Add to Claude**. It opens Ryze in the Claude directory; add it there, then come back and click **I've added it**.
   - **As a custom connector** — copy the connector link from the **Connect Claude** card on **Home**, or from the **Claude MCP** card under **Integrations → AI**. In Claude, open **Customize → Connectors**, click **+ Add → Add custom connector**, enter the name `Ryze AI` and paste the link, then click **Continue** and **Connect**.
2. On the Ryze page that opens, sign in if asked, choose the workspaces Claude can access under **Workspaces** (all are selected by default) and click **Allow connection**.
3. Start a new chat in Claude and ask — for example "Pull the last 7 days of Google Ads spend and break it down by campaign."

Any Claude plan works; on the Free plan you can add one custom connector. Full step-by-step with screenshots: [Connect Claude](/claude/connect).
