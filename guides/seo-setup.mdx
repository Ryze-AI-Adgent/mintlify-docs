## In this guide

- The setup chat: your website, **Is this you?**, connecting Search Console (required) and Google Analytics
- Choosing where articles are published and whether each one waits for your approval
- What happens next: Ryze picks keywords, plans mentions and builds your content plan — **Home** shows the progress
- When to expect results

## Steps

1. Start the setup chat. Ryze opens it when you first set up SEO. If you left before the end, open **Home** and click **Finish SEO setup** under **Finish setup**.
2. Enter your website. Ryze reads it and asks **Is this you?** — click **Yes, that's us**, or **No, let me fix it** to correct what you sell, your industry and your market.
3. Connect your data. Search Console is required — the chat says **Connect Search Console to continue.** Connect Google Analytics too if you can, so Ryze sees what converts.
4. Answer **Where should your articles be published?** — pick your platform and connect it. If none fits, choose **None of these fit**: Ryze asks which platform your site is on and hosts your blog on a free Ryze subdomain for now. You can connect your own domain later. See [Ryze Hosting](/integrations/ryze-hosting).
5. Answer **How should new articles go out?** — **Publish automatically**, or **I'll approve each one**.
6. Optionally add Ryze to Claude and invite your team, then finish the chat.

## What happens next

You don't pick keywords or pages yourself. On **Home**, Ryze shows each step as it runs: pulling your data, picking keywords, planning mentions, building your content plan and preparing publishing. Your articles then appear in **SEO → Content Plan**, and **When to expect results** on Home shows where you are on the timeline.
