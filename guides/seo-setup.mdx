## In this guide

- Where setup lives: the **Finish setup** checklist on **Home**
- The setup chat: your team, your website, **Is this you?**, connecting Search Console (you can skip it, but the Technical Audit needs it) and Google Analytics
- Choosing where articles are published and whether each one waits for your approval
- What happens next: Ryze picks keywords, plans mentions and builds your content plan — **Home** shows the progress
- When to expect results

## Steps

1. Start the setup chat. Ryze opens it right after checkout. Until setup is complete, **Home** shows a **Finish setup** checklist — if you left the chat before the end, click **Finish SEO setup** there. See [Home and Finish setup](/home).
2. Answer **How did you find us?** and **How big is your team?** (**Just me**, **2–5**, **6–20**, **21–50** or **51+**).
3. Optionally invite your team under **Who else is on your team?** — invites are free, no extra seats, and teammates get full access. Click **Skip for now** to do it later. If you answered **Just me**, this step is skipped.
4. Watch the two-minute video under **Seen how it works?** and click **I've watched**.
5. Enter your website. Ryze reads it and asks **Is this you?** — click **Yes, that's us**, or **No, let me fix it** to correct what you sell, your industry and your market.
6. Connect your data. Search Console shows what Google already sends you, and the [Technical Audit](/seo/optimizations) needs it to run. Connect Google Analytics too if you can, so Ryze sees what converts. To go on without them, click **Skip for now** — you can connect them later in **Integrations** or from the **Connect Search Console & Analytics** card on Home.
7. Answer **Where should your articles be published?** — pick your platform and connect it. Besides WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix and the other CMSs, the list includes Squarespace, Lovable, GoDaddy Airo, Base44 and Replit. If you're not ready, choose **Not now, connect my site later**: Ryze asks which platform your site is on and hosts your blog on a free Ryze subdomain for now. You can connect your own domain later. See [Ryze Hosting](/integrations/ryze-hosting).
8. Answer **How should new articles go out?** — **Publish automatically**, or **I'll approve each one**. Then click the button at the end of the chat.

After setup, your agent takes over in the same chat. It offers keywords to pick, connects your Google Business Profile if you're a local business (and asks which location, if you have several), offers to audit your site and fix the errors with you, and writes your first article with a **Publish now** option. ## What happens next

You don't pick keywords or pages yourself. On **Home**, Ryze shows each step as it runs: pulling your data, picking keywords, planning mentions, building your content plan and preparing publishing. Your articles then appear in **SEO → Content Plan** — up to 90 per billing cycle, published at up to 3 a day on weekdays. The [Technical Audit](/guides/technical-audit) scans your site every week once Search Console is connected, checking the pages that get the most impressions. **When to expect results** on Home shows where you are on the timeline.
