## In this guide

- Where setup lives: the **Finish setup** checklist on **Home**
- The setup chat: your website, **Is this you?**, connecting Search Console (optional, but recommended) and Google Analytics
- Choosing where articles are published and whether each one waits for your approval
- What happens next: Ryze picks keywords, plans mentions and builds your content plan — **Home** shows the progress
- When to expect results

## Steps

1. Start the setup chat. Ryze opens it right after checkout. Until setup is complete, **Home** shows a **Finish setup** checklist — if you left the chat before the end, click **Finish SEO setup** there. See [Home and Finish setup](/home).
2. Enter your website. Ryze reads it and asks **Is this you?** — click **Yes, that's us**, or **No, let me fix it** to correct what you sell, your industry and your market.
3. Connect your data. Search Console is optional, but recommended: without it Ryze can't see what Google already sends you or whether your articles get indexed. Connect Google Analytics too if you can, so Ryze sees what converts. To go on without them, click **Skip for now** — you can connect them later from the **Connect Search Console & Analytics** card on Home.
4. Optionally invite your team — they get full access. Click **Skip for now** to do it later.
5. Answer **Where should your articles be published?** — pick your platform and connect it. If none fits, choose **None of these fit**: Ryze asks which platform your site is on and hosts your blog on a free Ryze subdomain for now. You can connect your own domain later. See [Ryze Hosting](/integrations/ryze-hosting).
6. Answer **How should new articles go out?** — **Publish automatically**, or **I'll approve each one**. Then click the button at the end of the chat.

## What happens next

You don't pick keywords or pages yourself. On **Home**, Ryze shows each step as it runs: pulling your data, picking keywords, planning mentions, building your content plan and preparing publishing. Your articles then appear in **SEO → Content Plan** — up to 90 per billing cycle, published at up to 3 a day on weekdays. The [Technical Audit](/guides/technical-audit) scans your site every week; with Search Console connected, it checks the pages that get the most impressions. **When to expect results** on Home shows where you are on the timeline.
