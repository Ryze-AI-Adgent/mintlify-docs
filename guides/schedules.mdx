## In this guide

- Turning a task into a schedule
- Choosing frequency and recipients
- Editing or pausing a schedule

## Where to find it

Open **AI Marketer → Schedules**.

## Steps

1. Click **Create with Agent** and say what you want in chat, or open a ready-made task from **View templates**. The agent asks a few questions and creates the scheduled task.
2. Open the task and click **Edit** to fine-tune it: **When should this run?** (**On a repeating schedule** or **Once, at a specific time**), **How often should this run?**, the time and **Timezone**, and the Slack channels and email recipients that get each result. Click **Save**.
3. Click **Run now** to test the task right away.
4. Use the toggle to pause or resume a repeating task. **Refine with Agent** lets the agent change it for you.

Each run is billed in credits like any agent work. See [Credits](/agent/credits).
