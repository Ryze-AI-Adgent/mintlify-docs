## In this guide

- How partners are matched to your articles
- Where exchange links are placed
- How often links go out

## Steps

1. Let it run. The **Backlink exchange** switch is on by default and there is no outreach to do — exchange links start once you have published articles on your own domain. Who takes part and how partners are matched: [Backlink Exchange](/seo/backlink-exchange).
2. See your links: open **SEO → Backlinks & PR** and go to the **Backlinks** tab.
3. To opt out, open **SEO → Preferences → Publishing** and turn off **Backlink exchange**.
