Category pages are usually the highest-value SEO pages on an ecommerce site: they match broad commercial queries and concentrate internal links. They are also usually the thinnest pages on the site — a heading and a product grid.

On Shopify these pages are called **collections**, and the rules are the same on every platform. The full guide — content above and below the grid, one page per intent, titles and meta, pagination, empty pages and structured data — is in [collection page optimisation](/shopify/collection-pages).
