LLM SEO is the practice of optimizing content so large language models retrieve, understand, and reproduce it accurately when answering questions.

It is a loose umbrella term. In most usage it means the same thing as [generative engine optimization](/concepts/generative-engine-optimization); some practitioners use it more narrowly for the model-facing side — how content is parsed and represented — as distinct from how it is discovered.

## The two paths into an answer

A model produces information about a brand either by searching the live web at answer time (retrieval) or from what it absorbed during training. Mentions come from the model's training data and move slowly; citations come from live retrieval and respond to content — see [AI visibility](/concepts/ai-visibility).

## What LLM SEO changes about writing

The writing rules are the same as for GEO — front-load the fact, write self-contained sections, name the subject, state limits, keep URLs stable. See [how to write for AI answers](/concepts/generative-engine-optimization#how-to-write-for-ai-answers).

## What it does not do

LLM SEO does not let you control what a model says about you. It improves the odds that when a model looks for information, it finds an accurate, well-structured source rather than a competitor's page or an outdated third-party summary.
