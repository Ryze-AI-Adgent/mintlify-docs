Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring content so it can be used as the direct answer to a question — in AI assistants, featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, and voice results.

## AEO vs GEO

The terms overlap and are often used interchangeably. AEO predates generative engines — it grew out of optimizing for featured snippets — while GEO is the newer framing for multi-source generated answers. The techniques converge, so the comparison and the writing rules live in one place: see [generative engine optimization](/concepts/generative-engine-optimization#also-called-aeo-and-llm-seo) and [how to write for AI answers](/concepts/generative-engine-optimization#how-to-write-for-ai-answers).

## Question formats worth targeting

- Definitional — "what is X"
- Comparative — "X vs Y"
- Procedural — "how to do X"
- Evaluative — "best X for Y"

Definitional and procedural questions are the most reliably winnable, because there is a correct answer and a clearly-written page can supply it. Evaluative questions tend to be answered from a mix of sources and brand priors.
