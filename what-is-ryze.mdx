Ryze AI is a marketing agent for ecommerce brands. It runs two things — paid advertising and SEO — and connects your marketing accounts to AI assistants so you can manage them in plain language instead of through dashboards.

## What it does

**Paid Ads.** An AI copilot for campaigns across Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Microsoft Ads and more. Ask about spend, ROAS, and performance; create campaigns; generate ad creatives; research competitor ads through the Meta ad library.

**SEO Autopilot.** An engine that audits your site, chooses keywords and pages worth targeting, then writes and publishes articles, ships on-page technical fixes, and builds off-page mentions. It runs on a monthly cycle rather than requiring step-by-step direction.

Both run in the same account and workspace.

## How you use it

Ryze works through **MCP** (Model Context Protocol). You add Ryze as a connector in [Claude](/claude/connect) or [ChatGPT](/chatgpt/connect), then ask questions in an ordinary chat. Ryze exposes tools the assistant calls on your behalf — Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, Search Console, and more.

There is also a dashboard at [reports.get-ryze.ai](https://reports.get-ryze.ai) for reports, agent activity, and settings.

## What it connects to

Ryze supports 50+ integrations across three categories:

- **Analytics** — Google Analytics, Google Search Console
- **Advertising** — Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Microsoft Ads, and other ad platforms
- **Commerce & CMS** — Shopify, WordPress, Webflow, Framer, Wix, Ghost, GitHub

Connections are made in **Workspace Settings → Integrations**, not inside the assistant. See [Connect your platforms](/integrations/overview).

## Who it is for

Ecommerce brands and the agencies that run marketing for them. Workspaces separate clients or sites, and [white-label branding](/white-label/branding) is available for agencies reselling the product.

## What it does not do

- **It is not a dashboard product.** The primary interface is a chat assistant. If you want to click through charts, this is not the shape of tool you are looking for.
- **It does not act without permission.** Actions that change your accounts require approval. See [Permissions & approvals](/claude/permissions).
- **It does not invent metrics.** Search volume and keyword difficulty come from real DataForSEO data; topics are built from your actual Search Console and store signals.
- **The free Claude plan will not work.** Custom connectors require a paid Claude plan (Pro, Team, or Max).

| **Paid Ads Autopilot** | $89/month | Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn and more; unlimited accounts; Claude integration; Meta ad library research |
| **SEO Autopilot** | $129/month | Full SEO and GEO optimization; 100+ pages generated per month; pages tuned to demand from 39 sources |
| **Traffic Printer** | $599/month | SEO Autopilot plus 100+ backlinks per month; DA 40+ and 30%+ traffic growth guaranteed or money back |
| **Ecom Autopilot** | $1,499/month | Paid Ads and SEO packages combined; 30% more traffic and 15% more conversions guaranteed or money back; sell directly in ChatGPT via UCP |

Paid Ads Autopilot includes a 7-day free trial. SEO Autopilot can be trialled from $1 for 3 days.

Usage is metered in credits. Failed runs, system errors, and internal tool calls do not consume credits — you are only charged for work that lands. See [Credits & usage](/agent/credits).

Current pricing is at [get-ryze.ai/payment-setup](https://www.get-ryze.ai/payment-setup). For invoices, payment method, and plan changes, see [Billing](/billing/invoices-and-payment).
