Managed Campaigns are autonomous. You set the mandate — budget, platforms, conversion goal, location, landing page — and the agent does the rest: creates the ads, launches them on your connected ad accounts, and keeps reviewing performance.

Find them under **Ads → Managed Campaigns** in the sidebar. In a workspace with Paid Ads and no campaign yet, the chat also shows a **Your agent runs your ads from one daily budget** banner — click **Create campaign** to start from there.

## How it works

1. **Create a campaign** — click **New Managed Campaign** and go through the three steps described below. Anything the agent should know — offers to push, claims to avoid — goes in **Anything the agent should know?**
2. **The agent sets it up** — it checks your budget, landing page and conversion tracking, generates on-brand creatives, builds the campaign structure, and launches it. It stops and asks you on the campaign page only when it can't launch without you — at most two questions.
3. **The agent keeps reviewing it** — it reads performance, pauses losers, shifts budget to winners inside your total, and rotates fresh creatives when the current ones fatigue. It sets the date of its next review itself — you see it in **Next check** on the campaign page.
4. **You watch Progress** — every run ends with a short note in **Progress** on the campaign page, and each entry links to the full agent chat.

Inside the mandate the agent acts on its own. Changes it wants to make beyond the mandate — a higher budget, a new platform, a different goal — come to your [Inbox](/agent/approvals) as approvals you decide on. You can also approve or reject them by telling your agent in chat. If you ask for such a change yourself in **Tell agent**, the agent applies it (see [Steer the campaign](#steer-the-campaign)).

## Create a campaign

Click **New Managed Campaign**. The dialog has three steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Where should it run?">
    Enter the daily budget in US dollars under **How much per day?**. The estimated monthly ad spend for that budget shows right under the field. For budgets of $250/day and more, the dialog reminds you that the budget is in USD, not in your local currency.

    Under **Where should your ads run?**, tick the platforms and pick the ad account for each. Only platforms with a connected ad account are shown — connect them under [Integrations](/integrations/overview) first. With no ad account connected, the dialog says "Connect an ad account first — campaigns run on your connected accounts." and shows a **Connect an ad account** button. With two or more platforms, set each platform's share of the budget; the shares must add up to 100%.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What should it drive?">
    Under **What do you want more of?**, pick the goal: **More sales**, **More leads**, **More sign-ups**, **More trials**, **More subscribers**, **More add-to-carts**, **More checkouts**, **More bookings**, **More inquiries** or **More traffic**.

    Next to each option you see how many of those events your Meta and Google Ads accounts recorded in the last 30 days (**N events / 30d**, in amber when it's zero). **—** means Ryze can't count that event for the selected platforms. If you pick a goal with no tracked events, the dialog warns that the agent can't optimize for it — fix tracking or pick another goal.

    Then enter your landing page URL under **Where should ads send people?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Campaign details">
    Everything is pre-filled — adjust if you want:

    - **Campaign name**.
    - **Where are your customers?** — the country to target.
    - **Special ad category** — **None**, **Financial products**, **Employment**, **Housing** or **Social or political**. Pick one if it applies to your ads: the ad platforms require it.
    - **Anything the agent should know?** — optional: offers to push, audiences or claims to avoid, tone of voice, seasonal angles.

    Click **Create campaign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Creating the campaign authorizes the agent to build and launch it — setup starts on its own right away. To review it before it goes live, see [Can I review the campaign before it goes live?](#faq) below.

## What happens in your ad account

- The agent creates its own campaign on each platform, named "*your campaign name* [Managed by Ryze]" (plus a short id if that name is already taken), in the exact ad account you picked. Ad sets and ads inside it get plain descriptive names.
- Your existing campaigns are never paused, edited or reused. Before building, the agent reads the last 90 days of the account — what spent, what converted, top ads, converting search terms — to learn from it.
- If one of your own campaigns already sends traffic to the same landing page or bids on the same keywords or audience, the agent leaves it alone and names the overlap in **Progress** so you can decide.
- Everything it builds is visible in each platform's own ads manager. On the campaign page, every ad has an **Open on *platform*** link.

## Campaign statuses

- **Not launched** — just created. Setup starts on its own ("Launch queued — the agent picks this campaign up in a moment"). If it doesn't start, a **Launch** button appears.
- **Setting up** — the agent is checking your account, tracking and landing page, and building the campaign.
- **Needs your input** — the agent stopped with questions. Answer them in the reply box on the campaign page, or tell your agent in chat — it passes your answer on to the campaign.
- **Live** — running. The agent reviews it on its own schedule — see **Next check**.
- **Paused** — delivery is stopped on the platforms. **Resume** turns it back on — the agent first checks that the landing page is up and conversion tracking still works.
- **Failed** — setup couldn't finish. **Retry setup** starts it again.
- **Archived** — ended by the agent at your request.

You may also see **Blocked** with an **I fixed it** button when something outside Ryze stops delivery. It's rare — normally the agent asks you through **Needs your input** instead.

## The campaign page

- **Top row** — **Budget**, **Goal**, **Location**, **Age**, **Managed for** and **Next check**.
- **Results** — **Spend**, **Results**, **CPA** and **ROAS**.
- **Ads** — every ad the agent built, with **Spend**, **Impressions**, **Clicks**, **CPA** and **ROAS**. Sort by any of them and filter by **Active**, **Inactive** or **All statuses**. Click an ad for its preview and the **Open on *platform*** link.
- **Refresh from the ad platforms** pulls fresh numbers on demand.
- **Progress** — the agent's notes from every run. **View all** shows the full history. Right after launch it reads "The agent just started — its updates appear here as it works."
- The page counts only the platforms in the campaign's mandate.

## Platforms

Managed Campaigns run on Meta, Google Ads, LinkedIn, Microsoft Ads, TikTok, Snapchat, Reddit, Pinterest and OpenAI Ads — on whichever of them you've connected under **Integrations**.

## What the agent will never do

- Set budgets above your mandate.
- Add platforms, widen targeting, or change your landing page on its own.
- Touch anything in your ad account outside the campaign it manages.

Two things to know about the budget:

- **It's a daily amount in US dollars.** If your ad account bills in another currency, the agent converts the amount into that currency when it sets the budget on the platform.
- **Platforms can spend more than the daily budget on a strong day** — Meta up to 75% more, Google up to twice — and even it out over the following days. That's how platform daily budgets work.

## If there are no results yet

The agent never stops a campaign just because the budget is small or results are slow. What it does instead:

- **Before the verdict date** (when the budget has had time to collect about 40 conversions) — it keeps working on delivery, keywords, search terms, bids and creatives every review, but doesn't call the CPA or ROAS good or bad yet.
- **Day 14 with nothing to show** — 14 days of delivery without a single result (for a sales goal: ROAS below 1) — the agent makes one change it names, such as a fresh batch of creatives on new angles, new negative keywords, or moving money to the part that converts. It tells you in **Progress** what it changed and why. The campaign keeps running.
- **Pause for no results** — only after 30+ days of delivery with zero results, spend above 3× the expected cost per result, and conversion tracking re-checked and working. Then the agent pauses the campaign, marks it **Needs your input** and names what it tried and the ways out: a goal that happens more often, another connected platform, or another landing page or offer.
- **Winners get more** — after the verdict date, if the campaign beats its target and spends its whole daily budget, the agent sends a "Raise daily budget from X to Y" approval for +20% to your [Inbox](/agent/approvals).

A ROAS that looks too good (above about 20) is checked for double-counted conversions before the agent scales anything.

## Steer the campaign

- **Tell agent** — send instructions in your own words ("stop pushing the bundle offer, lead with free shipping"). The agent acts on them and reports back in **Progress**. Available while the campaign is Live or Paused. You can also ask here for changes the **Edit mandate** dialog doesn't cover — "add Google Ads", "optimize for leads instead": your instruction becomes part of the mandate and the agent applies it on the platforms.
- **Edit mandate** — from the campaign's menu, change the daily budget, location, landing page, the split between platforms and **Anything the agent should know?**. Available while the campaign is Live or Paused. Click **Send to the manager** and the agent applies the difference on the ad platforms. A budget change of more than about 20% or a targeting change resets the platforms' learning phase — expect 3–7 days of noisier delivery.
- **Pause** and **Resume** — stop and restart delivery on the platforms.

## Pricing

Managed Campaigns are part of your Paid Ads plan. The agents that set up and run a Managed Campaign — setup, reviews, new creatives, your **Tell agent** messages — don't use your credits. See [Credits & Usage](/agent/credits). Ad spend itself is billed by the ad platforms to your ad account, as usual.

## FAQ

**Can I stop it at any time?**
Yes — **Pause** on the campaign page stops all delivery on the platforms. **Resume** turns it back on. To remove a campaign from Ryze, pause it first, then choose **Delete** from the menu; ads already created on the ad platforms stay in your ad account.

**Can I review the campaign before it goes live?**
By default, creating the campaign authorizes the agent to launch it. If you want to approve first, write "Don't launch without my approval" in **Anything the agent should know?**. The agent builds everything paused, marks the campaign **Needs your input** and waits. Review the ads in your ad account (or on the campaign page), then give your go in the reply box on the campaign page — the agent launches.

**Will the agent launch on a small budget?**
Yes. The budget is your choice — the agent launches with any daily budget at or above each platform's minimum. Before building, it works out how long the budget needs to collect roughly 40 conversions at your expected cost per result, and tells you that date honestly. A small budget gets a smaller structure: one ad set, the tightest keyword group, the strongest ads. If the daily budget (or a platform's share of it) is below that platform's minimum, the agent asks you on the campaign page whether to raise it to the minimum or run on the other platforms only.

On a small budget with a sales goal, the agent may suggest optimizing for add-to-carts so the platform learns faster — it never switches the goal on its own. If the conversion event hasn't recorded anything recently, the agent still launches and checks the event on its first reviews.

**Why did the agent ask me to raise the budget?**
When the budget would take more than about 30 days to reach a verdict, the agent sends a "Raise daily budget from X to Y" approval to your [Inbox](/agent/approvals) — once. Approve it, reject it, or ignore it: the campaign keeps running either way, and the agent doesn't bring the budget up again. Separately, a campaign that beats its target after the verdict date gets a +20% budget approval (see [If there are no results yet](#if-there-are-no-results-yet)).

**What if the agent has a question?**
The campaign shows "The agent needs your input" with its questions. Answer in the reply box — or tell your agent in chat, and it passes your answer on to the campaign — and the agent picks up exactly where it stopped. The organization owner also gets an email with the agent’s questions and a link to the campaign — and a message in your default Slack channel if Slack is connected. Campaigns waiting for you also show up in the **Needs you** list on Home.

**Whose ad accounts does it use?**
Yours. The agent works inside the ad accounts you connected — spend goes to the ad platforms as usual, and everything it builds is visible in each platform's own ads manager. See [What happens in your ad account](#what-happens-in-your-ad-account).

**What happens to my campaigns if I cancel Paid Ads?**
When the Paid Ads subscription for the workspace ends, each live Managed Campaign is paused on the ad platforms at its next scheduled check (the **Next check** date on the campaign page). Until then it keeps running and spending as set up — so if you want ads to stop right away, press **Pause** on the campaign before you cancel. Nothing is deleted. While the plan is inactive the **Ads** section is locked; once you resubscribe, **Progress** shows "Paused: paid-ads subscription ended" and **Resume** turns the campaign back on.

**I got "You already have … active campaigns" when creating a campaign. What now?**
A workspace can have up to 5 Managed Campaigns at once — paused campaigns and ones waiting for your input count too; archived and failed ones don't. A paused campaign keeps its slot until you delete it. To free a slot, ask the agent to end a campaign (it's archived), or pause a campaign and choose **Delete** from its menu (ads already created stay in your ad account), then create the new one.
