Find it under **Ads → Competitor Ads** in the sidebar. Ryze pulls the ads brands are running right now across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn — so you always know what your competitors are putting out.

The page has two tabs:

- **Explore** — live ads from brands in your industry, whether you track them or not. Filter by industry, platform, format, duration and status, and sort by **Newest** or **Trending**. Like what a brand is doing? Use the track button on its ad to add it to your list.
- **Tracked Competitors** — the ads of the brands you track.

## Track a competitor

Click **Add competitor**, then enter the brand name and its domain, and click **Track**.

When you don't have any competitors yet, the Tracked Competitors tab offers **Discover competitors**: Ryze finds brands running ads in your space and you pick the ones to track (or **Add manually**).

The first sync usually takes 3–5 minutes — the page updates on its own when it's ready. **Re-sync** at the top pulls fresh ads for every tracked brand on demand.

<Tip>
You can track up to 5 competitors per workspace. Pick the brands you actually compete with for the most useful view.
</Tip>

## Browse their ads

On **Tracked Competitors**, each brand is a chip at the top — click one to see only its ads. Use the filters to narrow things down:

- **Platform** — Google, Meta, or LinkedIn.
- **Format** — text, image, or video.
- **Duration** — how long an ad has been running. Ads that run a long time are usually the ones that work.
- **Status** — active or inactive.

Click any ad to open it. You'll see the format, platform, how long it's been running, and whether it's still active — plus **Open original**.

To stop tracking a brand, click the trash icon on its chip.

## Get an email when they launch new ads

Click the bell icon on a competitor's chip to turn on new-ad alerts, and Ryze emails you when they ship new ads — so you don't have to keep checking.

How it works:

- **Ryze refreshes your competitors' ads once a week** and checks each tracked brand for new creatives.
- **If a competitor launched new ads, you get one email** with a summary of who shipped what, and a link back to this page.
- **No new ads, no email.** You only hear from Ryze when there's something new to see.

<Note>
Alerts are off by default — turn them on per competitor.
</Note>

## Turn an ad into your own

Found an ad worth borrowing? Click **Iterate with AI** to drop its copy straight into chat, then ask Ryze to rework it for your brand.

> Here's a competitor's ad copy. Write me 3 stronger variations I can use.

<Tip>
Pair this with [AI Ad Creatives](/paid-ads/ai-creatives) — rewrite the copy, then generate matching visuals on your brand.
</Tip>
