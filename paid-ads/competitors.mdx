Track any brand and Ryze pulls the ads they're running right now across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn — so you always know what your competitors are putting out.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/competitors-page.png" alt="The Competitors page with live ads from tracked brands" />
</Frame>

## Track a competitor

Click **Track competitor**, then add a brand name and its website.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/competitors-track-dialog.png" alt="Tracking a competitor by name and domain" />
</Frame>

Ryze starts pulling their live Google and Meta ads right away. The first sync takes a couple of minutes — the page updates on its own when it's ready.

<Tip>
You can track up to 5 competitors per workspace. Pick the brands you actually compete with for the most useful view.
</Tip>

## Browse their ads

Each competitor shows a strip of the ads they're currently running. Use the filters at the top to narrow things down:

- **Platform** — Google, Meta, or LinkedIn.
- **Duration** — how long an ad has been running. Ads that run a long time are usually the ones that work.
- **Status** — active or inactive.

Click **View all** on any competitor to see their full ad library.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/competitors-all-ads.png" alt="The full gallery of a competitor's live ads" />
</Frame>

Click any ad to open it. You'll see the format, platform, how long it's been running, and whether it's still active — plus a link to the original.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/competitors-creative-lightbox.png" alt="A single competitor ad opened with its details" />
</Frame>

## Get an email when they launch new ads

Turn on alerts for any competitor and Ryze emails you when they ship new ads — so you don't have to keep checking.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/competitors-notify-dialog.png" alt="Enabling new-ad email alerts for a competitor" />
</Frame>

How it works:

- **Ryze refreshes your competitors' ads once a week.** Every Monday it checks each tracked brand for new creatives.
- **If a competitor launched new ads, you get one email** that morning with a summary of who shipped what, and a link back to this page.
- **No new ads, no email.** You only hear from Ryze when there's something new to see.

<Note>
Alerts are off by default — turn them on per competitor. The alert email goes to your organization's owner.
</Note>

## Turn an ad into your own

Found an ad worth borrowing? Click **Iterate with AI** to drop its copy straight into chat, then ask Ryze to rework it for your brand.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/competitors-iterate-chat.png" alt="Iterating on a competitor's ad copy in chat" />
</Frame>

> Here's a competitor's ad copy. Write me 3 stronger variations I can use.

<Tip>
Pair this with [AI Ad Creatives](/paid-ads/ai-creatives) — rewrite the copy, then generate matching visuals on your brand.
</Tip>
