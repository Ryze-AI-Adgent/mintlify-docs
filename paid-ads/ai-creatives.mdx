Ryze generates ready-to-run ad creatives from a short brief — on-brand and in multiple directions.

## Generate creatives in chat

Just describe what you want:

## Write a good brief

The more you give Ryze, the better the output. Include:

- **Brand** — who it's for and the vibe
- **Format** — e.g. vertical 4:5 for feed
- **Angle** — the hook, offer, or message
- **Style** — modern, minimal, bold, etc.

<Tip>
Ask Ryze to iterate: swap a hook, change the angle, or push a direction further. Tell it what to keep and what to change.
</Tip>

## The + menu in the composer

The **+** button in the chat composer gives you four ways to steer a creative run without typing it all out:

- **Attach files** — upload images from your computer, or pick files from Google Drive.
- **Reference ads** — pick one or more of your existing ad creatives as visual references; they're attached to your message so the new directions follow their look.
- **Use brand kit** — a toggle that tells Ryze to use your workspace brand styles for everything it generates in this conversation.
- **Aspect** — **Auto**, **Square**, **Story**, or **Wide**. Pick one to fix the creative size for the whole conversation instead of describing the format in every message.

Brand kit and aspect are conversation-level settings: once set, every creative in that chat follows them.


## Add your brand references

Ryze pulls your logo and palette from your site automatically and feeds them into every creative it generates. To steer the look further, add reference creatives.

Open **Brand** in the sidebar, switch to the **Visual** tab and find **Ad creative examples**. Ryze auto-pulls creatives from your connected Meta and LinkedIn ad accounts; click **Add ad creatives** to upload your own. They're saved per workspace and used as visual references the next time you generate.

<Tip>
No references yet? Ryze still generates from your brief and your site's identity — but adding a few of your best-performing creatives is the fastest way to make every new one look unmistakably yours.
</Tip>

## Review, annotate, and iterate

Every creative in chat opens **fullscreen** — click it to inspect the details, download it, or leave feedback right on the image.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/creative-fullscreen.png" alt="A creative opened fullscreen with Add comments and Download buttons" />
</Frame>

Click **Add comments**, then tap anywhere on the image and describe the change — "remove logo", "make the headline bigger", "different background". Each note is pinned to the exact spot you tapped.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/creative-annotate.png" alt="Adding a pinned comment on a creative" />
</Frame>

Hit **Send** and your notes go back to the agent as visual feedback — it sees the circled areas and regenerates with your changes applied.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/creative-feedback-chat.png" alt="The agent receiving annotated feedback in chat" />
</Frame>

## See your live ads in chat

The same widget shows creatives that are already running. Ask about your Meta, LinkedIn, Snapchat, or TikTok ads and the agent pulls the actual images with their performance — spend, clicks, CTR — side by side.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/chat-creatives-grid.png" alt="Live Meta creatives with performance stats shown in chat" />
</Frame>

Combined with annotation, this closes the loop: pull a live ad, circle what's tired, and have the agent generate the refresh.
