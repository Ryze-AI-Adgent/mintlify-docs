Ryze generates ready-to-run ad creatives from a short brief — on-brand and in multiple directions.

## Generate creatives in chat

Just describe what you want:

> Can you help me generate creatives with a modern style for the Ryze AI brand?

Ryze returns several distinct directions, each with its own aesthetic — so you can pick the lane that fits.

## Write a good brief

The more you give Ryze, the better the output. Include:

- **Brand** — who it's for and the vibe
- **Format** — e.g. vertical 4:5 for feed
- **Angle** — the hook, offer, or message
- **Style** — modern, minimal, bold, etc.

<Tip>
Ask Ryze to iterate: swap a hook, change the angle, or push a direction further. Tell it what to keep and what to change.
</Tip>

## UGC video ads

Ask the agent for a UGC video and it renders a vertical, phone-shot-looking clip: an AI person holding **your real product** and speaking to camera, lip-synced, in a natural voice. Rendering takes up to five minutes — you don't have to wait in the chat; the finished video lands in **Ads → My Creatives** on its own.

It works from your actual product photos, not a description. The agent writes a brief covering the product as it appears in the photo, who is holding it, the room, and the exact spoken lines. You can direct any of that — a different actor, a different room, or your own script.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 5–12 seconds (8 by default). **Cost:** 10 credits per video.

- **Films well:** rigid packaged goods with one large, clear label — bottles, cans, jars, boxes, devices.
- **Films badly:** fabric with dense repeated print, and anything where fine print has to stay legible while the product moves. If your product is in this group, the agent tells you instead of handing you a clip with a scrambled label.
- **Give it one clear product photo**, and write the dialogue like speech — two to four short sentences a person would actually say.
- **If a render comes back rejected**, the brief tripped content moderation or the photo was unusable. Change the brief or the photo — the same brief fails the same way.
- The person on screen is generated for that clip — never ask for a real, named person.

## The + menu in the composer

The **+** button in the chat composer gives you three ways to steer a creative run without typing it all out:

- **Attach files** — **Upload from computer**, **Choose from Media** (your workspace's image library), or **Upload from Drive**.
- **Reference ads** — pick one or more of your existing ad creatives as visual references; they're attached to your message so the new directions follow their look.
- **Image aspect** — **Auto**, **Square** (1:1), **Story** (9:16), or **Wide** (1.91:1). Pick one to fix the creative size for the conversation instead of describing the format in every message.

## Add your brand references

Ryze reads your brand colors and fonts from your site and applies them to every creative it generates. You can review and edit them under **Brand → Visual**.

Product photos, logos and your best-performing creatives go to **Media** in the sidebar — upload them there and the agent picks from them before it looks anywhere else. See [Media library](/workspaces/media).

<Tip>
No references yet? Ryze still generates from your brief and your site's identity — but adding a few of your best-performing creatives to Media is the fastest way to make every new one look unmistakably yours.
</Tip>

## Review, annotate, and iterate

Every creative in chat opens **fullscreen** — click it to inspect the details, download it, or leave feedback right on the image.

Click **Add comments**, then tap anywhere on the image and describe the change — "remove logo", "make the headline bigger", "different background". Each note is pinned to the exact spot you tapped.

Hit **Send** and your notes go back to the agent as visual feedback — it sees the circled areas and regenerates with your changes applied.

## See your live ads in chat

The same widget shows creatives that are already running. Ask about your Meta, LinkedIn, Snapchat, or TikTok ads and the agent pulls the actual images with their performance — spend, clicks, CTR — side by side.

Combined with annotation, this closes the loop: pull a live ad, circle what's tired, and have the agent generate the refresh.

## Browse your creatives

All generated creatives live under **Ads → My Creatives** in the sidebar.
