**Ad Templates** is a library of ads that work, sorted by industry. Use them as visual references: pick the ones you like and the agent makes versions for your brand. Find it under **Ads → Ad Templates** in the sidebar.

## Browse by category

Filter the library with **Category**: Beauty & Personal Care, Fashion & Apparel, Food & Beverage, Home & Garden, Pet, Baby & Kids, Electronics, Automotive, Outdoor & Sports, Health & Fitness, Software & SaaS, Finance & Fintech, Healthcare & Medical, Travel & Hospitality, or Other. **All** shows every template.

## Turn templates into your own ads

1. Click the templates you like to select them — up to 3 at a time. **Clear** resets the selection.
2. Click **Use as reference**.
3. A chat opens with the templates attached. The agent makes versions for your brand and suggests a few angles for how to run them.

Then iterate as with any creative — see [AI Ad Creatives](/paid-ads/ai-creatives).

## Other places to find templates

- **Ads → My Creatives** — the **Start from a winning ad in your niche** shelf above your creatives.
- **The chat composer** — **+ → Reference ads → Templates** attaches templates to any message.
