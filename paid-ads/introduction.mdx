Paid Ads gives your AI marketer direct access to your ad accounts. Connect Google Ads, Meta and any other ad platform under **Integrations** once, then just ask — in the Ryze chat, or from Claude or ChatGPT if you've connected them.

## What you can do

- **Understand performance** — ask about spend, ROAS, CTR, and trends across accounts. **Analytics → Ads Performance** shows the same numbers as a dashboard.
- **Manage campaigns** — create campaigns, adjust budgets, pause or resume, with the approvals you choose. Or hand a campaign over completely with [Managed Campaigns](/paid-ads/managed-campaigns).
- **Generate creatives** — produce on-brand ad creatives from a short brief. See [AI Ad Creatives](/paid-ads/ai-creatives).
- **Watch competitors** — see the live ads other brands are running. See [Competitor Ads](/paid-ads/competitors).

Everything lives under **Ads** in the sidebar: **Managed Campaigns**, **My Creatives**, **Ad Templates** and **Competitor Ads**.

## Example prompts

Start a chat and try:

> Pull the last 7 days of Google Ads spend and break it down by campaign.

> Which Meta campaigns have the best ROAS this month?

> Generate 3 modern ad creatives for our spring launch.
