## What you can do

- **Understand performance** — ask about spend, ROAS, CTR, and trends across accounts.
- **Manage campaigns** — create campaigns, adjust budgets, pause or resume — with your approval.
- **Generate creatives** — produce on-brand ad creatives from a short brief.

## Example prompts

Start a chat and try:

> Pull the last 7 days of Google Ads spend and break it down by campaign.

> Which Meta campaigns have the best ROAS this month?

> Generate 3 modern ad creatives for our spring launch.
