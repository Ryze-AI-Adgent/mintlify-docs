Paid Ads gives your AI marketer direct access to your ad accounts. Connect Google Ads, Meta and any other ad platform under **Integrations** once, then just ask — in the Ryze chat, or from Claude or ChatGPT if you've connected them.

New here? Follow [Getting started with Paid Ads](/paid-ads/getting-started).

## What you can do

- **Understand performance** — ask about spend, ROAS, CTR, and trends across accounts. **Analytics → Ads Performance** shows the same numbers as a dashboard. See [Search and Ads Performance](/analytics/performance).
- **Manage campaigns** — create campaigns, adjust budgets, pause or resume, with the approvals you choose. Or hand a campaign over completely with [Managed Campaigns](/paid-ads/managed-campaigns).
- **Generate creatives** — produce on-brand ad creatives from a short brief. See [AI Ad Creatives](/paid-ads/ai-creatives).
- **Watch competitors** — see the live ads other brands are running. See [Competitor Ads](/paid-ads/competitors).
- **Track your site** — install the [Ryze Pixel](/paid-ads/ryze-pixel) once for first-party web analytics and, if you choose, forward conversions server-side to Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and GA4. It lives under **Analytics → Web Analytics**.

## Where everything lives

- **Analytics → Ads Performance** — the dashboard of your connected ad accounts.
- **Analytics → Web Analytics** — the Ryze Pixel and your site's analytics.
- **Ads → Managed Campaigns**, **Ads → My Creatives**, **Ads → Ad Templates** and **Ads → Competitor Ads**.

## Plan and access

- Paid Ads is a separate plan: $89/month with a 7-day free trial, covering up to 3 workspaces. The Agency plan ($279/month) covers unlimited workspaces. See [Plans & pricing](/plans/overview).
- Without it, the **Ads** section and **Ads Performance** show an **Unlock** badge.
- Web Analytics (Ryze Pixel) is available on every plan.
- Once your ad accounts are connected, the agent checks them several times a day on its own and sends proposed fixes to your [Inbox](/agent/approvals). This background research doesn't use your credits.

## Example prompts

Start a chat and try:

> Pull the last 7 days of Google Ads spend and break it down by campaign.

> Which Meta campaigns have the best ROAS this month?

> Generate 3 modern ad creatives for our spring launch.
