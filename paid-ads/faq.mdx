---

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Which ad platforms does Paid Ads support?">
Google Ads and Meta are the core. Claude can read any platform you've connected in your workspace.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is it safe to let Ryze create or change campaigns?">
Yes — changes go through approval. Set write and delete tools to **Needs approval** in Claude, and you confirm every change before it happens. See [Permissions & Approvals](/claude/permissions).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I generate ad creatives?">
Ask in chat — describe the brand, format, and style. See [AI Ad Creatives](/paid-ads/ai-creatives).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How often does Ryze refresh competitor ads?">
Once a week. Every Monday Ryze checks each competitor you track for new ads, and if you've turned on alerts, emails you a summary when one launches something new. See [Competitor Ads](/paid-ads/competitors).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Ryze run tasks automatically — a daily check, a weekly report?">
Yes. Use **Schedules** in the sidebar: the agent runs the task daily, weekly or monthly in a fresh chat and emails you the result. Each run costs credits per AI step, so keep the scope tight. See [Scheduled Tasks](/agent/schedules).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Ryze create and change campaigns itself, or only give advice?">
It acts directly in your connected accounts through the official APIs — creating campaigns, adjusting budgets, bids, keywords and creatives. Every change goes through Approvals (or "Needs approval" in Claude) before it lands. To start, describe the campaign in chat; new campaigns then go through the platform's own review (about a business day on Google).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use my own images, upload a CSV, or share a file with the agent?">
Yes. Use the **+** button in the chat composer to attach CSV, XLSX, PDF, MD or images, or pick from Google Drive. Logos and product shots go under **Brand → Visual**. The agent can also hand you files back — for example a CSV feed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export a report to Excel?">
Reports export as PDF (download or send by email) and as a shareable link. Excel/XLSX export isn't available; for a spreadsheet, ask the agent for the data as a table and paste it into your sheet.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I give Ryze read-only access?">
Ryze isn't read-only by design, but you control changes: keep write and delete tools on **Needs approval** and reject anything you don't want in Approvals. If you need a hard guarantee, connect a Google or Meta login that only has read access.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
