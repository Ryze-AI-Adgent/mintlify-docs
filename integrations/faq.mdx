<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How do I add a second Google Ads account? I only see Disconnect.">
Each platform connects one account per workspace. To use a second account on a different login, [create another workspace](/workspaces/create-workspace) and connect it there.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I manage many accounts. How should I set this up?">
Use one workspace per account or client. If you manage a large number and want help structuring it, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An account shows as disconnected even though it's active.">
Click **Reconnect** on the platform card in **Workspace Settings → Integrations**, then start a new chat in Claude.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I connected GSC and GA4 — is there anything else to do?">
Connect whichever platforms you want Claude to read. Nothing else is required — just start a new chat after connecting.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I connect Shopify?">
Shopify needs a manual setup step. Email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) and tell us which store domain you want connected.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
