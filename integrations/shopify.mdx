Connecting Shopify takes under a minute and needs only your store domain.

## The connect window

When you click **Connect** on the Shopify card, the window has two tabs:

- **Shopify app** (default) — enter your store domain and approve Ryze in your Shopify admin. No API keys to copy. The steps below cover this tab.
- **Custom app** — your store domain plus the **Client ID** and **Client secret** of an app you create in your store. See [Shopify Custom App](/integrations/shopify-custom-app).

## Connect with the Shopify app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations → Shopify">
    In Ryze, go to **Integrations** and click **Connect** on the **Shopify** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your store domain">
    On the **Shopify app** tab, type your `.myshopify.com` domain (e.g. `yourstore.myshopify.com`) and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve in Shopify">
    You're redirected to your Shopify admin in the same tab. Approve the access prompt, and you come back to Ryze with the store connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it — no app creation, no API keys, no credentials to copy.

## After connecting

Click **View** on the Shopify card to optionally choose:

- **Blog** — the existing Shopify blog your articles publish to;
- **Article template** — the theme article template published articles use, or **Theme default**.

Click **Save**. Both choices are optional.

## Several stores

Each store is its own connection. To add another one, click **View** → **Add one more account** and connect the next store. See [Connect multiple accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts).

## When you need a custom app

The standard Shopify app lets the agent edit product, collection, page and blog content and SEO fields. Theme file edits (for example `robots.txt`, structured data snippets or code in `theme.liquid`) and URL redirects need your own custom app — see [How do I connect Shopify?](/integrations/faq) in the Integrations FAQ and [Shopify Custom App](/integrations/shopify-custom-app).

## Troubleshooting

- **"Must be a valid yourstore.myshopify.com domain"** — enter only the `.myshopify.com` domain, not your custom domain or a URL with a path.
- **"This Shopify store is already connected to another Ryze workspace. Shopify keeps one live connection per store — disconnect it there first, then retry."** — open the other workspace → **Integrations** → Shopify, disconnect the store, then connect it here.
- **"The Shopify install took too long to come back. Open the link again to retry."** — open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on the Shopify card again.

More fixes: [Shopify not connecting](/troubleshooting/shopify-not-connecting). If it still fails, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) with your store domain.
