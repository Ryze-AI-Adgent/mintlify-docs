---
title: "Connect Shopify"
description: "Connect your Shopify store with just the store domain — Ryze handles the rest."
---

Connecting Shopify takes under a minute and needs only your store domain.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations → Shopify">
    In Ryze, go to **Integrations** and pick **Shopify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your store domain">
    Type your `.myshopify.com` domain (e.g. `yourstore.myshopify.com`) and continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve in Shopify">
    A Shopify window opens — approve the access prompt, and the connection completes automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it — no app creation, no API keys, no credentials to copy. If the approval window reports an error, retry from the Integrations page; if it persists, contact [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) with your store domain.
