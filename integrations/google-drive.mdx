---
title: "Connect Google Drive"
description: "Connect Google Drive so you can hand the agent Sheets, Docs, and files you pick — to read, update, or create."
---

Google Drive connects with **OAuth** on your Google account. Once connected, you pick specific files from the chat composer and the agent can read them, update them, or create new ones — for example, reading a keyword list from a Sheet, or writing a report into a Doc.

## Connect in Ryze

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar and find the **Google Drive** card (under **Files**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect and sign in with Google">
    Approve access. Ryze only works with the files you explicitly pick — it doesn't browse your Drive on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also connect straight from the chat: click **+** (**Add to message**) in the composer, then **Attach files** → **Upload from Drive**. If Drive isn't connected yet, Ryze starts the connection and brings you back to the file picker afterwards.

Drive connects one Google account per workspace.

## Using it in chat

Click **+** (**Add to message**) in the composer → **Attach files** → **Upload from Drive**, then pick one or more files in the Google picker. They're attached to your message, and the agent can read, update, or create files as you ask.

## Limits

- **Images** picked from Drive aren't supported — upload them from your computer instead (**Attach files** → **Upload from computer**).
- Files over **10 MB**, binary files, and Google formats other than Sheets, Docs and Slides are attached by name only — the agent can't read their content.
- When the agent writes into a Google Doc, it writes **plain text** (no headings, links or formatting), and it can only add text at the end or replace the whole document.

## Troubleshooting

- **Reconnect needed** — Google revoked the access (password change or a revoked app). Click **Reconnect**.
- **Picker shows the wrong account or no files** — the picker always opens the Google account you connected on the **Integrations** page; the account you're signed in to in the browser doesn't matter. To use another Google account, click **View** on the **Google Drive** card, disconnect it with the trash icon, and connect again with the other login. Only one Google account can be connected for Drive.
- **"Images from Google Drive aren't supported — upload them directly."** — upload the image from your computer instead.
