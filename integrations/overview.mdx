Connect your platforms in Ryze so the agent can read your data and act on it — in Ryze, and in Claude or ChatGPT if you've connected them. Most platforms are connected on the **Integrations** page; you can also connect many of them from Claude or ChatGPT with a link (see [below](#connect-from-claude-or-chatgpt-with-a-link)).

Ryze supports **50+ integrations**. They're grouped into seven categories, matching the filters on the Integrations page:

- **AI** — Anthropic, Claude MCP, ChatGPT MCP, Grok Bot, Custom MCP Server
- **Analytics** — Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Ahrefs, Semrush, Mixpanel, Amplitude, PostHog, CallRail, Microsoft Clarity
- **Commerce & CMS** — Shopify, WordPress, Framer, GitHub, HubSpot, Wix, Ryze Hosting, Ghost, Lightspeed eCom, Webflow, Magento, HighLevel, Directus, Custom Webhook, WooCommerce, Duda, Squarespace, Lovable, GoDaddy Airo, Base44, Replit, BigCommerce, Contentful, Sanity
- **CRM** — Zoho CRM, ClickUp
- **Advertising** — Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, OpenAI Ads, Thrad, Microsoft Ads, Snapchat Ads, TikTok Ads, Reddit Ads, Pinterest Ads, Criteo, StackAdapt
- **Messaging** — Klaviyo, Slack, Mailchimp, Instantly, Omnisend
- **Files** — Google Drive

## Open Integrations

Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar. Use the **Search integrations** box or the filters to find any platform:

- **All** — every platform.
- **Connected** — only what this workspace has already connected, including the Claude, ChatGPT and Grok connectors. Handy to check what's connected or to disconnect something. If nothing is connected yet, it shows *No integrations connected yet*.
- **AI**, **Analytics**, **Commerce & CMS**, **CRM**, **Advertising**, **Messaging**, **Files** — the categories listed above.

## Connect a platform

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Find the platform's card and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or enter credentials">
    For Google, Meta, and other sign-in platforms, you're redirected to that platform to sign in and approve access. For platforms that use an API key or token (like Framer or Duda), you paste your credentials and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish setup if asked">
    Some platforms need one more choice after connecting — for example, the **Site** for Search Console or the **Property** for Google Analytics. The window opens on its own; pick the option and click **Save**. Until then the card shows **Require setup**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card then shows a **Connected** badge. In Ryze, the agent can use the new connection from your next message — there's no need to start a new chat.

## Connect from Claude or ChatGPT with a link

You can also ask Ryze in Claude or ChatGPT to connect a platform. The agent sends you a one-time link:

1. Open the link. Sign in to the platform, or paste its API key on the page. Where the page shows **Where do I find these?**, it opens that platform's setup guide.
2. If asked, pick the accounts Ryze should use.
3. When the page says the platform is connected (for example, *Google Ads is connected*), close the tab and go back to your chat.

In Claude or ChatGPT, start a new chat to use the new platform. If its tools still don't appear, refresh the Ryze connector — see the [Claude FAQ](/claude/faq) or [Connect Ryze to ChatGPT](/chatgpt/connect#connected-a-new-platform-after-installing).

A link works once and for 24 hours. If the page says *This connection link has expired or was already used*, ask the agent for a new one.

Some platforms can only be connected in the Ryze app — including Ryze Hosting, GitHub, Custom MCP Server, and the Claude, ChatGPT and Grok connectors.

## Manage a connected platform

Click **View** on a connected card to:

- change the site, property, or other selection, then click **Save**;
- choose **Agent default accounts** — the accounts the agent uses first on platforms like Google Ads and Meta Ads;
- connect another login with **Add one more account**, where the platform supports it — see [Connect multiple accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts);
- disconnect the account with the trash icon.

## Reconnect a platform

If a card shows **Reconnect needed** and has a **Reconnect** button, click it and sign in again.

If the platform still has another working account, the card shows **View** instead. Click **View** — it opens on the broken account — and click **Reconnect** there.

## Don't see your platform?

Search for it first. If nothing matches, click **Request an integration** and tell us what you need.

If you want to publish articles to a site Ryze doesn't connect to yet, you still have options:

- **Ryze Hosting** — Ryze hosts your blog on a free Ryze subdomain right away, or on your own subdomain such as `blog.yoursite.com` after you add one DNS record. No CMS needed. See [Ryze Hosting](/integrations/ryze-hosting).
- **Custom-coded site** (Next.js, Vercel, Netlify, Hugo and similar) — publish articles as files committed to your repository with [GitHub](/integrations/github), or have your developer receive each article through a [Custom Webhook](/integrations/custom-webhook).
- **Someone else builds your site** — invite them to your workspace (**Workspace Settings → Team** — see [Invite teammates](/workspaces/invite-teammates)) or send them this page.
- **Weebly, Square Online, Pixieset, Google Sites and similar** — there's no publishing connection for these yet. Use Ryze Hosting for the blog and link to it from your site's menu.
