Connect your platforms in Ryze so the agent can read your data and act on it — in Ryze, and in Claude or ChatGPT if you've connected them. Everything happens on the **Integrations** page, not in Claude.

Ryze supports **50+ integrations**. They're grouped into seven categories, matching the filters on the Integrations page:

- **AI** — Anthropic, Claude MCP, ChatGPT MCP, Grok Bot, Custom MCP Server
- **Analytics** — Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Ahrefs, Semrush, Mixpanel, Amplitude, PostHog, CallRail, Microsoft Clarity
- **Commerce & CMS** — Shopify, WordPress, Framer, GitHub, HubSpot, Wix, Ryze Hosting, Ghost, Lightspeed eCom, Webflow, Magento, HighLevel, Directus, Custom Webhook, WooCommerce, Duda, Squarespace, Lovable, GoDaddy Airo, Base44, Replit, BigCommerce, Contentful, Sanity
- **CRM** — Zoho CRM, ClickUp
- **Advertising** — Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, OpenAI Ads, Thrad, Microsoft Ads, Snapchat Ads, TikTok Ads, Reddit Ads, Pinterest Ads, Criteo, StackAdapt
- **Messaging** — Klaviyo, Slack, Mailchimp, Instantly, Omnisend
- **Files** — Google Drive

## Open Integrations

Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar. Use the **Search integrations** box or the category filters to find any platform.

## Connect a platform

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Find the platform's card and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or enter credentials">
    For Google, Meta, and other sign-in platforms, you're redirected to that platform to sign in and approve access. For platforms that use an API key or token (like Framer or Duda), you paste your credentials and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish setup if asked">
    Some platforms need one more choice after connecting — for example, the **Site** for Search Console or the **Property** for Google Analytics. The window opens on its own; pick the option and click **Save**. Until then the card shows **Require setup**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card then shows a **Connected** badge.

<Tip>
After connecting a new platform, start a **new chat** so the agent picks up the change.
</Tip>

## Manage a connected platform

Click **View** on a connected card to:

- change the site, property, or other selection, then click **Save**;
- choose **Agent default accounts** — the accounts the agent uses first on platforms like Google Ads and Meta Ads;
- connect another login with **Add one more account**, where the platform supports it — see [Connect multiple accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts);
- disconnect the account with the trash icon.

## Reconnect a platform

If a card shows **Reconnect needed**, click **Reconnect** and sign in again. Then start a new chat.
