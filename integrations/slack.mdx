Slack connects with **OAuth** — you add the Ryze app to your Slack workspace and approve its permissions. After that, you talk to the agent right in Slack: mention it in a channel or send it a direct message, and it answers in a thread with the same tools and data it has in Ryze.

## Connect in Ryze

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar and find the **Slack** card (under **Messaging**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect and pick the Slack workspace">
    You're sent to Slack. Pick the Slack workspace to install Ryze into and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card shows **Connected**. Click **View** to see the Slack workspace name. One Slack workspace is tied to one Ryze workspace, so the agent answers from that workspace's connected accounts.

## Using the agent in Slack

- **Mention it in a channel** — `@Ryze AI how did Meta spend compare to last week?` The agent replies in a thread under your message; keep asking in that thread and it keeps the context.
- **Direct message it** — open a DM with Ryze and ask there; every DM is handled, no mention needed.
- **Attach images** — drop up to three screenshots (JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB each) into your message and the agent reads them. Other file types, including PDFs, aren't read yet — paste the text into the message instead.

<Note>
Invite the Ryze app to a channel before mentioning it there. For private channels, the app has to be added by a member.
</Note>

## Choose where notifications go

Results of [scheduled tasks](/agent/schedules) and other agent notifications can be posted to Slack channels.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Slack connection">
    Click **View** on the Slack card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the channels">
    Under **Default notification channels**, search for the channels, tick them, and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Scheduled tasks post their results to these channels unless a task has its own **Slack channels** set. The same channels are pre-filled when you choose who hears about new approvals — see [Get notified](/agent/approvals#get-notified).

<Note>
If no channel is selected, the field reads **All channels**, but nothing is posted to Slack by default. Pick at least one channel to get results there. Invite the Ryze app to a channel first — private channels appear in the list only after the app is added.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

- **No reply to a channel message** — you didn't mention `@Ryze AI`, or the app isn't in that channel yet. Mention it, or DM it.
- **"Not in channel"** — invite the **Ryze AI** app to the channel (`/invite @Ryze AI`) and try again.
- **Reconnect needed** — the Slack app was removed or the install was revoked. Click **Reconnect** on the card.
