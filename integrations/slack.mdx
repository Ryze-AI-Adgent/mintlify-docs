title: "Connect Slack"
description: "Add Ryze to your Slack workspace and ask the agent about your marketing — ads, spend, traffic, revenue — without leaving Slack."
---

Slack connects with **OAuth** — you add the Ryze app to your Slack workspace and approve its permissions. After that, you talk to the agent right in Slack: mention it in a channel or send it a direct message, and it answers in a thread with the same tools and data it has in Ryze.

## Connect in Ryze

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Workspace Settings → Integrations** and find the **Slack** card (under **Messaging**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect and pick the Slack workspace">
    You're sent to Slack. Pick the Slack workspace to install Ryze into and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card shows **Connected** with the Slack workspace name. One Slack workspace is tied to one Ryze workspace, so the agent answers from that workspace's connected accounts.

## Using the agent in Slack

- **Mention it in a channel** — `@Ryze AI how did Meta spend compare to last week?` The agent replies in a thread under your message; keep asking in that thread and it keeps the context.
- **Direct message it** — open a DM with Ryze and ask there; every DM is handled, no mention needed.
- **Attach images and PDFs** — drop a screenshot or a PDF into your message and the agent reads it.

<Note>
Invite the Ryze app to a channel before mentioning it there. For private channels, the app has to be added by a member.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

- **No reply to a channel message** — you didn't mention `@Ryze AI`, or the app isn't in that channel yet. Mention it, or DM it.
- **"Not in channel"** — invite the **Ryze AI** app to the channel (`/invite @Ryze AI`) and try again.
- **Reconnect needed** — the Slack app was removed or the install was revoked. Click **Reconnect** on the card.
