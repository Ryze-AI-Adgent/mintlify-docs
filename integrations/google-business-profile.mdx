Google Business Profile is your business listing on Google Search and Maps. Connect it to use [Local SEO](/seo/local-seo): map rankings around your office, reviews and replies, posts, and your listing's categories, description, hours, photos, website and phone.

## Requirements

- A Google account that owns or manages the listing in Google Business Profile
- A **verified** listing. For an unverified one Google shares only part of the data: no map coordinates, and reviews may not load.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar. Use the **Search integrations** box or the **Analytics** filter to find **Google Business Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You're redirected to Google. Sign in with the account that manages your listing and allow access. The Google screen may show the name of our connection partner rather than Ryze — that's expected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Location">
    Back in Ryze, the Google Business Profile window opens on its own. Pick the **Location** this workspace works on and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connection is one location, and Local SEO in a workspace works on that location. Until a Location is saved, the card shows **Require setup** — click **View** to finish. To work on another location, connect it in its own workspace.

## Change or remove the location

Click **View** on the card to see the connected **Location**. To switch to a different business, disconnect it and connect again, choosing the right **Location**. If your location isn't in the list, you signed in with a Google account that doesn't manage it — reconnect with the one that does.

If the Local page says **Google has no coordinates for this location yet. Verify the listing on Google and reconnect it.**, finish verification in Google Business Profile, then reconnect here.

## What Ryze uses it for

- **Map rankings** — your position on Google Maps from 49 spots around your office, and the businesses that beat you
- **Reviews** — every review, which ones need a reply, and posting replies to Google
- **Posts** — the posts on your profile and new ones written with AI
- **Business profile** — editing categories, description, hours, photos, website and phone
- **Weekly proposals** — the agent compares your listing with the map leaders and proposes fixes for your approval
- **AI Marketer chat** — ask about reviews, calls, direction requests, website clicks and the searches people find you by

Changes to your public listing — replies, posts, photos, edits — follow your chat's [**Tool approvals**](/agent/chat#tool-approvals) setting when the agent makes them, and nothing from the weekly proposals is applied without your approval.

## Data timing

Google reports profile performance (views, calls, direction requests, website clicks) with a **2–3 day delay**. New posts can take 24–48 hours to appear on Google, and Google reviews new photos and listing edits before they show.

See [Local SEO](/seo/local-seo) for how to use everything above.
