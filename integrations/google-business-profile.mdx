Google Business Profile is your business listing on Google Search and Maps. Connect it to use [Local SEO](/seo/local-seo): map rankings around your office, reviews and replies, posts, and your listing's categories, description, hours, photos, website and phone.

## Requirements

- A Google account that owns or manages the listing in Google Business Profile
- A **verified** listing. For an unverified one Google shares only part of the data: no map coordinates, and no profile performance (views, calls, direction requests, website clicks and the searches people find you by). Reconnecting doesn't change this — verify the listing on Google.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar. Use the **Search integrations** box or the **Analytics** filter to find **Google Business Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You're redirected to Google. Sign in with the account that manages your listing and allow access. The Google screen may show the name of our connection partner rather than Ryze — that's expected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Location">
    Back in Ryze, the Google Business Profile window opens on its own. Pick the **Location** this workspace works on and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until a Location is saved, the card shows **Require setup** — click **View** to finish.

You can connect several locations in one workspace: click **View** on the card → **Add one more account**, sign in, pick another **Location** and click **Save**. On the Local page, switch between them with the **Location** selector — keywords and map scans are kept per location.

## Change or remove the location

Click **View** on the card to see the connected locations. To add another one, use **Add one more account**. To remove one, open it in the window and disconnect it with the trash icon. If your location isn't in the list, you signed in with a Google account that doesn't manage it — reconnect with the one that does.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Google did not grant Ryze access to your Business Profile…"** — connect again and keep the **Manage your Business Profile** box ticked on Google's consent screen.
- **"Google found no Business Profile managed by the Google account you signed in with…"** — connect again with the Google account that owns or manages the business at business.google.com, or create the profile there first.
- **"This location is not on Google Maps yet, so its rankings cannot be scanned…"** — finish verifying the listing on Google, or pick a location in the **Location** selector that already shows on Maps.
- **"The connected Google account does not manage this location…"** — reconnect with an account that manages it, or ask the owner to add that account to the profile.
- **"Google no longer has this location…"** — reconnect Google Business Profile and pick a current location.
- **"This location is no longer connected to the workspace. Pick another one."** — choose another location in the **Location** selector, or connect this one again.

## What Ryze uses it for

- **Map rankings** — your position on Google Maps from 49 spots around your office, and the businesses that beat you
- **Reviews** — every review, which ones need a reply, and posting replies to Google
- **Posts** — the posts on your profile and new ones written with AI
- **Business profile** — editing categories, description, hours, photos, website and phone
- **Weekly proposals** — the agent compares your listing with the map leaders and proposes fixes for your approval
- **AI Marketer chat** — ask about reviews, calls, direction requests, website clicks and the searches people find you by

Changes to your public listing — replies, posts, photos, edits — follow your chat's [**Tool approvals**](/agent/chat#tool-approvals) setting when the agent makes them, and nothing from the weekly proposals is applied without your approval.

## Data timing

Google reports profile performance (views, calls, direction requests, website clicks) with a **2–3 day delay**. New posts can take 24–48 hours to appear on Google, and Google reviews new photos and listing edits before they show.
