LinkedIn Ads, Microsoft Ads, TikTok Ads, Snapchat Ads, Reddit Ads, and Pinterest Ads all connect the same way: you sign in to the platform and approve access — no keys to copy. Once connected, you can ask the agent about campaign performance and spend in plain language, in Ryze or through Claude and ChatGPT.

For Google Ads and Meta Ads, see [Connect Google Ads](/integrations/google-ads) and [Connect Meta Ads](/integrations/meta-ads). Criteo and StackAdapt have their own pages: [Criteo](/integrations/criteo), [StackAdapt](/integrations/stackadapt).

## Connect a platform

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar and find the platform's card — use the **Advertising** filter or the search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You're sent to the platform to sign in. Use the login that has access to the ad accounts you want Ryze to work with, and approve every permission it asks for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the card">
    Back in Ryze, the card shows **Connected**. There's no ad account to pick: Ryze sees every ad account that login can reach.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
In Ryze, the agent can use the new platform from your next message. In Claude or ChatGPT, start a **new chat**. If the new platform's tools still don't appear there, refresh the Ryze connector — see the [Claude FAQ](/claude/faq) or [Connect Ryze to ChatGPT](/chatgpt/connect#connected-a-new-platform-after-installing).
</Tip>

## Default accounts and more logins

Click **View** on the card to:

- choose **Agent default accounts** — the ad accounts the agent works with first when you don't name one. It can still reach all the others. See [Agent default accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts#agent-default-accounts);
- connect another login with **Add one more account**. With several logins, the card shows how many accounts are connected, and the star marks the primary one. See [Connect multiple accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts).

## Troubleshooting (all platforms)

- **Card shows Reconnect needed** — the platform expired or revoked access (password change, permission change, or a teammate reconnecting). Click **Reconnect** and sign in again.
- **An ad account is missing** — the login you used doesn't have access to it on the platform. Ask the account owner to grant access, or connect a login that has it with **Add one more account**.

Still stuck? Email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai).

## Microsoft Ads

Campaign performance, spend, and keyword insights from Microsoft Advertising.

When you click **Connect**, Ryze first asks how you sign in to Microsoft Advertising:

- **Microsoft account** — if you sign in to Microsoft Advertising with a Microsoft account;
- **Google account** — if you sign in to Microsoft Advertising with Google.

Pick the one you actually use and click **Continue**. The same choice appears when you open a connect link a teammate sent you.

If the connection fails, Ryze tells you why:

- *"Microsoft Advertising knows this email under a different sign-in type than the one you just used…"* — connect again and pick how you normally sign in at ads.microsoft.com: **Google account** if you sign in with Google, **Microsoft account** if you use a Microsoft, work or school account. Then choose that same account on the sign-in screen.
- *"Microsoft Advertising has no active user for the sign-in used for this connection…"* — connect again with the sign-in type and the exact email that open your ad account at ads.microsoft.com. If that email has never used Microsoft Advertising, sign up at ads.microsoft.com first, then connect again.
- *"Your organization's Microsoft 365 settings block Ryze from connecting to Microsoft Ads…"* — you signed in with a work account, and your company's Microsoft 365 doesn't let outside apps in yet. Either sign in with the personal Microsoft account that owns your Microsoft Advertising login, or ask your Microsoft 365 admin to approve Ryze at the link shown in the message, then click **Connect** again.

  If the admin approval link doesn't help and the error mentions **AADSTS650052** ("your organization lacks a service principal for Microsoft Advertising API Service"), your company's Microsoft Entra ID hasn't registered the Microsoft Advertising API yet. An admin (Global Administrator or Cloud Application Administrator) adds it once, for example in Azure Cloud Shell: `az ad sp create --id d42ffc93-c136-491d-b4fd-6f18168c68fd`, or in PowerShell: `New-MgServicePrincipal -AppId d42ffc93-c136-491d-b4fd-6f18168c68fd`. Wait a few minutes, then click **Connect** again.

## Snapchat Ads

Campaign performance, spend, and audience insights. Follow the [common steps](#connect-a-platform).

If you see *"This Snapchat login is not a member of any Ads Manager organization"*, accept the organization invite from Snapchat's welcome email, or add this login in Snap Business Manager, then connect again. You can also sign in with a login that is already a member.

## Reddit Ads

Campaign performance, spend, and campaign management. Follow the [common steps](#connect-a-platform) and sign in with the Reddit login that has access to your ad accounts.

If the agent reports *"Reddit rejected the refresh token as revoked or expired"*, Reddit ended Ryze's access. Click **Reconnect** on the Reddit Ads card and sign in again.
