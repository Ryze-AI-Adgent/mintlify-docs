Google Search Console supplies the queries and pages you already appear for. It is the most important connection for Ryze SEO — without it, topic selection is materially weaker.

## Requirements

- A verified Search Console property for your site
- At least **Full** user access on that property
- The property type matching how your site is served — a Domain property covers all subdomains and protocols; a URL-prefix property covers only that exact prefix

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Workspace Settings → Integrations**. Use the search box or the **Analytics** filter to find Search Console.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You are redirected to Google to sign in and approve access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the property">
    Choose the property for the site this workspace covers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Start a **new chat** in Claude or ChatGPT afterwards so the assistant picks up the connection.

## What Ryze uses it for

- Keyword discovery from queries you already receive impressions for
- Finding pages ranking just outside click-earning positions
- Pool B topic mining from impression data
- Indexation monitoring
- Measuring whether published work earned impressions and clicks

## Data timing

Google reports with a **2–3 day delay** and provides up to **16 months** of history. Recently published pages will not appear immediately.

## Troubleshooting

**No properties listed.** The Google account you authorized has no property access. Sign in with the account that does.

**Data looks incomplete.** Check the property type — a URL-prefix property for `https://www.example.com/` excludes `https://example.com/` traffic.

**Connection drops.** Click **Reconnect** and re-authorize. Google tokens can be revoked by password changes or admin policy.
