Google Search Console supplies the queries and pages you already appear for. Connect it before you start SEO: without it, Ryze can't pick keywords from your own search data or show whether Google indexed and ranked the articles it publishes.

## Requirements

- A verified Search Console property for your site
- A Google login with access to that property
- The property type matching how your site is served — a Domain property covers all subdomains and protocols; a URL-prefix property covers only that exact prefix

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar. Use the **Search integrations** box or the **Analytics** filter to find **Google Search Console**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You're redirected to Google. Sign in and approve access — keep every requested permission checked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Site">
    Back in Ryze, the Google Search Console window opens on its own. Ryze pre-selects the **Site** that matches your workspace domain. Check it, change it if needed, and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until a Site is saved, the card shows **Require setup**. Click **View** to finish.

## Change the Site or default accounts later

Click **View** on the card. There you can:

- change the **Site** for this workspace;
- pick **Agent default accounts** — the sites the agent looks at first when you don't name one. It can still reach every site on that login. See [Agent default accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts#agent-default-accounts);
- add another Google login with **Add one more account**.

## What Ryze uses it for

- Keyword discovery from queries you already receive impressions for
- Finding pages ranking just outside click-earning positions
- Finding new article topics in your impression data
- Indexation monitoring
- Measuring whether published work earned impressions and clicks

## Data timing

Google reports with a **2–3 day delay** and provides up to **16 months** of history. Recently published pages will not appear immediately.

## Troubleshooting

**No sites to pick.** The Google login you used has no access to a verified property. Connect with the login that does, or add it with **Add one more account**.

**Data looks incomplete.** Check the property type — a URL-prefix property for `https://www.example.com/` excludes `https://example.com/` traffic.

**Card shows Reconnect needed.** Click **Reconnect** and sign in to Google again. Google access can be revoked by a password change or an admin policy.
