Google Search Console supplies the queries and pages you already appear for. We recommend connecting it before you start SEO — it's optional, and you can skip it during setup and connect it later in **Integrations**. Without it, Ryze can't pick keywords from your own search data or show whether Google indexed and ranked the articles it publishes.

## Requirements

- A verified Search Console property for your site
- A Google login with access to that property — it doesn't have to be the same email you use for Ryze. To let Ryze also resubmit your sitemap, that login needs **Owner** or **Full** permission on the property; a Restricted user can connect, but sitemap submission fails.
- The property type matching how your site is served — a Domain property covers all subdomains and protocols; a URL-prefix property covers only that exact prefix

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar. Use the **Search integrations** box or the **Analytics** filter to find **Google Search Console**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You're redirected to Google. Sign in and approve access — keep every requested permission checked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Site">
    Back in Ryze, the Google Search Console window opens on its own. Ryze pre-selects the **Site** that matches your workspace domain. Check it, change it if needed, and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until a Site is saved, the card shows **Require setup**. Click **View** to finish.

## Change the Site or default accounts later

Click **View** on the card. There you can:

- change the **Site** for this workspace;
- pick **Agent default accounts** — the sites the agent looks at first when you don't name one. It can still reach every site on that login. See [Agent default accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts#agent-default-accounts);
- add another Google login with **Add one more account**.

## What Ryze uses it for

- Keyword discovery from queries you already receive impressions for
- Finding pages ranking just outside click-earning positions
- Finding new article topics in your impression data
- Indexation monitoring
- Measuring whether published work earned impressions and clicks
- Resubmitting the sitemap that lists each newly published article, automatically after it goes live — pages with `noindex` or a 404 are skipped. See [Programmatic SEO](/seo/programmatic)
- Checking a page's index status when you ask (URL Inspection)
- Submitting or removing sitemaps in Search Console when you ask (removing one doesn't touch the file on your site) — these changes follow your chat's [**Tool approvals**](/agent/chat#tool-approvals) setting

## Data timing

Google reports with a **2–3 day delay** and provides up to **16 months** of history. Recently published pages will not appear immediately.

## Troubleshooting

**No sites to pick.** The window shows **"This Google account has no verified site in Search Console yet."** The Google login you used has no access to a verified property. Connect with the login that does, or add it with **Add one more account**. If the site isn't in Search Console yet, use the **Add your site in Search Console** link, add and verify the site there, then click **View** on the card again. You can also skip this step and connect it later.

**Your site is missing from the list, or Ryze shows no Search Console data for it.** The property is verified under a different Google login than the one you connected. Ryze lists only properties the connected login has verified access to. In Search Console, open the property → **Settings → Users and permissions** and add the connected Google login with **Full** or **Owner** permission, then click **View** on the card again. Or connect the Google login that verified the site with **Add one more account**.

**Data looks incomplete.** Check the property type — a URL-prefix property for `https://www.example.com/` excludes `https://example.com/` traffic.

**Card shows Reconnect needed.** Click **Reconnect** and sign in to Google again. Google access can be revoked by a password change or an admin policy.
