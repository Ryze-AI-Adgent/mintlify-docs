---
title: "Connect Lightspeed eCom"
description: "Connect your Lightspeed eCom store with an API key and secret so Ryze can publish blog articles straight to your store."
---

Lightspeed eCom connects with an **API key and secret** you create in your store's back office. Once connected, programmatic articles publish straight to the blog you pick on your Lightspeed store.

## Step 1 — Create an API key in Lightspeed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Developers">
    In your store's **Back office**, go to **Settings → Developers → API keys**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a key">
    Create a new key with **Blogs — read and write** access, and copy both the **API key** and the **API secret**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note your shop language code">
    It's the language segment in your store URL — for example `en`, `us`, or `nl`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2 — Connect in Ryze

In **Workspace Settings → Integrations**, click **Connect** on the **Lightspeed eCom** card and fill in:

- **API key** and **API secret** — from the key you just created
- **Shop language code** — e.g. `en`

Click **Continue**. Ryze detects your store's cluster automatically and verifies the credentials.

## Step 3 — Choose the blog

Pick the **Blog** articles should publish to. The card then shows the blog, cluster, and language.

## Troubleshooting

- **Credentials rejected** — check the key has **Blogs read/write** and that the secret belongs to the same key.
- **No blogs listed** — create a blog in Lightspeed first (Back office → Content → Blogs), then reconnect.
- **Wrong language** — the language code must match the segment in your storefront URL; reconnect with the right one.
