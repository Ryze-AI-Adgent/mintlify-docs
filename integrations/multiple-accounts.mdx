title: "Connect Multiple Accounts"
description: "Connect several accounts for the same platform, choose which one the agent uses by default, and decide when to use separate workspaces."
---

You can connect more than one account for the same platform in a single workspace — for example, two Google Ads logins or several GA4 accounts. Each account is kept separately, with its own property or ad-account selection, and you choose which one is **primary**.

## Which platforms support several accounts

You can connect several accounts to: Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Microsoft Ads, TikTok Ads, Snapchat Ads, Reddit Ads, Pinterest Ads, OpenAI Ads, Thrad, Shopify, WordPress, HubSpot, HighLevel, Zoho CRM, AppsFlyer and Custom MCP Server.

Every other platform holds one connection per workspace. To switch to another account there, click **View** on the card, disconnect the current account with the trash icon, and connect again.

## What is the primary account?

When a platform has more than one connected account, one of them is the **primary**. The primary is the account the agent — in Ryze, Claude or ChatGPT — uses **by default** when you don't name a specific one. You can change it at any time, and you can still ask the agent to use any of the other accounts (see [Use a specific account](#use-a-specific-account)).

A platform with a single connected account doesn't need a primary — that account is always used.

## Add an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the platform">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar and click **View** on the connected platform's card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add one more account">
    Click **Add one more account** and authorize the other login (OAuth) or enter its credentials (API-key platforms). The new account is added alongside the existing ones — nothing is overwritten.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick its property">
    Choose the property or ad account for the new connection, then click **Save**. Each account keeps its own selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **Back** and **Next** to page through the connected accounts (for example, *1 of 2*).

## Set the primary account

Next to the account field is a **star**:

- A **filled** star marks the current primary.
- An **empty** star means that account isn't primary — click it to make it the primary.

Setting a new primary only changes the agent's default; it doesn't disconnect anything.

## Agent default accounts

For platforms where you manage many accounts — an agency running dozens of clients through one Google Search Console or Google Ads login — you can pick which accounts the **Ryze agent** works with by default. Open the platform's dialog and use **Agent default accounts** to select them.

**Agent default accounts** is available for Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Microsoft Ads, TikTok Ads, Snapchat Ads, Reddit Ads and Pinterest Ads. Slack has **Default notification channels** instead — the channels where agent results and notifications go.

- When you don't name an account, the agent uses your defaults instead of guessing across everything it can see.
- All other accounts stay reachable — just name one in chat and the agent switches to it.
- Selecting all accounts (or none) means no filter: the agent considers everything.

This applies everywhere the agent works: platform chat, scheduled tasks, and MCP.

## Use a specific account

By default the agent uses the **primary** account. To work with another one, just ask — in Ryze, Claude or ChatGPT, no setup required:

> **You:** Show my connected Google Analytics accounts.

> **You:** Pull last week's spend for my other Google Ads account.

The agent lists the connected accounts and uses the one you point to. It sees a new account or a changed primary from your next message.

## One workspace or many?

The rule of thumb: **one workspace = one business.**

- Several accounts that belong to the **same business or brand** — multiple Google Ads logins, regional GA4 properties, a second Meta ad account — connect them all to **one workspace**. Chats, reports, scheduled tasks and the agent in Claude or ChatGPT can then work across all of them.
- **Different clients or separate businesses** — give each its **own workspace**. Every workspace keeps its own connections, team, settings and data, so client accounts never mix, and you switch between workspaces in seconds. See [Create a Workspace](/workspaces/create-workspace).
