title: "Connect Multiple Accounts"
description: "Connect several accounts for the same platform and choose which one Claude uses by default."
---

You can connect more than one account for the same platform in a single workspace — for example, two Google Ads logins or several GA4 accounts. Each account is kept separately, with its own property or ad-account selection, and you choose which one is **primary**.

## What is the primary account?

When a platform has more than one connected account, one of them is the **primary**. The primary is the account Claude uses **by default** when you don't name a specific one. You can change it at any time, and you can still ask Claude to use any of the other accounts (see [Using a specific account in Claude](#using-a-specific-account-in-claude)).

A platform with a single connected account doesn't need a primary — that account is always used.

## Add an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the platform">
    Go to **Workspace Settings → Integrations** and click **View** on the connected platform's card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add account">
    Click **+ Add account** and authorize the other login (OAuth) or enter its credentials (API-key platforms). The new account is added alongside the existing ones — nothing is overwritten.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick its property">
    Choose the property or ad account for the new connection, then click **Save**. Each account keeps its own selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **Back** and **Next** to page through the connected accounts (for example, *1 of 2*).

## Set the primary account

Next to the account field is a **star**:

- A **filled** star marks the current primary.
- An **empty** star means that account isn't primary — click it to make it the primary.

Setting a new primary only changes Claude's default; it doesn't disconnect anything.

## Using a specific account in Claude

By default Claude uses the **primary** account. To work with another one, just ask — no setup required:

> **You:** Claude, show my connected Google Analytics accounts.

> **You:** Pull last week's spend for my other Google Ads account.

Claude lists the connected accounts and uses the one you point to.

<Tip>
After adding an account or changing the primary, start a **new chat** in Claude so it picks up the change.
</Tip>
