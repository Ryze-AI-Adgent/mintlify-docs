Connecting Meta gives Ryze access to your Facebook and Instagram campaigns, and enables Meta ad library research for competitor intelligence.

## Requirements

- A Meta Business account with an ad account
- At least **Advertiser** access on the ad account; **Admin** to allow changes
- Access granted through Meta Business Manager rather than a personal profile, where possible

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    **Workspace Settings → Integrations**, then Meta under **Advertising**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Sign in to Meta and approve access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the ad account">
    Choose the ad account for this workspace. Business Manager users will see every account they have access to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Start a **new chat** afterwards.

## What you can ask

> How did my Meta campaigns perform last week compared to the week before?

> Which creatives have the best ROAS this month?

> Show me what competitors are running in the Meta ad library.

## Competitor ad research

Meta's ad library is public, and Ryze uses it to surface what competitors are currently running — creative formats, messaging, and how long ads have been live. Longevity is the useful signal: ads that have run for months are usually working.

See [Competitor ad library](/ads/competitor-ad-library).

## What the agent can do

With approval: campaign creation and adjustment, budget management, and [AI creative generation](/ads/creative-generation). Changes require sign-off by default — see [Permissions & approvals](/claude/permissions).

## Troubleshooting

**No ad accounts listed.** The authorized Meta user lacks access. Check assignments in Business Manager.

**Connection drops repeatedly.** Meta tokens expire more aggressively than Google's, particularly where two-factor policies or Business Manager role changes are involved. Reconnect from the card.

**Attribution disagrees with GA4 or Shopify.** Expected. Meta uses its own attribution windows and modelling. Do not attempt exact reconciliation — track each consistently.

**Missing conversion data.** Usually a Pixel or Conversions API configuration issue on the Meta side.
