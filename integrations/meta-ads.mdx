Connecting Meta Ads gives Ryze access to your Facebook and Instagram campaigns — performance, spend, and audiences.

## Requirements

- A Meta Business account with an ad account
- At least **Advertiser** access on the ad account; **Admin** to allow changes
- Access granted through Meta Business Manager rather than a personal profile, where possible

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar and find **Meta Ads** under **Advertising**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You're redirected to Meta. Sign in and approve access — keep every requested permission checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

There's no ad account to pick. Ryze sees every ad account that login can reach, and the card shows **Connected**. The agent can use it from your next message.

## Choose the accounts the agent uses by default

Click **View** on the card and use **Agent default accounts** to select the ad accounts the agent should work with first. It can still reach all the others — just name one in chat. See [Agent default accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts#agent-default-accounts).

To connect another Meta login, click **View** → **Add one more account**.

## What you can ask

> How did my Meta campaigns perform last week compared to the week before?

> Which creatives have the best ROAS this month?

> Which ad sets are spending without conversions?

## What the agent can do

Campaign creation and adjustment, budget management, and [AI ad creatives](/paid-ads/ai-creatives).

The agent works with ads. It can't publish organic posts to your Facebook Page or Instagram, and it can't read comments on your Page — ask it to turn a post into an ad, or to write the post for you to paste.

The agent can also see the lead forms (Instant Forms) on your Facebook Page. If it reports a permissions error, reconnect Meta under **Integrations** so Ryze gets the lead access it needs. To download the leads themselves, use Meta (Ads Manager or Meta Business Suite).

By default the agent runs its tools on its own: the [**Tool approvals**](/agent/chat#tool-approvals) setting in the chat composer starts on **Skip all approvals**. To review changes before they happen, switch it to **Approve write & delete** (reads run freely, changes need approval) or **Manual approve** (every tool that touches your data needs approval). Scheduled tasks run without an approval step.

Improvements the agent finds on its own go to your [Inbox](/agent/approvals) instead: nothing there changes until you approve. In Claude, approvals are set in Claude itself — set write and delete tools to **Needs approval** to be asked first (see [Permissions & approvals](/claude/permissions)).

To see what competitors are running on Meta, use [Competitor Ads](/paid-ads/competitors).

## Troubleshooting

**An ad account is missing.** The connected Meta user lacks access to it. Check assignments in Business Manager, or connect the right login with **Add one more account**.

**Card shows Reconnect needed.** Meta access expires more often than Google's, particularly after two-factor or Business Manager role changes. Click **Reconnect** and sign in again.

**Attribution disagrees with GA4 or Shopify.** Expected. Meta uses its own attribution windows and modelling. Track each consistently rather than reconciling exactly. To compare with Ads Manager, ask the agent to report with the same attribution window you use there — for example "use 7-day click and 1-day view". Supported windows: 1-day click, 7-day click, 28-day click and 1-day view; a window Meta no longer supports is left out of the answer rather than shown as zero.

**Missing conversion data.** Usually a Pixel or Conversions API configuration issue on the Meta side.
