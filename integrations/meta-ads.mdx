Connecting Meta Ads gives Ryze access to your Facebook and Instagram campaigns — performance, spend, and audiences.

## Requirements

- A Meta Business account with an ad account
- At least **Advertiser** access on the ad account; **Admin** to allow changes
- Access granted through Meta Business Manager rather than a personal profile, where possible

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar and find **Meta Ads** under **Advertising**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You're redirected to Meta. Sign in and approve access — keep every requested permission checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

There's no ad account to pick. Ryze sees every ad account that login can reach, and the card shows **Connected**. Start a **new chat** so the agent picks up the connection.

## Choose the accounts the agent uses by default

Click **View** on the card and use **Agent default accounts** to select the ad accounts the agent should work with first. It can still reach all the others — just name one in chat. See [Agent default accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts#agent-default-accounts).

To connect another Meta login, click **View** → **Add one more account**.

## What you can ask

> How did my Meta campaigns perform last week compared to the week before?

> Which creatives have the best ROAS this month?

> Which ad sets are spending without conversions?

## What the agent can do

With approval: campaign creation and adjustment, budget management, and [AI ad creatives](/paid-ads/ai-creatives). Changes require sign-off by default — see [Approvals](/agent/approvals) and [Permissions & approvals](/claude/permissions).

To see what competitors are running on Meta, use [Competitor Ads](/paid-ads/competitors).

## Troubleshooting

**An ad account is missing.** The connected Meta user lacks access to it. Check assignments in Business Manager, or connect the right login with **Add one more account**.

**Card shows Reconnect needed.** Meta access expires more often than Google's, particularly after two-factor or Business Manager role changes. Click **Reconnect** and sign in again.

**Attribution disagrees with GA4 or Shopify.** Expected. Meta uses its own attribution windows and modelling. Track each consistently rather than reconciling exactly.

**Missing conversion data.** Usually a Pixel or Conversions API configuration issue on the Meta side.
