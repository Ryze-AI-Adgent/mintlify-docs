Connecting Google Ads lets you ask about campaign performance in plain language and lets the agent act on campaigns under your approval settings.

## Requirements

- A Google login with access to at least one Google Ads account; standard or admin access to allow changes
- For agencies: connect the manager (MCC) login once — the agent can reach every client account under it; name the client in chat

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar and find **Google Ads** under **Advertising**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You're redirected to Google. Sign in with the login that has access to your ad accounts and approve access — keep every requested permission checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it — there's no account to pick. Ryze sees every Google Ads account that login can reach, and the card shows **Connected**. The agent can use it from your next message.

## Choose the accounts the agent uses by default

If the login reaches many ad accounts, click **View** on the card and use **Agent default accounts** to select the ones the agent should work with first. It can still reach all the others — just name one in chat. See [Agent default accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts#agent-default-accounts).

To connect a second Google login, click **View** → **Add one more account**.

## What you can ask

> Pull the last 7 days of Google Ads spend and break it down by campaign.

> Which campaigns have ROAS below 2 this month?

> What changed in my account in the last week?

## What the agent can do

The agent can create and adjust campaigns, manage budgets, and generate ad creatives.

By default the agent runs its tools on its own: the [**Tool approvals**](/agent/chat#tool-approvals) setting in the chat composer starts on **Skip all approvals**. To review changes before they happen, switch it to **Approve write & delete** (reads run freely, changes need approval) or **Manual approve** (every tool that touches your data needs approval). Scheduled tasks run without an approval step.

Improvements the agent finds on its own go to your [Inbox](/agent/approvals) instead: nothing there changes until you approve. In Claude, approvals are set in Claude itself — set write and delete tools to **Needs approval** to be asked first (see [Permissions & approvals](/claude/permissions)).

<Note>
Check your **Tool approvals** setting before you ask the agent to work on a live spending account. This is the connection where an unwanted change costs real money.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

**"This Google account has no access to a Google Ads account."** You signed in with a login that isn't on any Google Ads account. Connect again with the login that has access to your ad account.

**"This Google login has no accessible Google Ads accounts — reconnect Google Ads with a login that can access the ad account."** The connected login doesn't reach any Google Ads account. Click **View** → **Add one more account** and sign in with the login that has access, then remove the old one with the trash icon.

**An account is missing.** The connected login can't reach it, or it sits under a manager account you didn't sign in with. Connect the right login with **Add one more account**.

**Changes rejected.** The connected user likely has read-only access. Google Ads permissions are set in Google, not in Ryze.

**Data looks delayed.** Google Ads reporting lags for recent conversions, particularly view-through and cross-device.
