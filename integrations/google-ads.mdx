Connecting Google Ads lets you ask about campaign performance in plain language and lets the agent act on campaigns under your approval settings.

## Requirements

- A Google Ads account with at least read access; standard or admin access to allow changes
- For agencies, connect the manager (MCC) login once — the agent can reach every client account under it; name the client in chat

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    **Workspace Settings → Integrations**, then Google Ads under **Advertising**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Sign in to Google and approve access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the account">
    Choose the specific Google Ads account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Start a **new chat** afterwards.

## What you can ask

> Pull the last 7 days of Google Ads spend and break it down by campaign.

> Which campaigns have ROAS below 2 this month?

> What changed in my account in the last week?

## What the agent can do

With approval, the agent can create and adjust campaigns, manage budgets, and generate ad creatives. Actions that change your account require sign-off by default — configure this in [Permissions & approvals](/claude/permissions).

<Note>
Review approval settings before enabling unattended changes on a live spending account. This is the connection where a misconfiguration costs real money.
</Note>

## Multiple accounts

A workspace can hold several Google Ads accounts — useful for brands running separate accounts by region or business unit. See [Connect multiple accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts) and [Managing multiple accounts](/integrations/managing-multiple-accounts).

## Troubleshooting

**No accounts listed.** The authorized Google account has no Ads access, or the account sits under an MCC you did not authorize.

**Changes rejected.** The authorized user likely has read-only access. Google Ads permissions are set in Google, not in Ryze.

**Data looks delayed.** Google Ads reporting lags for recent conversions, particularly view-through and cross-device.
