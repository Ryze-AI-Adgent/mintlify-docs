---
title: "Connect Google Analytics"
description: "Connect a GA4 property to Ryze so it can attribute traffic, conversions, and revenue across SEO and paid ads."
---

Google Analytics supplies the behaviour and conversion side of reporting — what visitors did after arriving. Ryze uses it to tie SEO and ad work to revenue rather than to traffic alone. On the Integrations page it's called **Google Analytics 4**.

## Requirements

- A **GA4** property (Universal Analytics is no longer supported by Google)
- At least Viewer access on the property
- Ecommerce tracking configured, if you want revenue attribution

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** in the left sidebar and find **Google Analytics 4** under **Analytics**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    You're redirected to Google. Sign in and approve access — keep every requested permission checked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Property">
    Back in Ryze, the Google Analytics 4 window opens on its own. Pick the **Property** for this workspace (listed as *account — property*) and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until a Property is saved, the card shows **Require setup**. Click **View** to finish.

## Change the Property or default accounts later

Click **View** on the card to change the **Property**, pick **Agent default accounts** (the properties the agent looks at first when you don't name one — see [Agent default accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts#agent-default-accounts)), or connect another Google login with **Add one more account**.

## What Ryze uses it for

- Traffic by channel, so organic and paid can be separated
- Conversion and revenue attribution
- Landing page performance

## Multiple properties

A brand running several domains can connect more than one Google login and choose a property for each. See [Connect multiple accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts). For separate clients, use a separate workspace.

## Troubleshooting

**No properties to pick.** The Google login you used lacks access. Connect with a login that has it.

**Revenue shows as zero.** Ecommerce tracking is likely not configured in GA4, or purchase events are not firing. This is a GA4 setup issue, not a Ryze one.

**Numbers disagree with Shopify.** Expected. GA4 and Shopify attribute differently and use different session and conversion definitions. Use each consistently rather than trying to reconcile them exactly.

**Card shows Reconnect needed.** Click **Reconnect** and sign in to Google again.
