HubSpot connects with **OAuth** — you sign in to HubSpot and approve access, so Ryze never sees your password. Once connected, the agent can read and edit your CRM contacts, companies, and deals, read your contact lists and account owners, and manage CMS blog content — and delete records or blog posts when you ask.

## Step 1 — Connect in Ryze

On the **Integrations** page, find the HubSpot card and click **Connect**. You're redirected to HubSpot to authorize.

## Step 2 — Authorize in HubSpot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to HubSpot">
    Sign in to the HubSpot account you want to connect, or create a new one.
    <Frame><img src="/images/hubspot-oauth-account.png" alt="The HubSpot screen to sign in or create an account when connecting Ryze AI" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the account">
    If you have access to more than one HubSpot account (portal), pick the one you want Ryze to work with, then click **Choose Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve access">
    Review the requested permissions and approve. HubSpot sends you back to Ryze and the connection is saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3 — Confirm the connection

Back in Ryze, the HubSpot card shows a **Connected** badge. Click **View** on the card to see the account you linked — identified by its HubSpot domain (like `yourcompany.com`).

<Frame><img src="/images/hubspot-connected.png" alt="The connected HubSpot account shown in Ryze with an option to add another account" /></Frame>

## Connect multiple accounts

You can connect more than one HubSpot account (portal) in the same workspace. Click **View** on the HubSpot card, then **Add one more account**, and repeat the authorization. See [Connect multiple accounts](/integrations/multiple-accounts) for how the agent picks between them.

## What the agent can do

Once connected, the agent works with HubSpot through your account:

- **CRM records** — read and edit contacts, companies, and deals
- **Search** — filter and query records with the HubSpot CRM Search API
- **Lists** — read your contact lists
- **Owners** — look up account owners to attribute records
- **CMS** — read and edit blog content
- **Delete** — delete records or blog posts when you explicitly ask. Most CRM deletes move the record to HubSpot's recycling bin; deleted blog posts may not be recoverable.

By default the agent runs its tools on its own: the [**Tool approvals**](/agent/chat#tool-approvals) setting in the chat composer starts on **Skip all approvals**. To review changes before they happen, switch it to **Approve write & delete** (reads run freely, changes need approval) or **Manual approve** (every tool that touches your data needs approval). Scheduled tasks run without an approval step.

## Publishing articles to HubSpot

HubSpot is also a **publishing destination**: programmatic articles can land on your HubSpot blog instead of (or as well as) a separate CMS.

- Click **View** on the HubSpot card, pick the **Blog** and the **Author** the posts should use, and click **Save** — both are stored choices, so every article publishes consistently. Reconnecting the same HubSpot account keeps them.
- Ryze publishes using HubSpot's own slug and publish rules, and only marks an article as published once HubSpot confirms the post is actually live.
- If an image can't be uploaded to HubSpot, the article still publishes and the skipped image shows as a **warning** on the article, so you can fix it rather than lose the post.

<Note>
The **Blog** and **Author** choices are only needed if you publish articles to HubSpot — if you use HubSpot for CRM only, leave them empty. If the lists are empty, create a blog in HubSpot (**Content → Blog**) and an author (**Content → Blog → Authors**), then click **View** on the HubSpot card and pick them — no need to reconnect.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

- **Wrong account connected** — each connection is one HubSpot portal. To switch, click **View** on the HubSpot card and connect the right portal with **Add one more account**, and remove the wrong one with **Disconnect** (the trash icon) in the same window.
- **Connection stopped working** — click **Reconnect** on the HubSpot card and re-authorize. This refreshes access if permissions changed or the token was revoked in HubSpot.
- **Missing records or fields** — HubSpot returns a default set of properties; ask the agent for the specific fields you need. If a whole object type is missing, the connected user may not have access to it in HubSpot.
