Ryze works inside ChatGPT. Once connected, you can ask ChatGPT about your Google Ads and Meta data in plain language — no dashboards, no exports.

<Note>
You'll need a ChatGPT plan that supports apps and connectors (Plus, Pro, or Business), and **Developer mode** turned on — the steps below cover it.
</Note>

<Note>
You also need an active Ryze plan or free trial (Paid Ads, Agency or an SEO plan). Without one, ChatGPT can list your workspaces and connect platforms, but every other Ryze tool replies that a subscription is required, with a link to start your trial or fix your payment.
</Note>

## Your connector URL

You'll need this when adding Ryze to ChatGPT:

```
https://connector.get-ryze.ai/mcp
```

## Add Ryze as a connector

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Apps">
    In ChatGPT, open **Settings**, then click the **Apps** tab on the left.
    <Frame><img src="/images/chatgpt-step-1.png" alt="Open the Apps tab in ChatGPT settings" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Advanced settings">
    On the Apps page, find **Advanced settings**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/chatgpt-step-2.png" alt="The Apps page with Advanced settings" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Developer mode">
    Enable **Developer mode**. This is what lets you add a custom connector.
    <Frame><img src="/images/chatgpt-step-3.png" alt="Enable Developer mode" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an app">
    Click **Create app** at the top.
    <Frame><img src="/images/chatgpt-step-4.png" alt="Click Create app" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    - **Name** — enter `Ryze AI`
    - **MCP server URL** — enter `https://connector.get-ryze.ai/mcp` (keep **Server URL** selected, not Tunnel)
    - **Authentication** — choose **OAuth**
    - Tick **I understand and want to continue**, then click **Create**
    <Frame><img src="/images/chatgpt-step-5.png" alt="The New App form filled in" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Ryze AI">
    ChatGPT opens a Ryze authorization page. Click **Sign in with Ryze AI**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/chatgpt-step-6.png" alt="Sign in with Ryze AI" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick workspaces and allow">
    On the Ryze page **Connect your data sources to AI**, choose the workspaces ChatGPT can access (or keep **All workspaces**, up to 20 per connection), then click **Allow connection**. When exactly one workspace is selected, the page also shows **Connect your accounts**, so you can connect Google, Meta and other ad accounts right there first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    You'll see a green **Ryze AI is installed** toast. The app page lists the connection details and the Ryze tools for the platforms connected in the workspaces you picked, plus Ryze's own tools (workspaces, connections, creatives and more). Platforms you haven't connected yet don't show tools until you connect them and refresh the app — see below.
    <Frame><img src="/images/chatgpt-step-7.png" alt="Ryze AI installed in ChatGPT" /></Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## New to Ryze?

If you don't have a Ryze account yet, click **Sign up** on the Ryze sign-in page (*Create your account to connect Ryze AI to your assistant*). Enter your email and your website, click **Create account**, then type the code we email you. Ryze creates your workspace and takes you back to **Connect your data sources to AI**, where **Connect your accounts** lets you link Google, Meta and other ad platforms. Then click **Allow connection**.

To use the tools, start your free trial in **Organization Settings → Billing**, or open the link ChatGPT shows when it says a subscription is required.

## Try it

Start a **new chat** in ChatGPT and ask for what you want:

> Pull the last 7 days of Google Ads spend and break it down by campaign.

ChatGPT uses Ryze tools to answer.

<Tip>
Always start a **new chat** after connecting or reconnecting. Existing chats won't pick up the connection.
</Tip>

## Connected a new platform after installing?

ChatGPT saves the list of Ryze tools at the moment you install the app. Platforms you connect in Ryze afterwards (Google Ads, GA4, Search Console, WordPress…) don't appear in ChatGPT on their own, even in a new chat.

To refresh the list: in ChatGPT open **Settings → Apps**, remove **Ryze AI**, install it again, then start a **new chat**. Reconnecting the platform on the Ryze side doesn't refresh ChatGPT's saved list.
