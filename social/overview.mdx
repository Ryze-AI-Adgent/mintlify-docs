Social is where you plan, write and publish posts for your social accounts and see what they bring you. It works with **Instagram**, **Facebook**, **TikTok**, **YouTube**, **LinkedIn** and **Threads**.

Click **Social** in the sidebar. It opens the **Social** page with your numbers, and shows two more items:

| Item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Calendar** | Your posts week by week — planned, scheduled and published |
| **Posts** | Every post in one list, with its networks, status and numbers |

How to write, schedule and publish a post: [Posts and calendar](/social/posts).

## Plan

Social is the **Social** package: $99/month for each website, billed monthly. It isn't part of the other plans. Without it, the Social pages show **Unlock Social** — *Plan, write and publish posts for unlimited social accounts.* — with **Subscribe**. You can also subscribe on **Organization Settings → Billing**, under **Channels**. See [Channel packages](/plans/overview#channel-packages).

The package doesn't add credits: what you ask the agent to do here uses your plan's credits or credits you buy. See [Credits & Usage](/agent/credits).

## Connect your social accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Click **Connect a social account** on the Social page, or open **Integrations** in the sidebar and pick the **Social** filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    On the network's card, click **Connect**, then **Continue** — *You'll be redirected to … to authorize access.*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and allow access">
    Follow the steps on the network's sign-in page and allow access. You're sent back to Ryze.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the Page or profile">
    For **Facebook**, pick the **Page** to post to. For **LinkedIn**, pick who to **Post as** — yourself or a company page. Click **Save**. Until you do, the card shows **Require setup**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Sign in with the right account — otherwise the network won't let Ryze post:

| Network | Sign in with |
|---|---|
| **Instagram** | The Instagram Business or Creator account itself. A personal account can't post this way; no Facebook Page is needed. |
| **Facebook** | The Facebook account that is an admin or editor of the Page. Tick the Page when Facebook asks. |
| **TikTok** | The TikTok account you post from. Accept every permission TikTok asks for. |
| **YouTube** | The Google account that owns the channel. For a channel on a Brand Account, pick the Brand Account when Google asks which account to use, and keep every permission ticked. |
| **LinkedIn** | The LinkedIn member who posts, or who is an admin of the company page. |
| **Threads** | The Instagram Business or Creator account that owns the Threads profile. |

You can connect more than one account of a network with **Add one more account** on its card — except YouTube, which takes one account per workspace.

## The Social page

Until an account is connected, the page shows *See what your social brings you* and **Connect a social account**. After that:

- **The headline** — the visits your posts sent to your site and how many views they had in the period. **Show in the headline** switches between **Visits**, **Views**, **Reach**, **Engagement**, **Clicks** and **Posts**.
- **A card per network** — the number you picked, compared with the previous period, and your followers.
- **Ask the agent** — type a request, for example *Ask for a post, a reel or anything about your social*, and the agent picks it up.
- **This week** — the agent's proposals for your social accounts waiting for your OK, labeled **Social**. See [Inbox and approvals](/agent/approvals).
- **Latest posts** — your newest published posts with their views, and **Open calendar**.

Ryze reads your posts and their numbers after an account is connected, so the first numbers show up once your first posts are in. **Sync now** pulls the latest ones. More about these pages: [Channel pages](/analytics/channel-pages).

## What the agent does

Ask in the Social page box or in any chat — for example "plan next week's posts from what worked on Instagram". The agent looks at what performed on your connected accounts and proposes posts with their captions, networks and times, and says what media each one needs. A post the agent creates with a time is scheduled; without one it's saved as a draft for you to finish. Either way it shows on the **Calendar**, where you can edit it.
