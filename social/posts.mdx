One post in Ryze can go to several networks at once, with the same caption and media. You need the [Social package](/social/overview#plan) and at least one [connected account](/social/overview#connect-your-social-accounts).

## Calendar

Open **Social → Calendar**. It shows one week: the posts planned, scheduled and published on each day — including posts you published straight on a network — with their views once they're out.

- **Previous week** / **Next week** move between weeks; **All networks** filters by network.
- **New post** opens the composer.
- **Plan a post** on a day asks the agent to propose a post for that day, from what performed on your accounts recently.
- On an empty week — *Nothing planned this week* — click **Plan the week** and the agent proposes a post for each day worth posting on, with its caption, networks, time and the media it needs.

## Posts

Open **Social → Posts** for every post in one list, with **Channels**, **When**, **Views**, **Engagement rate** and **Status**. Use **Search posts** or the tabs: **All**, **Scheduled**, **Drafts**, **Published**, **Failed** and **Partly sent**. **Plan posts** asks the agent to plan the coming week.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved without a time. It stays here until you schedule or publish it. |
| **Scheduled** | Goes out at the time you picked. |
| **Publishing** | Being sent right now. |
| **Published** | Out on its networks. |
| **Partly sent** | Reached only some of its networks. |
| **Failed** | No network took it. |
| **Needs you** | A post the agent proposed. Review it under **This week** on the Social page or in the [Inbox](/agent/approvals). |

## Write a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composer">
    Click **New post** in **Calendar** or **Posts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the caption">
    Type it in **Write your caption**. The counter shows the characters used against the shortest limit among the networks you picked. **Rewrite with AI** asks the agent for three versions that hook in the first line and keep your brand voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media">
    Click **Add media** and pick photos or videos from your [Media library](/workspaces/media). Use **Remove** to take one out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the networks">
    Under **Post to**, pick each network the post goes to. A network that isn't connected shows **Connect**, which takes you to Integrations. For Instagram, **Trial reel** shows a reel to people who don't follow you first, then to your followers if it performs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    **Preview** shows how the post looks on each network you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or schedule">
    Click **Publish now** to send it right away, or **Schedule**, pick the **Date** and **Time**, and confirm — the window says which networks it goes out on. **Save draft** keeps it without a time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also ask the agent to write a post in the Social page box or any chat. A post it makes with a time is scheduled; without one it's a draft you finish in the composer.

### What each network takes

| Network | Caption limit | Media |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | 2,200 characters | A photo or a video on every post, up to 20 files |
| **Facebook** | 63,206 characters | Optional: up to 20 images or one video, not both |
| **TikTok** | 2,200 characters | One video or up to 35 photos on every post; photos and a video can't be mixed |
| **YouTube** | 5,000 characters | Exactly one video |
| **LinkedIn** | 3,000 characters | Optional: up to 20 images or one video, not both |
| **Threads** | 500 characters | Optional, up to 20 files |

Images can be JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF; videos MP4, MOV or WebM.

## After a post goes out

Click a post in **Calendar** or **Posts** to open it. The top shows when it was **Posted** or **Scheduled**, when it was **Created** and its **Format** (**Post**, **Reel**, **Story**, **Carousel**, **Video** or **Photos**). **By network** shows where it stands on each network, with **Open on** and the network's name to see it live.

- **Edit** — change a draft or a scheduled post. A post that's already out can't be edited: you'll see *This post was already sent*.
- **Send to the remaining networks** — for a post that went out only on some networks.
- **Send again** — for a network that didn't take the post. If the account was disconnected, click **Reconnect** first.
- **Delete** — removes the post from Ryze. A scheduled post is cancelled on every network; a published post stays on the networks where it's already live.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *… is not connected. Connect it in Integrations, then try again.* | Connect the account in **Integrations**, or remove that network from **Post to**. |
| *… needs a photo or a video on every post.* | Add media, or remove that network. |
| *… takes exactly one video.* | Keep one video for YouTube. |
| *… can't mix photos and a video in one post.* | Use only photos or only one video. |
| *… captions stop at … characters.* | Shorten the caption. |
| *Pick a time at least a few minutes from now.* | Choose a later time. |
| *This exact post already went out on this account in the last 24 hours.* | Change the caption or media, or don't send it again. |
| *Not sent to …. Open the post to see why.* | Open the post: **By network** shows the reason, then use **Send again** or **Reconnect**. |
| *The network did not confirm this post. Check the account, then send it again.* | Check the post on the network first, so it doesn't go out twice. |
