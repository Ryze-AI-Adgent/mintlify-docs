Clients can open the app and sign in on your own domain, and get emails from your own address instead of Ryze.

Find it under **White Label → Domains** in your organization settings.

<Note>
  Domains are part of the **Agency** and **Traffic Printer** plans. You can connect them before turning white label on; they start working for clients once **Enable white label** is on. See [Branding](/white-label/branding#turn-it-on).
</Note>

## App domain

Enter the address you want clients to use, for example `app.youragency.com`, and click **Connect**. Ryze shows the DNS record to add at your DNS provider. Some providers can add it for you in one click.

DNS changes usually apply within minutes, sometimes up to 48 hours. Until the record is found, the card shows **Waiting for DNS**. Ryze checks automatically — or click **Check status** to re-check right away — and the card shows **Live** once the domain works. From then on, your branded login page and the links in client emails use your domain.

## Email sender

Enter the address client emails should come from, for example `reports@youragency.com`, and click **Connect**. Add the DNS records shown on the card at your DNS provider; the card shows **Waiting for DNS** until they're found. Click **Check status** to re-check right away. Once the domain is **Verified**, client emails go out from your address.

Until then, emails still carry your branding and come from a Ryze address.

## Change or remove a domain

To switch to a different domain, click **Remove**, then connect the new one. Removing the app domain sends clients back to the Ryze address to sign in; removing the sender sends emails from a Ryze address again, still with your branding inside.

A domain that is never verified is removed automatically after about two days, so it does not block anyone.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Use your own domain, not a Ryze one* | Enter a domain you own. For the email sender, use an address on your own domain. |
| *This domain is already connected elsewhere. Remove it there first, then retry.* | The domain is attached to another organization. Remove it there, then connect it again here. |
| *Remove the current one first, then connect the new one.* | You can have one app domain and one sender domain at a time. Click **Remove** on the current one first. |
| *White label is available on the Agency and Traffic Printer plans* | Upgrade to Agency or Traffic Printer to use white label. See [Plans & pricing](/plans/overview). |
| *Only the organization owner can change white label domains* | Ask the organization owner to make the change. |
