The **Access** tab is where you let clients into your branded app. They sign in at your address and see only your brand — never Ryze's.

Find it under **White Label → Access** in your organization settings.

<Note>
  White label is switched on by Ryze for your organization. If you do not see **White Label** in your organization settings, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) and we will enable it.
</Note>


## Branded login link

Share your branded login URL with clients — they sign in there, under your brand.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/white-label-access.png" alt="The White Label Access tab with the login URL and client invite" />
</Frame>

When a client opens the link, they get your branded sign-in page.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/white-label-login.png" alt="A branded login page under your own brand" />
</Frame>

## Invite clients

Under **Client users**, pick a **workspace** and enter the client's **email**, then **Invite**. If the email doesn't have an account yet, one is created automatically — the client just follows the link and signs in.

<Note>
Each client belongs to a workspace. Use one workspace per client so their data, brand, and access stay separate. See [Create a workspace](/workspaces/create-workspace).
</Note>
