The **Access** tab is where you let clients into your branded app. They sign in at your address and see your logo, colors, domain and email sender. A few service emails (such as billing emails) and some labels in the app still come from Ryze.

Find it under **White Label → Access** in your organization settings.

<Note>
  White label comes with the **Agency** and **Traffic Printer** ($599/month) plans, and clients see your brand only after the owner turns on **Enable white label**. See [Branding](/white-label/branding#turn-it-on).
</Note>


## Branded login link

Share your branded login URL with clients — they sign in there, under your brand.

  <img src="/images/white-label-access.png" alt="The White Label Access tab with the login URL and client invite" />
</Frame>

When a client opens the link, they get your branded sign-in page.

  <img src="/images/white-label-login.png" alt="A branded login page under your own brand" />
</Frame>

## Invite clients

Under **Client users**, pick a **workspace** and enter the client's **email**, then **Invite**. The client becomes a member of that workspace. If the email doesn't have an account yet, one is created automatically.

The client gets an invite email. The link in it opens your branded sign-in page with their email already filled in — they click **Continue** and type the code we email them. No password is needed. The link leads to your branded page only while **Enable white label** is on; otherwise it opens the standard Ryze sign-in.

<Note>
Each client belongs to a workspace. Use one workspace per client so their data, brand, and access stay separate. See [Create a workspace](/workspaces/create-workspace).
</Note>
