White label lets an agency brand Ryze as its own. Your clients see your colors and logo across the app, their reports, and the login page — Ryze stays behind the scenes.

Find it under **White Label → Branding** in your organization settings.

<Note>
  White label comes with the **Agency** plan. Only the organization **owner** sees the **White Label** section. If you do not see it, check that your organization is on Agency under **Billing**.
</Note>

## Turn it on

The **Enable white label** switch sits at the top of the White Label section. Until it is on, your clients still see Ryze, so you can set up colors, logo, domain, and sender first and switch everything live at once.

Turning it off brings Ryze back for your clients. Your saved colors, logo, and domains stay, and come back when you turn it on again. If the Agency plan ends, white label turns off the same way.

## Colors

Set three colors and Ryze derives the rest of the theme from them:

- **Primary** — your brand color, used for buttons, links, and accents.
- **Background** — the app's base background.
- **Secondary** — a supporting accent.

These apply everywhere your clients look — the app, the reports you share, and the branded login page.

Click **Save changes** to apply. Updates show up for your clients right away.

## Logo

Upload your logo in **Organization settings**: click the organization avatar at the top of the page and pick a PNG, JPEG, or WebP file up to 5 MB. It replaces the Ryze logo in the app, on the login page, and in emails once white label is on.
