White label lets an agency brand Ryze as its own. Your clients see your colors and logo across the app, their reports, and the login page. A few service emails (such as billing) and some labels in the app still come from Ryze.

Find it under **White Label → Branding** in your organization settings.

<Note>
  White label comes with the **Agency** and **Traffic Printer** ($599/month) plans. Only the organization **owner** sees the **White Label** section. If you do not see it, check that your organization is on Agency or Traffic Printer under **Billing**.
</Note>

## Turn it on

The **Enable white label** switch sits at the top of the White Label section. Until it is on, your clients still see Ryze, so you can set up colors, logo, domain, and sender first and switch everything live at once.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/white-label-enabled.png" alt="White Label with Enable white label switched on — the app now uses the agency's own colors" />
</Frame>

Turning it off brings Ryze back for your clients. Your saved colors, logo, and domains stay, and come back when you turn it on again. If the plan that includes it ends, white label turns off the same way.


## Colors

Set three colors and Ryze derives the rest of the theme from them:

- **Primary** — your brand color, used for buttons, links, and accents.
- **Background** — the app's base background.
- **Secondary** — a supporting accent.

These apply everywhere your clients look — the app, the reports you share, and the branded login page.

Click **Save changes** to apply. Updates show up for your clients right away.

## Logo

Upload your logo at the top of the **Branding** tab: click **Upload logo** (or **Replace logo** if one is already there) and pick a PNG, JPEG, or WebP file up to 5 MB. Once white label is on, it replaces the Ryze logo in the app, in the browser tab, on the login page, and in emails.
