The Ryze AI extension for Chrome shows AI visibility for any site you open: how often AI assistants mention it, the questions it comes up for, the pages they cite and the other sites named alongside it.

## Install and connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    In Ryze, open the account menu at the bottom left and click **Chrome Extension**. It opens the extension in the Chrome Web Store — click **Add to Chrome**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in from the extension">
    Open the extension and click **Sign in**. Ryze opens in a new tab; log in if you aren't already.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a workspace and allow">
    On **Connect the Ryze AI extension**, choose the **Workspace** the extension should use and click **Allow connection**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you see "This link is missing the extension callback", open the extension and click **Sign in** again. If Ryze says you have no workspace yet, finish onboarding in the app first.

## Limits

With any active Ryze subscription you can check 100 different sites a month; without one, 10. The count is per user, resets on the 1st of each month and is shown in the extension. Past the limit, the extension says **Monthly site limit reached** until the next month.

For tracking your own brand across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity over time, use [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility) in the app.
