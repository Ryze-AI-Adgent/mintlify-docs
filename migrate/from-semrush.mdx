Semrush and Ryze solve different problems. Semrush is a research and reporting platform — it tells you what to do. Ryze is an execution agent — it does the work. Understanding that difference matters more than any feature comparison.

## What each does

| | Semrush | Ryze |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword and competitor research | Extensive | Yes, via DataForSEO |
| Site audit | Yes | Yes |
| **Ships the fixes** | No | Yes |
| **Writes and publishes content** | Assists | Yes, automated |
| Backlink analysis | Extensive | Building, not analysis |
| Backlink building | No | Yes |
| AI visibility tracking | Yes | Yes |
| Paid ads management | Research only | Yes, executes |
| Rank tracking | Yes | Yes, daily |

## Where Semrush is stronger

Being straightforward: Semrush has a deeper research toolkit and a far larger historical database. If your work is analysis — competitive research, market sizing, backlink forensics, detailed keyword exploration — Semrush does more.

Some teams keep both: Semrush for research, Ryze for execution.

## Where Ryze is different

Ryze audits, decides, and then **acts** — shipping fixes, publishing content, building links, and managing campaigns, under your approval settings. Semrush will tell you that 400 pages have missing meta descriptions; Ryze writes and ships them.

## What does not transfer

Be clear-eyed about this before switching:

- **Historical keyword and ranking data stays in Semrush.** There is no import. Export what you need first.
- **Saved projects, dashboards, and reports** do not carry over.
- **Your rank tracking history restarts.** Ryze tracks daily from when you connect.

What *does* carry over is anything that lives outside the tool: your Search Console history (up to 16 months, available on connection), your site, and your content.

## Before you cancel

1. **Export keyword lists** you have curated — these represent real work
2. **Export position tracking history** for your key terms
3. **Export the backlink profile**, particularly for a migration or disavow record
4. **Export site audit findings** you have not yet actioned
5. **Screenshot or export dashboards** used in client reporting

Do this while the subscription is live. Exports are not available afterwards.
