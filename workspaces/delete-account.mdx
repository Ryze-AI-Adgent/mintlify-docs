title: "Delete Your Account"
description: "Permanently delete your organization, all of its workspaces, and cancel every active subscription."
---

Deleting your account removes the whole organization. Only the organization owner can do this, and it cannot be undone.

## Delete your organization

1. Cancel your subscription first: **Organization Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. While a subscription is active, the account can't be deleted.
2. Open **Organization Settings → General**.
3. Scroll to **Delete account** and click **Delete account**.
4. Type `DELETE` to confirm and click **Delete account**.

You are signed out as soon as the deletion finishes.

## What gets deleted

- Every workspace in the organization: SEO articles, keywords, mentions and backlinks, issues and reports, paid-ads creatives, competitor tracking, brand profiles, analytics, chat history, connected integrations, and scheduled tasks
- Hosted blog domains and the Ryze Pixel data for those workspaces
- Team members' access to the organization

## What stays

- Content you already published to your own site or CMS, and any live ads on your ad accounts
- Your login. You can sign in again and create a new organization at any time

<Warning>
There is no grace period and no restore. If you only want to stop paying, just [cancel your subscription](/billing/manage-subscription).
</Warning>
