A workspace holds one site or client — its connections, settings, and data. Create one per business you manage.

## Create a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace switcher">
    Click the workspace name at the top-left, then choose **Create new workspace**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/workspace-switcher-create.png" alt="Create new workspace from the switcher" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a **Name**, the **Domain**, an optional **Description**, and a **Location**. Tick **Copy integrations** to reuse connections from your current workspace.
    <Frame><img src="/images/create-workspace-dialog.png" alt="The Create workspace dialog" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create workspace**. Ryze reads your site to learn your business, catalog, and market.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching workspaces

Use the same switcher at the top-left to move between workspaces. Each one keeps its own connections, settings, and data.
