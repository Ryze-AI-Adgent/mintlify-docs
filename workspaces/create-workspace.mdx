A workspace holds one site or client — its connections, settings, and data. Create one per business you manage.

## Create a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace switcher">
    Click the workspace name at the top-left, then choose **Create New Workspace**. Only the organization owner can create workspaces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a **Name**, the **Domain**, and choose a **Location**, **Language** and **Industry**. Tick **Copy integrations** to reuse connections from your current workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create workspace**. Ryze reads your site to learn your business, catalog, and market.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Does a new workspace need its own plan?

Creating a workspace is free. What it can run depends on your plan:

- **Paid Ads** covers up to 3 workspaces. In a fourth you'll see "You've reached the 3-workspace limit" with **Upgrade to Agency** — Agency covers unlimited workspaces.
- An **SEO** plan covers one website. To run SEO in another workspace, open it and click **Add SEO for this website**. It's charged at your plan's price, prorated for the current billing period, and renews with your subscription. Extra websites can be added once your trial has converted to a paid plan.

See [Plans and pricing](/plans/overview#more-than-one-website).

## Switching workspaces

Use the same switcher at the top-left to move between workspaces. Each one keeps its own connections, settings, and data.
