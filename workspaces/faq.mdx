---
title: "Workspaces & team FAQ"
description: "Common questions about workspaces and team access."
---

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How do I add a teammate?">
Open **Workspace Settings → Team**, click **Invite member**, and enter their email. They're added to the workspace right away and get an email with a sign-in link. See [Invite teammates](/workspaces/invite-teammates).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I use separate workspaces for different clients?">
Yes. Create one workspace per client or site — each keeps its own connections, settings, and data. See [Create a Workspace](/workspaces/create-workspace).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can one workspace see the accounts of another?">
No — workspaces are isolated by design, and there's no "super workspace" that spans them. If a set of accounts belongs to the same business, connect them all to one workspace instead. See [Managing Multiple Accounts](/integrations/managing-multiple-accounts).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I switch between workspaces?">
Use the workspace switcher at the top-left and pick another workspace.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete a workspace?">
Open **Organization Settings → Workspaces** and click **Delete workspace** on the one you no longer need. This removes its connections, settings and data for good, so double-check you're in the right one. Only the organization owner can delete workspaces, and an organization always keeps at least one, so the last workspace can't be deleted.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I remove a teammate?">
To remove someone from one workspace, open **Workspace Settings → Team** and choose **Remove from workspace** next to their name. To remove them from every workspace at once, open **Organization Settings → Users** and choose **Remove from organization**. Only the organization owner sees the remove option, and the owner can't be removed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I invite someone whose email already has a Ryze account?">
Yes. Invite them from **Workspace Settings → Team** as usual. They're added right away, and the workspace appears in their switcher the next time they sign in; the email (check spam) just gives them a sign-in link. Every teammate gets their own login — no need to share passwords.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I run several websites. Do I need separate Ryze accounts?">
No — one account handles multiple sites. Create a workspace per site or brand and connect that site's accounts inside it. The property you pick when connecting Search Console or Analytics is only the default; the agent can work across every property on the connected Google login.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't sign in: the code or reset email never arrives.">
Some mail hosts (often Microsoft/Outlook domains) quarantine the one-time code. Check your spam or quarantine folder and mark our emails as not spam, or email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) from your account address. If you see "No account found with this email.", there's no account under that address yet: click **Sign up instead**, or check which address you registered with. Forgot your password? Click **Sign in with password** → **Forgot password?** → **Send reset code**. See [Sign in and passwords](/workspaces/sign-in).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I signed in and my organization is gone.">
Nothing is deleted. Ryze signs you in by email only — there's no Google sign-in — so an empty account usually means you used a different email address than the one you signed up with. Sign out and sign in with your original address. If you still can't find your organization, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why can't I see Organization Settings or Billing?">
Only the organization owner — the person who created the organization — can open **Organization Settings**, manage billing, create and delete workspaces, and remove people. Everyone else is a member of the workspaces they were added to. See [Owner and members](/workspaces/invite-teammates#owner-and-members).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I transfer ownership of the organization?">
Not from the app. If the owner needs to change, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) from the current owner's address.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I rename my workspace or organization?">
Workspace name and slug are under **Workspace Settings → Workspace**. The organization name is under **Organization Settings → General**.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
