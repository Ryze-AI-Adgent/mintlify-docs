---
title: "Workspaces & team FAQ"
description: "Common questions about workspaces and team access."
---

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How do I add a teammate?">
Open **Workspace Settings → Team**, click **Invite member**, and enter their email. They'll get a sign-in link. See [Invite Teammates](/workspaces/invite-teammates).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I use separate workspaces for different clients?">
Yes. Create one workspace per client or site — each keeps its own connections, settings, and data. See [Create a Workspace](/workspaces/create-workspace).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can one workspace see the accounts of another?">
No — workspaces are isolated by design, and there's no "super workspace" that spans them. If a set of accounts belongs to the same business, connect them all to one workspace instead. See [Managing Multiple Accounts](/integrations/managing-multiple-accounts).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I switch between workspaces?">
Use the workspace switcher at the top-left and pick another workspace.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete a workspace?">
Open **Organization Settings → Workspaces** and click **Delete workspace** on the one you no longer need. This removes its connections, settings and data for good, so double-check you're in the right one.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I remove a teammate?">
To remove someone from one workspace, open **Workspace Settings → Team** and choose **Remove from workspace** next to their name. To remove them from every workspace at once, open **Organization Settings → Users** and choose **Remove from organization**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I invite someone whose email already has a Ryze account?">
Yes. Invite them from **Workspace Settings → Team** as usual; they accept the email invite (check spam) and the workspace appears in their switcher. Every teammate gets their own login — no need to share passwords.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I run several websites. Do I need separate Ryze accounts?">
No — one account handles multiple sites. Create a workspace per site or brand and connect that site's accounts inside it. The property you pick when connecting Search Console or Analytics is only the default; the agent can work across every property on the connected Google login.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't sign in: the code or reset link never arrives.">
Some mail hosts (often Microsoft/Outlook domains) quarantine the one-time code. Check your spam or quarantine folder and mark our emails as not spam, or email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) from your account address. If you see "Signups not allowed for OTP", there's no account under that email yet: create one at reports.get-ryze.ai/signup or check which address you registered with.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I signed in with Google and my organization is gone.">
Nothing is deleted. Signing in with Google and signing in with the email code can create two different identities for the same address, and one of them is empty. Use the sign-in method you originally signed up with; if you need the two merged, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I rename my workspace or organization?">
Workspace name and slug are under **Workspace Settings → Workspace**. The organization name is under **Organization Settings → General**.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
