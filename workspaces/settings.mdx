---

Open the account menu at the bottom left → **Workspace Settings**. The page has four tabs: **Profile**, **Workspace**, **Notifications** and **Team**.

## Profile

Your personal account, the same in every workspace:

- **Full name** — click **Save changes** after editing it.
- **Email** — shown for reference; it can't be changed here.
- **Password** — click **Change**, enter your current password and the new one twice, then click **Update password**. Forgot your password? On the sign-in page click **Sign in with password** → **Forgot password?** and enter the code we email you. See [Sign in and passwords](/workspaces/sign-in).
- **API keys** — keys for your own AI agents. See [Ryze API for agents](/agent-api).

## Workspace

- **Name** — the display name for this workspace
- **Slug** — used in the workspace URL
- **Domain** — the site this workspace covers
- **Location** — the market this workspace targets
- **Language** — the language for generated content

Click **Save changes** when you're done.

## Notifications

Which emails you get for this workspace. See [Notifications](/workspaces/notifications).

## Team

Who has access to this workspace, and **Invite member**. See [Invite teammates](/workspaces/invite-teammates).

## Brand voice

How your brand sounds and looks lives on the **Brand** page. See [Brand](/agent/context).
