Open **Workspace Settings → Workspace** to manage how this workspace is set up.

## Workspace basics

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workspace-settings.png" alt="Workspace settings fields" />
</Frame>

- **Name** — the display name for this workspace
- **Slug** — used in the workspace URL
- **Description** — optional note
- **Domain** — the site this workspace covers
- **Location** — sets the language for generated content

Click **Save changes** when you're done.

## Brand voice

Scroll to **Brand voice** to teach Ryze how your brand sounds and looks. Everything Ryze writes — articles, ad copy, images — follows what you set here.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/brand-voice-1.png" alt="Brand voice: about the brand and voice & tone" />
</Frame>

- **About the brand** — what you do, for whom, and why
- **Voice & tone** — how you sound; words you love and avoid
- **Writing examples** — paste 2–3 real samples as a reference for the AI
- **Photo style** — words describing how images should look
- **Reference images** — upload up to 5 examples of your visual style

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/brand-voice-2.png" alt="Brand voice: writing examples, photo style, reference images" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
The more specific your brand voice, the more your content sounds like you and not generic AI.
</Tip>

Click **Save changes** to apply.
