Bring your team into Ryze so everyone works in the same place.

## Invite a teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Go to **Workspace Settings → Team**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/team-tab.png" alt="The Team tab in Workspace Settings with the Invite member button" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite member">
    Click **Invite member** at the top-right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Type their email and click **Send invite**. They'll get an email with a sign-in link.
    <Frame><img src="/images/invite-teammate-dialog.png" alt="The Invite teammate dialog" /></Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

They're added to the workspace right away and appear under **Members** — there's nothing to accept. The email just gives them a sign-in link; if the address has no Ryze account yet, one is created for it.

Anyone in the workspace can invite teammates. Only the organization owner can remove people.

<Tip>
Inviting your first teammate adds 200 bonus credits to your organization, once per organization.
</Tip>

## Manage people across the organization

The organization owner can open **Organization Settings → Users** to see everyone with access and choose **Remove from organization** to take someone out of every workspace at once. To remove someone from one workspace only, use **Remove from workspace** in **Workspace Settings → Team**. The owner can't be removed.

## Owner and members

Ryze has two levels of access:

- **Organization owner** — the person who created the organization. Only the owner can open **Organization Settings** (General, Workspaces, Users, Usage, Billing, White Label), manage billing, create and delete workspaces, remove people, set up white label and delete the account.
- **Members** — everyone else. Members work inside the workspaces they're added to — chat, reports, integrations, publishing — and can invite teammates to those workspaces.

There are no other roles. Ownership can't be transferred from the app — if the owner needs to change, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) from the current owner's address.

An organization always keeps at least one workspace, so the last workspace can't be deleted.
