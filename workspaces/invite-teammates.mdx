---

Bring your team into Ryze so everyone works in the same place.

## Invite a teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Go to **Workspace Settings → Team**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/team-tab.png" alt="The Team tab in Workspace Settings" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite member">
    Click **Invite member** at the top-right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Type their email and click **Send invite**. They'll get an email with a sign-in link.
    <Frame><img src="/images/invite-teammate-dialog.png" alt="The Invite teammate dialog" /></Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once they accept, they appear under **Members**.

## Need to manage users across the org?

Open [Users](https://reports.get-ryze.ai/settings/users) in your organization settings to see everyone with access.
