## Email notifications

Open the account menu at the bottom left → **Workspace Settings** → **Notifications**. Each switch is one type of email for this workspace:

| Email | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Approvals ready** | New findings from your agent that are waiting for your approval. |
| **Articles digest** | A summary of your articles a few times a week: new drafts, failed generations and articles waiting on a CMS connection. |
| **Pixel delivery issues** | An alert when a pixel destination stops accepting events or needs to be reconnected. See [Ryze Pixel](/paid-ads/ryze-pixel). |
| **Keywords and prompts refreshed** | A digest after each scheduled refresh: Search Console position moves and AI visibility for your prompts. See [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility). |

## Approval alerts in Slack or email

To choose who gets pinged when the agent has changes waiting for sign-off, open the **Inbox** (Home → **Needs you** → **View all**) and click **Notifications** at the top. You can send these alerts to Slack channels and email addresses. See [Inbox and approvals](/agent/approvals).

Scheduled tasks have their own **Email me when each run finishes** switch — see [Scheduled tasks](/agent/schedules).
