Ryze AI visibility tracks how often your brand and your pages appear in AI-generated answers, and ships the on-page work that makes your site retrievable by the systems producing those answers.

It covers two halves: **measurement** (what the engines currently say and cite) and **execution** (the changes that improve it).

## What is monitored

Ryze monitors visibility across:

- **ChatGPT**
- **Perplexity**
- **Google AI Overviews**
- **Claude**
- **Google and Bing** conventional search
- **Shopify** search surfaces

For each, Ryze records whether your brand was named, which of your URLs were cited, and which competitors appeared alongside you.

## What Ryze does about it

Measurement alone changes nothing. The execution side ships the work that makes pages retrievable:

- **Title and meta optimization** — clear, extractable page-level statements
- **Schema markup** — product and review structured data so machines can parse your catalogue
- **LLM-optimized product copy** — descriptions written to be parsed and quoted, not just to persuade
- **Programmatic pages** tuned for both LLMs and humans, built from real demand signals
- **Internal linking** so deep pages are reachable
- **Core Web Vitals** work, since pages that fail to render are pages that cannot be cited

Approved technical fixes ship within hours.

## The two numbers that matter

**[Citation share](/concepts/citation-share)** — how often your URLs are used as sources. Responds to content work over weeks to months.

**[Share of voice](/concepts/share-of-voice-ai)** — how often your brand is named. Reflects how established you are in the model's training data. Moves far more slowly and is not directly controllable.

Improving citations is the tractable goal. Read [why your brand isn't cited](/ai-visibility/not-cited) before setting expectations on share of voice.

## Honest limits

- **Citations are volatile.** The same question asked twice can return different sources. Judge by rate across many queries over time, never by a single result.
- **Ryze cannot make a model say something.** It improves the odds that when a model searches, it finds an accurate page of yours rather than a competitor's.
- **Platform-worded queries are structurally hard.** When a buyer asks a question phrased around a specific platform, that platform's own properties dominate the answer. There is limited room for third-party brands in those clusters.
