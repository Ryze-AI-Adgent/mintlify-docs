---
title: "Why your brand isn't cited"
description: "The usual reasons a brand gets no citations in AI answers, ordered from most to least common, and what to do about each."
---

If your domain gets no citations in AI answers, the cause is almost always one of the following. They are ordered from most to least common — work down the list.

## 1. You have nothing retrievable to cite

The most common cause by far. Answer engines cite pages that answer a specific question factually. Most sites have only marketing pages and a blog, neither of which is shaped to be extracted from.

A landing page argues that you are good. It does not state what something is, what it costs, how it works, or what its limits are. There is nothing for a model to lift.

**Fix:** publish factual, narrow pages — one topic each, definition in the first sentence. Documentation and help-centre content is consistently the most-cited vendor page type.

## 2. Your pages are not crawlable or not renderable

If body text requires JavaScript execution, many retrieval crawlers will not see it. Content behind auth, behind a cookie wall, or blocked in `robots.txt` is invisible.

**Fix:** confirm body content is present in the served HTML. Check `robots.txt` does not block the pages you want cited. Do not gate documentation behind a login.

## 3. Your pages are too broad

A page covering ten topics competes for none of them. Models retrieve to answer a specific question, and a narrow page that answers it precisely beats a comprehensive guide that mentions it.

**Fix:** split. One page, one thing. `what-is-x` outperforms `the-complete-guide-to-x`.

## 4. Your content is persuasive rather than factual

Marketing language gives a model nothing to extract. Sentences that could sit on any competitor's site carry no information.

**Fix:** state facts, numbers, limits, and constraints. Say what the product does *not* do. Cited pages read like specifications, not brochures.

## 5. The prompts you track are structurally unwinnable

Some prompt clusters are dominated by one platform's own properties, or by large review sites. In those, third-party content has a hard ceiling regardless of quality.

**Fix:** measure those clusters separately and stop spending content effort against them. See [prompt monitoring](/ai-visibility/prompt-monitoring).

## 6. Not enough time has passed

Pages must be crawled, indexed, and then retrieved before they can be cited. Weeks to months, not days.

**Fix:** wait, and judge by trend across a quarter.

## 7. Your brand is not in the model's priors

If competitors are *named* without any page being cited, they are in the training data and you are not. No amount of your own content changes that directly.

**Fix:** accept the timeline. Third-party presence over many months is the only path. See [competitor share-of-voice](/ai-visibility/share-of-voice).
