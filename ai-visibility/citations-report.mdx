The citations report shows which URLs are being used as sources in AI answers — yours and everyone else's — for your tracked prompt set.

## What the report contains

**Your cited URLs.** Every page of yours that appeared as a source, with how many times and for which prompts. This tells you which pages are actually working.

**Cited domains overall.** Every domain appearing as a source across the prompt set, ranked by citation count. This is the competitive field as the engines see it, which often differs from the one you track commercially.

**Per-prompt breakdown.** For each prompt, the sources the answer drew on. The most useful view for diagnosis.

**Engine split.** The same prompt across ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, and Claude, since results differ substantially by engine.

## How to read it

**Start with the prompts where you are absent.** Look at what *was* cited instead. The gap between those pages and yours is the actionable finding — usually a page that answers the question more directly than anything you have published.

**Look at page shape, not just domain.** When competitors are cited, note *which* page. It is usually a narrow, factual page — one feature explained, one setting defined, one problem solved — rather than a landing page or a long guide.

**Check whether your cited pages are the ones you intended.** Frequently the cited page is not the commercial page you care about. That is still useful, but it means the answer is not routing buyers where you want them.

**Read the domain distribution for structure.** If one platform's own domains take most citations for a cluster of prompts, that cluster has a ceiling. Do not spend content effort there — see [prompt monitoring](/ai-visibility/prompt-monitoring).

## Common patterns and what they mean

| pattern | likely cause |
|---|---|
| Cited on Perplexity, not ChatGPT | Pages are retrievable but not being selected — relevance or clarity |
| Cited nowhere at all | Nothing retrievable exists, or pages are not crawlable |
| Cited but never mentioned | Pages are useful; brand is not yet a recommendation |
| Only your blog is cited | Commercial pages are too promotional to extract from |
| Citations swing week to week | Normal volatility — read the monthly trend |

## Interpreting counts honestly

Citation counts are small numbers and behave like small numbers. A move from 3 to 5 is not a 67% improvement; it is noise unless it holds across several cycles.

The reliable read is **direction across a quarter**, on a stable prompt set, against a recorded baseline.
