SEO in Ryze is a content engine that runs on its own. It looks at your site and your search data, decides which pages and keywords are worth winning, then writes articles, publishes them to your store or CMS, and builds off-page mentions — month after month, without you steering each step.

</Frame>

## What it does

- **Audits your site** — pulls Google Search Console, DataForSEO, and your store to see where you stand.
- **Picks targets** — chooses the pages worth ranking and the keywords worth chasing.
- **Writes & publishes articles** — long-tail programmatic articles, drafted and pushed live to your CMS.
- **Fixes on-page issues** — an agent ships technical and GEO fixes for you.
- **Builds off-page authority** — guest posts, press releases, and the backlinks they earn.

## On-page vs off-page

Ryze works both sides of SEO:

- **On-page** — everything on your own site: programmatic articles and technical fixes.
- **Off-page** — everything that points back to your site: guest posts, press releases, and backlinks.

## See what the agent is doing

Because the engine runs on its own, **Agent Activity** is where you watch it work — everything Ryze has done for you, step by step, with timestamps: articles drafted, articles published, fixes shipped. Your home page shows the latest activity at a glance, and the full log lives under **Agent Logs** in the sidebar.
