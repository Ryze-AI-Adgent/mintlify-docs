SEO in Ryze is a content engine that runs on its own. It looks at your site and your search data, decides which pages and keywords are worth winning, then writes articles, publishes them to your store or CMS, and builds off-page mentions — month after month, without you steering each step.

## What it does

- **Audits your site** — pulls Google Search Console, live search data, and your store to see where you stand.
- **Picks targets** — chooses the pages worth ranking and the keywords worth chasing.
- **Writes & publishes articles** — long-tail articles in your [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic), drafted and pushed live to your CMS.
- **Fixes on-page issues** — the [Technical Audit](/seo/optimizations) finds technical and GEO issues, and the agent fixes what your platform lets it change.
- **Wins on Google Maps** — with [Local SEO](/seo/local-seo), tracks where your business shows up on the map around your office and helps you handle reviews, posts and your Google Business Profile listing.
- **Builds off-page authority** — on Traffic Printer, guest posts, a press release and 50 backlinks a month.

## What's included in your plan

Both SEO plans include Content Plan (up to 90 articles per billing cycle), Technical Audit, Keywords & Prompts, Local SEO, AI Visibility, Backlink Exchange and Blog Studio. **Traffic Printer** adds backlinks, 3 guest posts and 1 press release every month. See [Plans & pricing](/plans/overview).

## On-page vs off-page

Ryze works both sides of SEO:

- **On-page** — everything on your own site: Content Plan articles and technical fixes.
- **Off-page** — everything that points back to your site: guest posts, press releases, and backlinks.

## See what the agent is doing

Because the engine runs on its own, you don't need to watch it step by step:

- **Home** shows where your SEO stands — the **When to expect results** timeline, Search performance, AI visibility — and anything that needs your decision under **Needs you**.
- **Content Plan** lists your articles and their status: planned, drafted, published.
- **Technical Audit** shows the fixes, and **Backlinks & PR** shows guest posts, press releases and links.
