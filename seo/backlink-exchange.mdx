title: "Backlink Exchange"
description: "Reciprocal link building across the Ryze network — as your articles publish, they place relevant partner links from other sites, and those sites link back to you."
---

Backlink Exchange is how Ryze builds links between the sites in its network. As your programmatic articles publish, Ryze places a relevant link to a partner site inside the body — and that partner's articles place a link back to you. It runs automatically, with no outreach on your end.

## How it works

- **Reciprocal.** You link out to a partner, a partner links back to you — links flow both ways across the network.
- **Relevant.** Partners are matched by industry and audience, so every placement makes sense in context. Direct competitors and your own other workspaces are never matched.
- **Automatic.** Links go in as articles publish. One exchange link per article, placed naturally in the body — it's an outbound link and never crowds out the article's internal links.
- **Fair.** Sites that have received fewer links surface first, so placements spread evenly instead of piling onto a few domains.

## Who takes part

Only contributing sites are matched — a workspace needs a connected store and an active plan to both give and receive exchange links. That keeps the network made of real, live sites rather than empty ones.

## Turning it off

Backlink Exchange is on by default. To opt a workspace out, open **Content settings** and set **Backlink exchange** to disabled — Ryze stops placing exchange links for that workspace, in both directions.
