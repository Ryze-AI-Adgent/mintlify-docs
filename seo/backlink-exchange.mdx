Backlink Exchange is how Ryze builds links between the sites in its network. As your Content Plan articles publish, Ryze places a relevant link to a partner site inside the body — and that partner's articles place a link back to you. It runs automatically, with no outreach on your end.

## How it works

- **Paced, not on every post.** Ryze adds at most one exchange link for every **3 articles** you publish, so links drip out rather than appearing in every post.
- **One link, buried in real content.** When it does place a link, it's a single outbound link in the **second half** of the article, woven into a sentence that already makes a point — never a "useful resource" footnote, and never in the intro.
- **Only when it genuinely fits.** Most articles get no exchange link at all. Ryze places one only when a real, relevant partner exists — a forced link hurts both sites, so it would rather place nothing.
- **It doesn't touch your own links.** The exchange link is extra — it never counts against or crowds out the article's internal links.

## How partners are matched

Matching is strict. A partner is only picked if a reader of *that specific article* would actually click through to it for more on the topic — an adjacent niche, an alternative they're weighing, or something genuinely useful to the subject.

Ryze **never** matches:

- **Direct competitors** — and once flagged, you're never matched with them again (including the same brand on another domain).
- **Your own other workspaces** — links never bounce around inside your own account.
- **On shared geography or industry alone** — "both in Australia" or "both in finance" isn't relevance.
- **A business you might sell to** — a sales lead is not a link your reader clicks.

## Who takes part

Only live sites take part. A workspace **receives** exchange links when it has:

- an active SEO plan (SEO Starter or Traffic Printer),
- the **Backlink exchange** switch on,
- at least one published article,
- a publishing platform on its own domain — a connected CMS, or Ryze Hosting with a custom domain.

Your own articles **place** links to partners only while **Publishing mode** is **Automatic**. In **Manual** mode you can still receive exchange links, but none are placed in your articles.

Sites that have received fewer links are matched first, so placements spread evenly instead of piling onto a few domains.


<Note>
**Ryze-hosted blogs on the default address don't take part.** A workspace joins the exchange through a publish target on its own domain — a connected CMS, or Ryze Hosting with a custom domain. Articles on a `*.byryze.com` address neither receive exchange links nor hand them out, so links stay between real sites on their own domains. If you publish to both your CMS and a hosted blog, only the CMS copies are used for links.
</Note>


## Turning it off

Backlink Exchange is on by default. To opt a workspace out, open **SEO → Preferences → Publishing** and turn off the **Backlink exchange** switch. Ryze stops placing exchange links for that workspace, in both directions.
