AI Visibility shows how AI assistants answer the questions your buyers ask — and whether they name you. Open **Analytics → AI Visibility** in the sidebar. It's part of the SEO plans and opens the **GEO** tab of the [Search Performance](/analytics/performance) page.

## What you need

- **Google Search Console** — without it the whole page asks you to connect it. See [Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console).
- **Google Analytics** — the GEO tab uses it to measure traffic from AI assistants, so the tab stays closed until it's connected. See [Google Analytics](/integrations/google-analytics).
- **Prompts** — the questions to ask. Add them in **SEO → Keywords & Prompts**, on the **Prompts** tab: click **Add** and enter one prompt per line, or use **Suggest prompts**. You can track up to 50 prompts. See [Keywords & Prompts](/seo/keywords).

## Which assistants

Every prompt is asked on **ChatGPT**, **Claude**, **Gemini** and **Perplexity**.

## What you see

- **AI Share of Voice** — your rank among the brands named in answers, and how many answers name you.
- **AI answers naming you**, **Your site cited**, **AI Sessions** and the **LLM Optimization Score** from your latest technical audit.
- **Visibility Over Time** — share of answers naming each brand, one point per run.
- **Visibility by Assistant** and **Coverage by Model** — how you do on each assistant.
- **Rankings** — every brand the assistants named across your prompts.
- **Top Domains** — the sites the assistants linked to while answering.
- **Prompts** — every tracked prompt on every assistant, with score, whether you were cited, and the top brand.
- **AI Traffic**, **AI Chats Traffic** and **AI Landing Pages** — visits from AI assistants, from Google Analytics.

## Keeping it fresh

- **Refresh** — at the top of the page. On the GEO tab it re-asks all your prompts on the four assistants and updates the results.
- **Auto-refresh** — on **Keywords & Prompts**, open **Settings** next to Refresh and pick a schedule: **Off** (refresh only when you press the button), **Weekly** or **Monthly**. Off is the default. Auto-refresh also updates your keyword positions from Search Console.

After each scheduled refresh you get an email digest with position moves and your AI visibility. Turn it off under **Workspace Settings → Notifications** → **Keywords and prompts refreshed** — see [Notifications](/workspaces/notifications).

## Getting named more often

Being named depends on the content AI assistants can find and cite. The agent works on this through your [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic) and [Technical Audit](/seo/optimizations); the Top Domains list shows which sites the assistants trust for your topics.
