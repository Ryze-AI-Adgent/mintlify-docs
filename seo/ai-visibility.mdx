AI Visibility shows which AI assistants send people to your site, whether they name you when your buyers ask, and what to fix so they do. Open **Agentic** in the sidebar. It's included in both SEO plans, and without an SEO plan it's available as the **Agentic** package — see [Plans & pricing](/plans/overview#channel-packages).

## What you need

- **Your site's visits** — the page counts visits from AI assistants through **Google Analytics 4**, **PostHog** or the **Ryze Pixel**. Until one of them is connected, the page shows *See which AI assistants send people to you* with a **Connect** button for each. See [Google Analytics](/integrations/google-analytics), [PostHog](/integrations/posthog) and [Ryze Pixel](/paid-ads/ryze-pixel).
- **Prompts** — the questions your buyers ask. Type one into the box on the page and click **Track this prompt**, or add several in **SEO → Keywords & Prompts**, on the **Prompts** tab (**Add** or **Suggest prompts**). You can track up to 50 prompts. See [Keywords & Prompts](/seo/keywords).

Every prompt is asked on **ChatGPT**, **Claude**, **Gemini** and **Perplexity**.

## What you see

- **AI assistant visits** at the top — how many visits AI assistants sent in the period you picked. Click **Set up metrics** and the agent saves what counts as a result on your site (orders and revenue, leads, bookings or demos); then the top line shows what those visits earned or how many became results.
- **A card per assistant** — visits (or your metric) from each assistant, compared with the previous period. **Show on the assistant cards** switches between **AI visits**, **Conversion rate** and **Revenue**.
- **Fixes** — things that stop assistants from reading or naming you: AI crawlers blocked in robots.txt, robots.txt that doesn't load, no llms.txt, a homepage without Organization or WebSite schema, or a top assistant that rarely names you. Each one has a button that hands the fix to the agent.
- **Tracked prompts** — every prompt with the assistants that name you. Filter by **Named**, **Not named** or **Waiting**. Click a prompt to read each assistant's answer, where it ranks you (**Named #N**), which of your pages it cites, and which other brands it names. If none of your pages is cited, **Write page** asks the agent to write one that answers the question.
- **AI readiness** — three checks from your [Technical Audit](/seo/optimizations): **Lets every AI in**, **Introduces your business** and **Describes your business**. **Fix with the agent** works through the failing ones.
- **Sources** — the connected accounts the page reads from, and whether each one is synced.

**Sync now** at the top pulls the latest visits from the connected source.

The **GEO** tab of the [Search Performance](/analytics/performance#search-performance) dashboard shows the same AI traffic as charts: **AI Sessions**, **AI traffic**, **AI Chats Traffic** and **AI Landing Pages**.

## Keeping it fresh

- **Refresh** — on **SEO → Keywords & Prompts**. It re-asks all your prompts on the four assistants and updates the answers.
- **Auto-refresh** — on **Keywords & Prompts**, open **Settings** next to Refresh and pick a schedule: **Off** (refresh only when you press the button), **Weekly** or **Monthly**. Off is the default. Auto-refresh also updates your keyword positions from Search Console.

After each scheduled refresh you get an email digest with position moves and your AI visibility. Turn it off under **Workspace Settings → Notifications** → **Keywords and prompts refreshed** — see [Notifications](/workspaces/notifications).

## Getting named more often

Being named depends on the content AI assistants can find and cite. The agent works on this through your [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic) and [Technical Audit](/seo/optimizations); open a prompt to see which sites the assistants cite instead of you.
