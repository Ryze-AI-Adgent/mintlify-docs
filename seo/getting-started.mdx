## The setup chat

You answer one question at a time, in this order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="How you found us">
    A quick question about where you heard of Ryze.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How big is your team?">
    **Just me**, **2–5**, **6–20**, **21–50** or **51+**. Ryze asks this once per organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Who else is on your team?">
    Optional, and free — no extra seats. Invited teammates get full access: integrations, chat and Slack. Click **Skip for now** to do it later. If you answered **Just me**, this step is skipped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seen how it works?">
    A two-minute video on how Ryze SEO works. Click **I've watched** to go on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your website">
    Enter the site you want to grow. Ryze reads it and asks **Is this you?** — what you sell, your industry and your market. Click **Yes, that's us**, or **No, let me fix it** to correct the details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your data">
    Connect [Google Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console) — it shows what Google already sends you, and the [Technical Audit](/seo/optimizations) needs it to run. Connect [Google Analytics](/integrations/google-analytics) too if you can. No Search Console yet? Click **Skip for now** and connect it later in **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Where your articles are published">
    Pick the platform your site runs on and connect it. If you're not ready, choose **Not now, connect my site later** and tell Ryze which platform your site runs on — Ryze then hosts your blog on a free Ryze subdomain for now. You can connect your own domain later in [Blog Studio](/seo/preferences#blog-studio).
  </Step>
  <Step title="How new articles go out">
    **Publish automatically** — each article goes live on its scheduled date — or **I'll approve each one**. You can change this later in [SEO → Preferences](/seo/preferences#publishing). If Ryze couldn't set up a blog for you in the previous step, this question is skipped and articles wait as drafts until you connect a platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click the button at the end of the setup, and your agent continues in the same chat:

- It checks that your site opens. If the address is wrong, it tells you where to fix it: the **Domain** field in **Workspace Settings → Workspace**.
- It checks your publishing setup.
- It offers a first set of keywords to pick, each with its monthly search volume.
- For a local business, it offers to connect your [Google Business Profile](/seo/local-seo). If you have several locations, it asks which one to work on.
- It asks a few questions about your business and what you want from SEO.
- It offers to audit your site now and fix the errors with you, one by one.
- It plans your first articles from your picks and answers.
- It lets you choose reference images, so article visuals match your brand.
- It writes your first article in your style with a generated cover, shows it to you and asks whether to publish it to your site now.
- It suggests regular reports and checks that fit your goals — for a local business, review replies and a weekly report for your listing.

If you get stuck connecting something, tell the agent — it can do much of the setup for you in the chat.

The agent never publishes, runs an audit or sets up recurring tasks on its own — it asks first, and acts only after you say yes.

## What happens next

You don't need to do anything else. Ryze plans the rest of the month's [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic) and keeps writing and publishing articles on schedule. The [Technical Audit](/seo/optimizations) runs weekly on its own. On the Traffic Printer plan, guest posts, a press release and backlinks start too — see [Backlinks](/seo/backlinks).

Track progress on **Home** — the **When to expect results** timeline and any **Finish setup** steps left. See [Home and Finish setup](/home).
