---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Common questions about Ryze SEO — articles, publishing, keywords, and off-page."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many articles does Ryze publish?">
    Ryze works toward a monthly target and produces only what's still missing for the cycle. Articles are published at a steady daily pace rather than all at once, so your site grows naturally rather than spiking.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I choose the keywords and topics myself?">
    No — Ryze picks them for you from your real data (Search Console, DataForSEO, your store). You can still review and edit any article draft before it goes live. See [Keywords](/seo/keywords) and [Target Pages](/seo/target-pages).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I edit an article before it's published?">
    Yes. Open any draft and click **Edit** to change the title, slug, meta, body, image, or scheduled date. You can edit an article in any state except Published — once it's live, edits happen on your CMS.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I choose when an article publishes?">
    Yes. Every article gets an automatic publish date, spread out around five a day so your content rolls out steadily. Open a draft, click **Edit**, and change the **Scheduled date** to move it earlier or later — or point several at the same day for a bigger drop. Use **Calendar view** to see and rebalance the whole schedule at a glance. See [Programmatic Articles](/seo/programmatic#scheduling).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do articles get published?">
    To the first CMS connected in your workspace — Shopify, WordPress, Framer, Wix, or GitHub. Connect one under [integrations](/integrations/overview) first; without a CMS there's nowhere to publish. See [Programmatic Articles](/seo/programmatic).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if publishing fails?">
    The article is marked **Failed** with the error. Ryze retries a few times automatically before skipping, and you can always re-publish manually from the article.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are the search volumes and difficulty real?">
    Yes. Volume and difficulty come from live DataForSEO data for your country. Ryze never invents metrics.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Ryze build backlinks?">
    Yes, at scale and with no outreach from you. The main driver is Ryze's **partner network**, which places around 100 backlinks a month back to your pages — plus the links earned from [guest post](/seo/guest-posts) placements and [press release](/seo/press) syndication. Ryze also tracks your domain rating and referring domains over time. See [Backlinks](/seo/backlinks).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between on-page and off-page?">
    On-page is everything on your own site — programmatic articles and technical fixes. Off-page is everything pointing back to your site — guest posts, press, and the backlinks they earn.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
