<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many articles does Ryze publish?">
    Up to 90 articles per billing cycle, up to 3 a day on weekdays. Ryze produces only what's still missing for the cycle, and articles are published at a steady daily pace rather than all at once, so your site grows naturally rather than spiking. Move scheduled dates to publish less often or stack several on one day. See [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic#scheduling).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I choose the keywords and topics myself?">
    No — Ryze picks them for you from your real data (Search Console, live search data, your store). You can delete keywords you don't want, ask the agent to add new ones, and review and edit any article draft before it goes live. See [Keywords & Prompts](/seo/keywords).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I edit an article before or after it's published?">
    Yes. Open the article and click **Edit** (in the list: **⋯ → Edit / Reschedule**) to change the title, slug, meta, body, image, or scheduled date on the edit page, then click **Save**. Published articles stay editable too: save your edits, then click **Publish edits** on the article page to update the live copy (the slug of a live article can't change). From the **⋯** menu of a published article you can also **Republish**, **Unpublish** or **Delete** it. See [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic#edit-move-or-remove-a-published-article).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I choose when an article publishes?">
    Yes. Every article gets an automatic publish date, spread out so your content rolls out steadily. Open a draft, click **Edit**, and change the **Scheduled date** to move it earlier or later — or point several at the same day for a bigger drop. Use **Calendar view** on Content Plan to see the whole schedule at a glance. See [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic#scheduling).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do articles get published?">
    To the platform set in **Publish to** under **SEO → Preferences → Publishing** — Shopify, WordPress, Webflow, Framer, Wix, GitHub, Ryze Hosting and more. Connect one under [Integrations](/integrations/overview) first; without one there's nowhere to publish. See [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic#where-it-publishes).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if publishing fails?">
    Ryze retries the publish up to 3 times. After that the article is marked **Failed** with the error — open it from the **Failed** tab and click **Retry**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are the search volumes and difficulty real?">
    Yes. Volume and difficulty come from live search data for your country. Ryze never invents metrics.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Ryze build backlinks?">
    On the Traffic Printer plan — yes, with no outreach from you. The main driver is Ryze's **partner network**, which places 50+ backlinks a month, steadily through the month — plus the links earned from [guest post](/seo/guest-posts) placements and [press release](/seo/press) syndication. On SEO Starter, click **Unlock Mentions** on the **Backlinks & PR** page to add them; see [Plans & pricing](/plans/overview). Both SEO plans take part in [Backlink Exchange](/seo/backlink-exchange). See [Backlinks](/seo/backlinks).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between on-page and off-page?">
    On-page is everything on your own site — Content Plan articles and technical fixes. Off-page is everything pointing back to your site — guest posts, press, and the backlinks they earn.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my backlinks and articles if I cancel?">
    Nothing we build for you is removed when a subscription ends. Backlinks and mentions we place live on third-party sites and are never taken down by us; articles published on your own site are yours outright. The one caveat: those pages belong to the sites that host them, so we cannot guarantee a third party keeps a page up forever. What does stop is new work — new articles, new placements, and replacing links that a host site later drops.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why were my articles published to a separate blog instead of my existing one?">
    That's intentional. If no blog is selected in the integration settings, Ryze creates a dedicated blog (on Shopify it's called "Articles") so a high article volume stays off your main pages. Prefer your existing blog? Pick it in the blog picker under the CMS integration's settings. Articles are indexed and interlinked by Ryze either way.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change or drop the keywords Ryze picked?">
    Yes. On **SEO → Keywords & Prompts**, open the actions menu on a keyword's row and click **Delete**. To add keywords, click **Explore Queries** or ask the AI Marketer chat, for example "add the keyword soundproof office pod". You can steer future picks through the **Brand** page (Identity and Context). See [Keywords & Prompts](/seo/keywords).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I see my backlinks, and when do they start?">
    Each link is under **SEO → Backlinks & PR**, on the **Backlinks** tab. Your overall authority over time is on **Analytics → Search Performance**, **SEO** tab, card **Authority & Backlinks**. You don't start anything: on Traffic Printer the first backlinks and guest posts usually land within 1–3 weeks, the press release within days. See [Backlinks](/seo/backlinks).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Ryze write in my language? Can I change the interface language?">
    Content — articles, ad copy, chat — comes in whatever language you brief it in. For articles, set the **Article language** and **Target market** under **SEO → Preferences → Content**. The dashboard itself is English only for now.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
