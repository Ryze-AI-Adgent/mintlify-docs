Guest posts are off-page content: articles written to live on other sites and link back to yours. Ryze drafts them for you, you approve, and the placement earns a backlink to your site.

Find them under **Backlinks & PR** in the SEO sidebar, alongside press releases and backlinks.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Ryze writes a guest-post draft — body, meta title, and meta description — built around your priority keywords and pages. Open it to read and edit it, or click **Improve This Placement** to have the agent review it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Click **Approve** on the post. Nothing is sent to a publication until you approve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submitted to publisher">
    The post is handed to the target publication. It's usually live within 24–48 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Published">
    The post is live. Its placement link — the live page pointing back to your site — appears under **Target publication**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If something blocks a post, it's marked **Failed** — open it to see the reason.

## Why they matter

Each placed guest post is a backlink. Backlinks are one of the strongest signals for domain authority — see [Backlinks](/seo/backlinks) for how Ryze tracks them.
