Backlinks are links from other sites pointing to yours. They're one of the strongest signals search engines use to judge authority, and Ryze builds them for you — you don't run any outreach yourself.

Find them under **Backlinks & PR** in the SEO sidebar, alongside guest posts and press releases.

## Where backlinks come from

Ryze builds backlinks through three channels:

- **Partner network** — Ryze runs a large network of partner sites, which places links back to your pages. This is the main driver — 50+ backlinks a month on the Traffic Printer plan, placed steadily through the month, with no outreach on your side.
- **Guest post placements** — each placed [guest post](/seo/guest-posts) links back to your site.
- **Press syndication** — each [press release](/seo/press) pickup across news outlets is another link.

Every link is recorded as a backlink with its anchor text, referring URL, and the page and keyword it points to.

Separately, [Backlink Exchange](/seo/backlink-exchange) swaps relevant links between sites in the Ryze network — on both SEO plans.

## The Backlinks & PR page

The page header reads **Mentions** — it's the same page as **Backlinks & PR** in the sidebar.

- **Timeline card** at the top — this cycle's press, backlinks and guest posts, with **X of Y live** for each and when they usually land. Press goes out within days; the first backlinks and guest posts land in 1–3 weeks, then more every few days until the cycle closes.
- **Tabs** — **All**, **Press**, **Guest Post** and **Backlinks**.
- **Backlinks** tab columns — **URL (referring)**, **Page (target)**, **Product**, **Keyword**, **Anchor** and **Status**.
- Click a press release or guest post to read, edit, approve or publish it. **Improve Mentions** asks the agent to review your drafts; it asks you before changing anything.
- The gear icon (**Settings**) opens [SEO → Preferences](/seo/preferences), where the **Press releases** and **Backlink exchange** switches live.

You don't need to start anything — placements land on their own through each cycle.

## Tracking authority

Beyond the links Ryze builds, it tracks your overall backlink profile from DataForSEO — your **domain rating**, total backlinks, and referring domains over time. Find it under **Analytics → Search Performance**, on the **SEO** tab, in the **Authority & Backlinks** card — see [Search Performance](/analytics/performance).
