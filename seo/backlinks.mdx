Backlinks are links from other sites pointing to yours. They're one of the strongest signals search engines use to judge authority, and Ryze builds them at scale — you don't run any outreach yourself.

Find them under **Mentions** in the SEO sidebar, alongside guest posts and press releases.

</Frame>

## Where backlinks come from

Ryze builds backlinks through three channels:

- **Partner network** — Ryze runs a large network of partner sites, which places links back to your pages at volume. This is the main driver — around 100 backlinks a month, with no guest posts or manual outreach required. See [Backlink Exchange](/seo/backlink-exchange).
- **Guest post placements** — each placed [guest post](/seo/guest-posts) links back to your site.
- **Press syndication** — each [press release](/seo/press) pickup across news outlets is another link.

Every link is recorded as a backlink with its anchor text, referring URL, and the page and keyword it points to.

## Tracking authority

Beyond the links Ryze builds, it tracks your overall backlink profile from DataForSEO — your **domain rating**, total backlinks, and referring domains over time, so you can watch authority climb as the network does its work.
