Target pages are the handful of pages Ryze commits to winning. They're the anchor for the whole engine: keywords are chosen to serve them, and anchored articles (Pool A) are written to drive traffic and links to them.

Find them under **Target Pages** in the SEO sidebar.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/seo-target-pages.png" alt="The Target Pages list in the SEO dashboard" />
</Frame>

## How Ryze picks them

Ryze doesn't ask you to guess. It scores candidate pages on real signals from your connected accounts:

- **Store revenue** — your best products by revenue (from Shopify order data).
- **Search clicks** — your top pages and freshly rising pages (from Google Search Console).
- **Organic traffic** — the pages already pulling rankings (from DataForSEO).

It aggregates those signals, then an AI step ranks the strongest candidates and selects the best set for the cycle. Pages picked in earlier cycles aren't re-picked, so coverage spreads over time instead of repeating.

## Status

Each target page is **active** or **inactive**. Active pages are the ones the engine is currently building content around.

<Note>
Target pages are picked automatically from your data each cycle. The better your store and Search Console are connected, the sharper the picks — connect those first.
</Note>
