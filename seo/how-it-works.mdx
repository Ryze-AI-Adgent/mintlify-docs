Ryze SEO is one engine with a clear chain: it learns your site, picks what to target, turns that into a plan of content, then produces and ships it. Everything downstream is anchored to the same foundation, so the articles and mentions you get are about the pages and keywords that actually matter for your business.

## The chain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Audit">
    Ryze pulls your data — Google Search Console (the queries and pages you already show up for), DataForSEO (your rankings, top pages, and competitors), and your store (your best products by revenue). This is the ground truth everything else is built on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick target pages">
    From that data it picks the **target pages** worth winning — the product and landing pages with the strongest signals. See [Target Pages](/seo/target-pages).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick keywords">
    It expands each target into keyword ideas, checks real search volume and difficulty, and selects the keywords worth chasing. See [Keywords](/seo/keywords).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan topics">
    Targets and keywords become a plan of article topics, split into pools (see below). Each topic lands with a real keyword, volume, and difficulty attached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate & publish">
    Each topic is written into a full article — body, meta, and a featured image — then published to your store or CMS. See [Programmatic Articles](/seo/programmatic).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Off-page">
    Alongside articles, Ryze produces guest posts and press releases, which earn the backlinks that build your domain authority. See [Guest Posts](/seo/guest-posts), [Press Releases](/seo/press), and [Backlinks](/seo/backlinks).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pool A and Pool B

Article topics are planned in two pools so coverage is both focused and broad:

- **Pool A — anchored.** Topics tied directly to your picked target pages, spread evenly across them. These are the articles meant to drive traffic and links to the pages you most want to rank.
- **Pool B — open catalog.** Topics that expand beyond your target pages into adjacent demand. Ryze mines your Search Console impressions, looks at gaps versus competitors, and brainstorms new angles across your wider catalog.

Together the pools keep Ryze from piling everything onto a handful of pages while still covering the broader space your buyers search.

## The monthly cycle

The engine runs on a monthly cycle tied to your billing date. Each cycle Ryze works toward a target volume of content, and only produces the **deficit** — what's still missing for the month — so it doesn't double up on work already done.

<Note>
Ryze never invents metrics. Search volume and difficulty come from real DataForSEO data; topics are built from your actual Search Console and store signals.
</Note>
