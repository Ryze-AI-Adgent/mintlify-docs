Ryze SEO is one engine with a clear chain: it learns your site, works out what to rank for, turns that into a plan of content, then produces and ships it. Everything downstream is anchored to the same foundation, so the articles and mentions you get are about the keywords and pages that actually matter for your business.

## The chain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Audit">
    Ryze pulls your data — Google Search Console (the queries and pages you already show up for), DataForSEO (your rankings, top pages, and competitors), and your store (your best products by revenue). This is the ground truth everything else is built on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find keywords">
    From that data Ryze derives keyword seeds for your business — anchored to what you sell and who you sell to — expands them through DataForSEO into real keyword ideas with actual volume and difficulty, and selects the ones worth chasing. See [Keywords](/seo/keywords).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan topics">
    Keywords become a plan of article topics, split into two pools (see below). Each topic lands with a real keyword, volume, and difficulty attached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate & publish">
    Each topic is written into a full article — body, meta, and a featured image — with internal links to your own key pages, then published to your store or CMS. See [Programmatic Articles](/seo/programmatic).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Off-page">
    Alongside articles, Ryze produces guest posts and press releases, and places reciprocal links across its network — the backlinks that build your domain authority. See [Guest Posts](/seo/guest-posts), [Press Releases](/seo/press), [Backlinks](/seo/backlinks), and [Backlink Exchange](/seo/backlink-exchange).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two pools

Article topics are planned in two pools so coverage is both focused and broad:

- **Pool A — priority-anchored.** Topics built around your priority keywords and the products that matter most — the articles meant to drive traffic and links to your key pages.
- **Pool B — open.** Topics that expand into adjacent demand. Ryze mines your Search Console impressions, looks at gaps versus competitors, and brainstorms new angles across your wider catalog.

Together the pools keep Ryze from piling everything onto a handful of topics while still covering the broader space your buyers search.

## The monthly cycle

The engine runs on a monthly cycle tied to your billing date. Each cycle Ryze works toward a target volume of content, and only produces the **deficit** — what's still missing for the month — so it doesn't double up on work already done. New articles are spread out across the month rather than dropped all at once.

See the whole plan laid out by day in the [content calendar](/seo/programmatic#content-calendar).
