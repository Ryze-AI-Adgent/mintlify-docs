Ryze SEO is one engine with a clear chain: it learns your site, works out what to rank for, turns that into a plan of content, then produces and ships it. Everything downstream is anchored to the same foundation, so the articles and mentions you get are about the keywords and pages that actually matter for your business.

## The chain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Audit">
    Ryze pulls your data — Google Search Console (the queries and pages you already show up for), live search data (your rankings, top pages, and competitors), and your store (your best products by revenue). This is the ground truth everything else is built on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find keywords">
    From that data Ryze derives keyword seeds for your business — anchored to what you sell and who you sell to — expands them with live search data into real keyword ideas with actual volume and difficulty, and selects the ones worth chasing. See [Keywords & Prompts](/seo/keywords).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan topics">
    Keywords become a plan of article topics, split across seven article types (see below). Each topic lands with a real keyword, volume, and difficulty attached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate & publish">
    Each topic is written into a full article — body, meta, and AI-generated images — with internal links to your own key pages, then published to your store or CMS on its scheduled date. See [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Off-page">
    On the Traffic Printer plan, Ryze also produces guest posts and a press release every month and places 50+ backlinks through its partner network. On both SEO plans, [Backlink Exchange](/seo/backlink-exchange) swaps relevant links between sites in the network. See [Guest Posts](/seo/guest-posts), [Press Releases](/seo/press) and [Backlinks](/seo/backlinks).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Article types

Topics are split across seven article types, so coverage is both focused and broad: **Best-N listicle** (about a quarter of the plan), **Alternatives**, **Vs comparison**, **Segment guide**, **Integration guide**, **Benchmark**, and **Question page** for the rest. Topics come from your keywords, your Search Console queries and pages, your competitors' top pages and your brand profile. See [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic#article-types).

## The monthly cycle

The engine runs on a monthly cycle tied to your billing date. Each cycle Ryze works toward up to 90 articles, and only produces the **deficit** — what's still missing for the cycle — so it doesn't double up on work already done. New articles are spread out across the cycle — up to 3 a day, on weekdays only — rather than dropped all at once.

See the whole plan laid out by day in the [content calendar](/seo/programmatic#content-calendar).
