Press releases are Ryze's PR channel. It writes a release and submits it to a newswire that syndicates to news outlets — each pickup a mention and a link back to your site.

Find them under **Backlinks & PR** in the SEO sidebar, alongside guest posts and backlinks.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Ryze writes a press release — title, summary, and body — around your company and a key page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and edit (optional)">
    Open the release to read it. Click **Edit** to change the text or the contact details, or click **Improve This Placement** to have the agent review it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submitted to publisher">
    While the **Press releases** switch (**SEO → Preferences → Publishing**) is on, a drafted release is submitted to the newswire automatically in the nightly run. Click **Publish now** on the release to send it right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Published">
    The newswire syndicates it to news outlets, usually within 24–48 hours after submission. Open the release to see **Published on N sites**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Editing a draft doesn't hold it back — the nightly run submits every drafted release. To hold a release, turn **Press releases** off in **SEO → Preferences → Publishing** (Ryze keeps drafting but stops sending) or delete the draft.
</Warning>

## Contact details

Open the release and click **Edit**. The **Contact information** card — **Contact name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Address**, **City** and **State** — is printed in the release's contact block. Check it before the release goes out.

## Restricted industries

The newswire's editors apply stricter rules to certain industries. For clients in those classes — designer-styled physical goods, supplements and wellness, cosmetic or surgical practices, and businesses serving crypto, gambling or cannabis — a ranking-style release ("Top 7 …", "Best … of 2026") is rejected for its format alone, while a plain company announcement about the same business passes review.

Ryze handles this for you: in a restricted class, releases are written as **announcements** — company news such as pricing, delivery terms, standards, or process — rather than rankings, and a release that was bounced for its format is rewritten the same way before it is resubmitted. You don't need to change anything; the draft you review will simply be shaped as an announcement.


## Why they matter

Press pickups generate backlinks across news sites, which strengthens your domain authority. See [Backlinks](/seo/backlinks).
