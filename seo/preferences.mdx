Open **SEO → Preferences** in the sidebar — the page header reads **Settings**. The gear icon (**Settings**) at the top of Content Plan and Backlinks & PR opens the same page. It has three tabs: **Content**, **Publishing** and **Blog Studio**.

Switches and dropdowns (**Article length**, **Publishing mode**, **Publish to**, **Article language**, **Target market**) save as soon as you change them. **Tone of voice** and **Custom writing instructions** share one **Save** button that appears once you edit them. **Override blog URL** saves when you click outside the field.

## Content

**Language & Region**
- **Article language** — every generated article is written in this language.
- **Target market** — search data and keyword research use this market.

**Writing**
- **Article length** — **Dynamic** (adapts to search intent), 1,200–1,600, 1,700–2,300 or 2,400–2,800 words.
- **Tone of voice** — e.g. "Confident, practical, no fluff".
- **Custom writing instructions** — anything the writer should always do or avoid.
- **Article examples** — reference posts the writer mimics for structure and style.

**Visuals**
- **AI images** — a cover and in-article images for every article. Turn it off to publish without images.
- **Image style** — Photo Realistic, Illustration, Sketch or Watercolor, or **Add your image** so generated images follow your own look.

Who you are, your products and audience live in [Brand](/agent/context), not here.

- **Publishing mode** — **Automatic** (content publishes on schedule; the default) or **Manual** (you publish drafts yourself). In Manual mode your articles don't place [Backlink Exchange](/seo/backlink-exchange) links, but you can still receive them.
- **Publish to** — which connected platform receives your articles.
- **Override blog URL** — only if readers see articles at a different address than the CMS, e.g. `https://www.example.com/blog`.
- **Backlink exchange** and **Press releases** — switches for these Backlinks & PR features. With **Press releases** on, drafted releases are submitted to the newswire automatically. See [Backlink Exchange](/seo/backlink-exchange) and [Press Releases](/seo/press).
- **Publishing platforms** — connect your CMS once and new articles get published for you. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview).

**Can I stop Ryze from creating drafts?** There's no switch that pauses the content plan. Set **Publishing mode** to **Manual** — drafts are still written, but nothing goes live until you click **Publish now**. Delete the planned topics or drafts you don't want, or move their scheduled dates, in [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic#can-i-stop-ryze-from-creating-drafts) — or ask the agent in chat to remove topics for you.

## Change settings from chat

You can also ask the agent in chat to change **Publishing mode**, **Publish to**, the blog URL, the **Press releases** and **Backlink exchange** switches, **Article language**, **Target market** and **Article length** — for example "switch publishing to manual". The agent can also update your **Tone of voice**, **Custom writing instructions**, **Article examples** and brand reference images when you ask. **AI images** and the preset **Image style** are changed on this page.

## Blog Studio

The **Blog Studio** tab opens a separate page for a blog hosted by Ryze — on a free Ryze subdomain or your own domain. It works once [Ryze Hosting](/integrations/ryze-hosting) is connected.

- **Design** — favicon, site name and meta description, theme, colors, fonts, corners and layout. **Match my website style** pulls colors, fonts and header/footer from your live site.
- **Code** — custom HTML and CSS for the head and footer of every blog page, plus switches to hide the built-in header or footer. Scripts and event handlers are removed for security, so analytics tags, ad pixels and chat widgets won't run here. To verify the blog in Search Console, use the **SEO** tab — it places the verification tag in the real page head.
- **SEO** — verify the blog in Search Console, link it from your main site, or turn on **Hide this blog from search engines** (under **Keep this blog out of Google**) if the same articles are also published on your own site.
