Open **SEO → Preferences** in the sidebar. The page has three tabs: **Content**, **Publishing** and **Blog Studio**. Switches and dropdowns save as soon as you change them; text fields have a **Save** button.

## Content

**Language & Region**
- **Article language** — every generated article is written in this language.
- **Target market** — search data and keyword research use this market.

**Writing**
- **Article length** — **Dynamic** (adapts to search intent), 1,200–1,600, 1,700–2,300 or 2,400–2,800 words.
- **Tone of voice** — e.g. "Confident, practical, no fluff".
- **Custom writing instructions** — anything the writer should always do or avoid.
- **Article examples** — reference posts the writer mimics for structure and style.

**Visuals**
- **AI images** — a cover and in-article images for every article. Turn it off to publish without images.
- **Image style** — Photo Realistic, Illustration, Sketch or Watercolor, or **Add your image** so generated images follow your own look.

Who you are, your products and audience live in [Brand](/agent/context), not here.

## Publishing

- **Publishing mode** — **Automatic** (content publishes on schedule) or **Manual** (you publish drafts yourself).
- **Publish to** — which connected platform receives your articles.
- **Override blog URL** — only if readers see articles at a different address than the CMS, e.g. `https://www.example.com/blog`.
- **Backlink exchange** and **Press releases** — switches for these Backlinks & PR features. See [Backlinks & PR](/seo/backlinks).
- **Publishing platforms** — connect your CMS once and new articles get published for you. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview).

## Blog Studio

The **Blog Studio** tab opens a separate page for a blog hosted by Ryze — on a free Ryze subdomain or your own domain. It works once [Ryze Hosting](/integrations/ryze-hosting) is connected.

- **Design** — favicon, site name and meta description, theme, colors, fonts, corners and layout. **Match my website style** pulls colors, fonts and header/footer from your live site.
- **Code** — your own markup and styles in the head and footer of every blog page.
- **SEO** — verify the blog in Search Console, link it from your main site, or turn on **Keep this blog out of Google** if the same articles are also published on your own site.
