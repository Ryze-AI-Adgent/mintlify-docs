Technical Audit checks the technical health of your site — the issues that can block Google and AI assistants from reading and ranking it — and an agent ships the fixes for you, so you don't have to touch code.

Find it under **SEO → Technical Audit** in the sidebar. The audit checks the top 300 pages from your Google Search Console, ranked by impressions — the pages that carry your traffic.

## Scores

- **Health Score** — 0–100 technical health of your site, weighted across SEO, LLM readiness and page speed.
- **SEO Optimization Score** — meta tags, links, sitemap, canonicals.
- **LLM Optimization Score** — llms.txt, AI crawlers, structured data: how well AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) can read and cite your site.
- **Page Speed Score** — Core Web Vitals from real Chrome users.

Click **What we check** for the full list of checks: llms.txt, JSON-LD schema, robots.txt, sitemap, SSL certificate, meta title and description, broken links, image optimization, page speed and canonical tags.

## Run a scan

The audit runs weekly on its own. Click **Run Scan** to run it anytime — it takes a few minutes, and you can close the page while it works.

## Read the issues

**Issues Detected** lists every problem with its severity — **Critical**, **Warning** or **Notice**. Switch between **Group by page** (each page with its score and issues, plus a row for site-wide checks) and **Group by issue** (each issue with the number of affected pages).

## Fix with the agent

Click **Fix with agent** on a page or an issue and the agent applies the fix for you across the affected pages — no manual editing. Important issues also show up in the **Needs you** list on Home.

If an issue is known and you accept it, open its actions menu and click **Acknowledge**; **Reopen** brings it back.
