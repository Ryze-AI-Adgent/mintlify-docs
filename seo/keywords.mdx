Keywords are what your content is built to rank for. Ryze generates and selects them automatically, grounded in real search data rather than guesswork.

Find them under **SEO → Keywords & Prompts** in the sidebar. The page has two tabs: **Keywords** (volume, difficulty, your Search Console position and its trend) and **Prompts** (the questions tracked on AI assistants — see [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility)).

## How keywords are generated

It's a three-stage pipeline:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Seed">
    Ryze derives a set of seed phrases from your site — what you sell, your industry, and the buyer vertical you serve — the starting points a buyer might search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand">
    Each seed is expanded through DataForSEO into hundreds of real keyword candidates, each carrying actual **search volume**, **difficulty**, **intent**, and CPC. Obvious noise (jobs, salaries, "how to become", PDFs) is filtered out, and very low-volume terms are dropped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select">
    An AI step picks the final set — the keywords most worth chasing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How keywords are chosen

The selection isn't "highest volume wins." Ryze weighs, in priority order:

1. **Real volume** — enough search demand to be worth it.
2. **On-topic** — a searcher would recognize your product as the answer.
3. **Commercial intent** — buying-intent keywords beat purely informational ones.
4. **Difficulty** — lower keyword difficulty preferred, so you can actually rank.
5. **Diversity** — no near-duplicate keywords, and no piling onto one head term.

The result is a focused set tied to your business and buyer vertical — quality over raw count.

## Add or remove keywords

- **Remove** — open the actions menu on a keyword's row and click **Delete**. The keyword leaves your list; this can't be undone.
- **Add or explore** — click **Explore Queries** and the agent reviews your current set — coverage, gaps, what's winnable — and suggests new keywords worth adding. It asks before changing anything. You can also just ask in any chat, for example "add the keyword soundproof office pod".

## Keep positions fresh

Click **Refresh** to pull fresh Search Console positions now. The **Settings** icon next to it sets **Auto-refresh** — **Off**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. After each refresh you get an email digest; you can turn it off in [Notifications](/workspaces/notifications).

<Note>
Volume and difficulty are pulled live from DataForSEO and reflect your workspace's country. Ryze never makes these numbers up.
</Note>
