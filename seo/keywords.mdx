Keywords are what your content is built to rank for. Ryze generates and selects them automatically, grounded in real search data rather than guesswork.

Find them under **SEO → Keywords & Prompts** in the sidebar (the page header reads **Queries** — it's the same page). The page has two tabs: **Keywords** (volume, difficulty, your Search Console position and its trend) and **Prompts** (the questions tracked on AI assistants — see [Prompts](#prompts) below and [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility)).

The row at the top shows **Keywords**, **Avg search volume**, **AI prompts** (n/50) and **AI visibility**.

## How keywords are generated

It's a three-stage pipeline:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Seed">
    Ryze derives a set of seed phrases from your site — what you sell, your industry, and the buyer vertical you serve — the starting points a buyer might search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand">
    Each seed is expanded with live search data into hundreds of real keyword candidates, each carrying actual **search volume**, **difficulty**, **intent**, and CPC. Obvious noise (jobs, salaries, "how to become", PDFs) is filtered out, and very low-volume terms are dropped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select">
    An AI step picks the final set — the keywords most worth chasing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How keywords are chosen

The selection isn't "highest volume wins." Ryze weighs, in priority order:

1. **Real volume** — enough search demand to be worth it.
2. **On-topic** — a searcher would recognize your product as the answer.
3. **Commercial intent** — buying-intent keywords beat purely informational ones.
4. **Difficulty** — lower keyword difficulty preferred, so you can actually rank.
5. **Diversity** — no near-duplicate keywords, and no piling onto one head term.

The result is a focused set tied to your business and buyer vertical — quality over raw count.

## Add or remove keywords

- **Remove** — open the actions menu on a keyword's row and click **Delete**. The keyword leaves your list; this can't be undone.
- **Add or explore** — click **Explore Queries** and the agent reviews your current set — coverage, gaps, what's winnable — and suggests new keywords worth adding. It asks before changing anything. You can also just ask in any chat, for example "add the keyword soundproof office pod".

## Prompts

Prompts are the questions your buyers ask AI assistants. During setup Ryze adds starter prompts — 5 topics with 5 prompts each — and you can track up to 50.

- Prompts are grouped by topic. To add more, click **Add**: pick a topic or create a **New topic…**, then enter one prompt per line — or use **Suggest prompts**. Details: [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility).
- To change a prompt, open its **⋯** menu and click **Edit** or **Delete**.

## Keep positions fresh

On the **Keywords** tab, **Refresh** pulls fresh Search Console positions now. On the **Prompts** tab, **Refresh** re-asks all your prompts on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity — you confirm first. The **Settings** icon next to it sets **Auto-refresh** for both — **Off**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. After each refresh you get an email digest; you can turn it off in [Notifications](/workspaces/notifications).

<Note>
Volume and difficulty are pulled live from search data and reflect your workspace's country. Ryze never makes these numbers up.
</Note>
