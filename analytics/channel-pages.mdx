Each marketing channel has its own page. Click **Website**, **Ads**, **SEO**, **Local** or **Social** under **Channels** in the sidebar: the page opens and the section expands to show its other items. **Agentic** opens [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility), which is built the same way.

| Page | What it shows | Needs | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Website** | Visits to your site and where they come from | Google Analytics 4, PostHog or the Ryze Pixel | Every plan |
| **Ads** | Spend and results by day | Meta Ads or Google Ads | Paid Ads |
| **SEO** | Clicks and orders from Google | Google Search Console (and Google Analytics for orders) | SEO plans |
| **Local** | Views and actions on your Google listing, and your reviews | Google Business Profile | Traffic Printer or the Local package |
| **Social** | Views of your posts, followers and visits from social | A social account | Social package |

Until the main source is connected, the page shows what it needs and a **Connect** button. See [Plans & pricing](/plans/overview#channel-packages) for the packages.

## What every channel page has

- **The headline** — one line with the main number for the period, for example how many visits or orders a channel brought. Where the page has a **Show in the headline** (or **Show on the … cards**) picker, use it to switch the number.
- **Cards** — the same number split by source, page group, platform or network, each compared with the previous period (**vs the previous period**).
- **Period** — pick the dates at the top of the page.
- **Sync now** — pulls the latest data from the connected account and shows when it last synced.
- **Set up metrics** — on the pages that offer it, the agent saves what counts as a result on your site (orders and revenue, leads, bookings or demos), and the headline can then show it. **Add metric** adds another one.
- **Ask the agent** — the box under the cards sends a request straight to the agent, for example a new article, a campaign or a review reply.
- **This week** — what needs you on this channel, such as approvals. On Website, Ads, SEO and Local, when nothing does, click **Find improvements**: Ryze checks the channel and new suggestions show up in a few minutes, or you see *Checked today. Nothing worth changing right now.* Looking for improvements uses credits — without them you'll see **Top up credits**.

## Website

The visits to your site, split into **Search**, **AI assistants**, **Ads** and **Other**. **Show in the headline** switches between **Visits**, **Revenue**, **Orders** and the metrics you saved. **Website health** shows what the [Technical Audit](/seo/optimizations) found, with **Open the full audit** on an SEO plan.

Ryze reads visits from Google Analytics 4 or PostHog when one of them is connected with a site picked. Otherwise it reads them from the [Ryze Pixel](/paid-ads/ryze-pixel). With the pixel, visits show the same day; with your analytics, about two days later. **Revenue** and **Orders** are offered only when your analytics records them, not with the pixel.

## Ads

What your ad spend brought: purchases, conversions or clicks. There's one card per connected ad platform, and **Show on the platform cards** switches between **Clicks**, **Spend**, **Purchases** and **Conversions**. **Active managed campaigns** lists your running [Managed Campaigns](/paid-ads/managed-campaigns), with **Open campaigns** and **New campaign**.

## SEO

The clicks Google sends you and, with Google Analytics, the orders and revenue they bring. The cards split your pages into **Homepage**, **Articles by Ryze**, **Products**, **Blog** and **Other pages**; **Show on the page group cards** switches between **Revenue**, **Orders**, **Clicks**, **Impressions** and **Visits**. **Latest articles** shows the newest articles from your [Content Plan](/seo/programmatic), with **Open calendar**.

## Local

What your Google listing brings you, for one location at a time — with several locations, pick one in **Location**. **Show in the headline** switches between **Actions** and **Views**. The cards show **Calls**, **Directions**, **Website clicks**, **Bookings**, **Messages**, **Food orders**, and views from **Google Search** and **Google Maps**. Google reports these numbers 3 to 4 days late.

**Reviews** shows your **Rating**, the number of reviews and how many are **Waiting for a reply**. Reviews waiting for a reply also show under **This week** with **Reply**; **Open reviews** opens the full list. See [Local SEO](/seo/local-seo).

## Social

The views of your posts and the visits they send to your site, with one card per connected network and its followers. **Show in the headline** switches between **Visits**, **Views**, **Reach**, **Engagement**, **Clicks** and **Posts**. **Latest posts** shows what you published, with **Open calendar**. See [Social](/social/overview).
