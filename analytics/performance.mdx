Open **Analytics** in the sidebar. It has two tabs:

- **Dashboards** — the **Built-in** dashboards (**Ads Performance**, **Search Performance** and **Web Analytics**) and **Your dashboards**.
- **Widgets** — every chart and table you can put on a dashboard: the ones made by Ryze and the ones you or the agent saved.

Every dashboard pulls data from your connected accounts. If an account is connected but nothing is chosen to sync yet, the dashboard says so and offers **Choose accounts** — see [Integrations](/integrations/overview).

## Period and sync

Pick the period at the top of a dashboard: **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days**, **Last 28 days**, **Last 90 days**, **This month**, **Last month**, or your own dates in the calendar. Numbers are compared with the same span before.

**Sync now** pulls the latest data from your connected accounts; the button shows when the data was last synced. If you've just synced, it tells you how many minutes are left before you can sync again.

## Built-in dashboards

### Search Performance

Part of the SEO plans; needs [Google Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console). Three tabs:

- **SEO** — clicks and impressions over time, **Domain Rating**, **Clicks by country**, **Where your queries rank**, **Branded vs non-branded**, **Authority & Backlinks** and **Domain Rating over time**.
- **GEO** — visits that come to your site from AI assistants: **AI Sessions**, **AI traffic**, **AI Chats Traffic** and **AI Landing Pages**. Needs Google Analytics. Which assistants name you and what to fix is on the [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility) page (**Agentic** in the sidebar).
- **Content** — how your published articles perform: totals, **Traffic from articles** and a table per article.

### Ads Performance

Your connected ad accounts — Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, Microsoft Ads, TikTok, Snapchat, Reddit, Pinterest and OpenAI Ads. If none are connected, connect one in [Integrations](/integrations/overview). Four tabs:

- **Overview** — spend, clicks, CPC and CTR by day, **Channels** and **Top movers**.
- **Campaigns** — **Lowest CPC**, **Best CTR** and **Average cost per click**; spend by platform per day; the **Campaigns** table with **All** and **Active only**.
- **Creatives** — your running ads with their numbers.
- **Portfolio View** — every account side by side: spend, clicks and CPC by account, the **All accounts** table and **Account funnels**.

### Web Analytics

The dashboard of the [Ryze Pixel](/paid-ads/ryze-pixel) on your site, on every plan. Two tabs:

- **Web Analytics** — visitors, page views and sessions, traffic by day, visitors by country, **Pages**, **Sources** (by channel, referrer, UTM source, medium and campaign), devices, browsers and operating systems.
- **Web Vitals** — page speed from real visitors: LCP, INP, CLS, FCP and TTFB by page and by day.

## Your own dashboards

A built-in dashboard can't be changed. To make your own:

- **Duplicate** a built-in dashboard from its menu and change the copy, or
- click **New dashboard** on the Dashboards tab — the agent panel opens next to it, so you can describe what you want and the agent builds it. You can also just ask the agent in any chat, for example "make me a dashboard of Meta spend and leads by campaign".

On your own dashboard, click **Customize** to add tabs (**Add tab**), add widgets (**Add widget**, or drag one in from **Widgets**), move and resize cards, then **Save**. A dashboard holds up to 24 cards. **Edit details** changes its name and description; **Delete** removes the dashboard but keeps its widgets.

On the **Widgets** tab, **New widget** opens the widget editor: give it a name, write the query (or ask the agent to write it), press **Run** to see the result, then save it and **Add to dashboard**. A widget made by Ryze can't be changed — use **Save as a copy** and edit the copy.

## Pin a dashboard to the sidebar

Open a dashboard's menu and click **Pin to sidebar** — it appears under **Analytics** in the sidebar for everyone in the workspace. Up to 8 dashboards can be pinned; **Unpin from sidebar** removes one.

To dig into a number, ask the agent in **New chat** — it reads the same data. See [Chat with the AI Marketer](/agent/chat).
