Both dashboards live under **Analytics** in the sidebar and pull data from your connected accounts. In the sidebar they're called **Search Performance** and **Ads Performance**; each page opens with the title **Dashboard**.

Click **Refresh** at the top to pull the latest numbers; the line under the title shows when they were last updated. You can refresh again 5 minutes after the last refresh — until then the button is greyed out and reads **Refresh in 5m**.

**Analytics → Web Analytics** is a different page: the dashboard of the Ryze Pixel on your site. See [Ryze Pixel](/paid-ads/ryze-pixel).

## Search Performance

Open **Analytics → Search Performance**. It's part of the SEO plans, covers the last 28 days, and needs [Google Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console) — without it the page asks you to connect it.

Three tabs:

- **SEO** — four numbers on top: **Impressions · 28 D**, **Organic Clicks · 28 D**, **Domain Rating** and **Health Score** (from your latest Technical Audit). Below them: clicks and impressions over time, **Clicks by Country**, **Position Distribution** (Top 3 / 4–10 / 11–20 / 21+), **Branded vs Non-branded** and **Authority & Backlinks**.
- **GEO** — how AI assistants answer your buyers' questions and whether they name you, plus **AI Traffic** and **AI Landing Pages** — visits that come to your site from AI assistants. Needs Google Analytics. This is the same view as **Analytics → AI Visibility** — see [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility).
- **Content** — how your published articles perform: published count, clicks, impressions, how many are indexed and ranking, and a table per article.

## Ads Performance

Open **Analytics → Ads Performance**. It's part of Paid Ads and shows the last 30 days across your connected ad accounts. If none are connected, click **Open Integrations** — see [Integrations](/integrations/overview).

Use the account selector at the top to choose which accounts to include — up to 30. Four tabs:

- **Overview** — **Spend**, **Results**, **Best CPA** and **Clicks**; three trend charts (**Spend & ROAS**, **Conversions & rate**, **Clicks & CTR**); **ROAS by day & hour** and **Spend by day & hour** (hourly data from Google and Meta only); **Channels**; **Top movers**; **Cost per conversion**.
- **Campaigns** — **Active campaigns**, **Best CPA**, **Conversion rate** and **Avg CPC**; **Daily conversions by platform** and **Daily spend by platform**; the **Top campaigns** table.
- **Creatives** — your running ads with spend, CTR, CPC and results, as cards or a table. Needs a Meta or LinkedIn account with running ads.
- **Portfolio View** — every selected account side by side: **Spend by account**, **ROAS by account**, **CPA by account**, **Account funnels** (impressions → clicks → conversions per account) and the **All accounts** table.

If accounts report in different currencies, totals are shown in one currency and the page tells you which accounts are left out.

To dig into a number, ask the agent in **New chat** — it reads the same data. See [Chat with the AI Marketer](/agent/chat).
