title: "Search and Ads Performance"
description: "Live dashboards for your organic search and your ad accounts, under Analytics in the sidebar."
---

Both dashboards live under **Analytics** in the sidebar and pull data from your connected accounts. Click **Refresh** at the top to pull the latest numbers; the line under the title shows when they were last updated.

## Search Performance

Open **Analytics → Search Performance**. It's part of the SEO plans and needs [Google Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console) — without it the page asks you to connect it.

Three tabs:

- **SEO** — organic clicks, impressions and position over time, clicks by country, position distribution (Top 3 / 4–10 / 11–20 / 21+), branded vs non-branded clicks, and authority & backlinks history.
- **GEO** — how AI assistants answer your buyers' questions and whether they name you. Needs Google Analytics. This is the same view as **Analytics → AI Visibility** — see [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility).
- **Content** — how your published articles perform: published count, clicks, impressions, how many are indexed and ranking, and a table per article.

## Ads Performance

Open **Analytics → Ads Performance**. It's part of Paid Ads and shows the last 30 days across your connected ad accounts. If none are connected, click **Open Integrations** — see [Integrations](/integrations/overview).

Use the account selector at the top to choose which accounts to include. Four tabs:

- **Overview** — spend, results, best CPA and clicks; top movers; cost per conversion; daily spend and conversions by platform; top campaigns; channels; ROAS and spend by day & hour (hourly data from Google and Meta only).
- **Campaigns** — active campaigns, best CPA, conversion rate, average CPC, and the full campaigns table.
- **Creatives** — your running ads with spend, CTR, CPC and results, as cards or a table. Needs a Meta or LinkedIn account with running ads.
- **Portfolio View** — every selected account side by side: spend, ROAS and CPA by account, and an all-accounts table.

If accounts report in different currencies, totals are shown in one currency and the page tells you which accounts are left out.

To dig into a number, ask the agent in **New chat** — it reads the same data.
