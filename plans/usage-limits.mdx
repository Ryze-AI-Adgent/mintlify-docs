Ryze meters usage in **credits**. Credits are consumed by work the agent completes on your behalf.

## What consumes credits

Work that lands — an article generated and published, a fix shipped, a campaign action executed, a report produced.

## What does not consume credits

- **Failed runs.** If work does not complete, you are not charged for it.
- **System errors.** Faults on the Ryze side are never billed.
- **Internal tool calls.** Steps the agent takes to reach a result are not billed separately.

You are charged for outcomes, not attempts. See [Credits & usage](/agent/credits) for how consumption is reported.

## Published output limits

| plan | output |
|---|---|
| Paid Ads Autopilot | Unlimited ad accounts |
| SEO Autopilot | 100+ pages generated per month |
| Traffic Printer | 100+ pages, plus 100+ backlinks per month |
| Ecom Autopilot | Combined ads and SEO packages |

## How content volume is actually paced

The SEO engine runs on a **monthly cycle tied to your billing date** and works toward a target volume for that cycle. Two consequences people find surprising:

- **It produces only the deficit.** If work is already done for the month, it does not duplicate it to hit a number.
- **Output is spread across the month**, not delivered in a batch. Week one is a fraction of the monthly total. See [your first 7 days](/first-week).

## Rate of change, not just volume

Some limits are about timing rather than quantity:

| activity | cadence |
|---|---|
| Rank tracking | Daily |
| AI visibility summaries | Weekly, Wednesdays |
| Technical fixes | Shipped within hours of approval |
| Content | Monthly cycle, spread across the month |

See [monitoring interval](/ai-visibility/monitoring-interval).

## Workspaces and accounts

A workspace holds one brand's accounts. You can connect multiple accounts for the same platform within a workspace — several Google Ads logins, several GA4 properties. Agencies typically run one workspace per client. See [agency and multi-workspace](/plans/agency-multi-workspace).

## If you need more

If your catalogue or content requirements exceed a plan's published output, contact [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) rather than assuming a hard cap. Requirements above the standard plans are handled case by case.
