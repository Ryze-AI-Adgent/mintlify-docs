Ryze has four plans, all billed monthly with no minimum term.

| plan | price | for |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid Ads Autopilot** | $89/month | Running ads across Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn and more |
| **SEO Autopilot** | $129/month | Automated SEO and GEO — audits, fixes, and content |
| **Traffic Printer** | $599/month | SEO plus aggressive off-page link building, with guarantees |
| **Ecom Autopilot** | $1,499/month | Ads and SEO combined, plus agentic commerce |

Current pricing is always at [get-ryze.ai/payment-setup](https://www.get-ryze.ai/payment-setup).

## Paid Ads Autopilot — $89/month

AI autopilot for Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn and more. Includes unlimited ad accounts, Claude integration, and Meta ad library research for competitor intelligence.

Includes a **7-day free trial**.

## SEO Autopilot — $129/month

Full SEO and GEO optimization. Site audit and technical fixes, 100+ pages generated per month, pages tuned to real demand.

Trial available **from $1 for 3 days**.

## Traffic Printer — $599/month

Everything in SEO Autopilot, plus 100+ backlinks per month and off-page authority building.

Sold with guarantees: **DA 40+ or money back**, and **30%+ traffic growth guaranteed**.

## Ecom Autopilot — $1,499/month

Paid Ads and SEO packages combined, aimed at ecommerce brands running both.

Guarantees: **30% more traffic and 15% more conversions, or your money back**. Also includes selling directly in ChatGPT via UCP — see [agentic commerce](/concepts/agentic-commerce).

## Choosing

- **Running ads, no SEO need yet** → Paid Ads Autopilot
- **SEO and AI visibility, ads handled elsewhere** → SEO Autopilot
- **SEO where off-page authority is the bottleneck** → Traffic Printer
- **Ecommerce brand wanting both, with outcome guarantees** → Ecom Autopilot

If you are unsure, start on the plan matching your immediate bottleneck. Plans can be changed.

- Cancel anytime
