Ryze has five plans. Paid Ads and SEO are separate products — you can run one or both.

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid Ads** | $89/month | Running Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn and other ad accounts with the AI agent |
| **Agency** | $279/month | Agencies running Paid Ads for many clients |
| **SEO Starter** | $129/month | SEO and AI-search content for one website |
| **Traffic Printer** | $599/month | SEO plus backlinks and off-page authority |
| **Shopify CRO** | $1,499/month | Done-for-you A/B tests on your store, plus SEO and Paid Ads |

Subscribe, switch, or cancel on **Organization Settings → Billing**. Prices are in USD.

## Trials and annual billing

- **Paid Ads** and **Agency**: 7-day free trial with a one-time $1 setup fee.
- **SEO Starter**: 3-day free trial with a one-time $1 setup fee.
- **Traffic Printer** and **Shopify CRO** start without a trial.
- **Annual billing** saves 30% in the first year. After that, the plan renews at 12× the monthly price.

The exact trial length and amount are shown before you confirm. See the [Billing FAQ](/billing/faq) for how trials convert and how to cancel.

## Shopify CRO — $1,499/month

- Done-for-you A/B tests on your store — designed, applied and measured by the Ryze team
- Results on the **A/B Testing** page in the app

This plan isn't self-serve: open **A/B Testing** in the app and click **Talk to us**.

## More than one website

An SEO plan covers one website. To run SEO on another site, open its workspace and click **Add SEO for this website** — it's charged at your plan's price, prorated for the current period, and renews and cancels together with your subscription. Extra websites can be added after the trial converts to a paid plan.

Paid Ads covers up to 3 workspaces; for more, upgrade to Agency.

## Credits

The AI agent runs on credits. Your plan adds a credit allowance every billing period, and you can buy more anytime on the **Billing** page. See [Credits & Usage](/agent/credits).
