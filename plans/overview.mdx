Ryze has five plans. Paid Ads and SEO are separate products — you can run one or both.

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid Ads** | $89/month | Running Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn and other ad accounts with the AI agent |
| **Agency** | $279/month | Agencies running Paid Ads for many clients |
| **SEO Starter** | $129/month | SEO and AI-search content for one website |
| **Traffic Printer** | $599/month | SEO plus backlinks and off-page authority |
| **Shopify CRO** | $1,499/month | Done-for-you A/B tests on your store, plus SEO and Paid Ads |

Subscribe, switch, or cancel on **Organization Settings → Billing** — see [Manage your subscription](/billing/manage-subscription). Prices are in USD.

## Trials and annual billing

- **Paid Ads** and **Agency**: 7-day free trial.
- **SEO Starter**: 3-day free trial.
- **Traffic Printer** and **Shopify CRO**: no trial.
- **Annual billing** saves 30% in the first year. After that, the plan renews at 12× the monthly price.

The exact trial length and price are shown before you confirm. What happens when the trial ends, and how to cancel, is explained in the [Billing FAQ](/billing/faq).

## More than one website

An SEO plan covers one website. When you subscribe with several workspaces, Ryze asks **Which website is this plan for?** — SEO runs on the website you pick.

To run SEO on another site, open its workspace and click **Add SEO for this website** — it's charged at your plan's price, prorated for the current period, and renews and cancels together with your subscription. Extra websites can be added after the trial converts to a paid plan.

**Cancel plan** on an SEO subscription ends SEO for every website on it. To stop SEO for one website only, or to move your SEO plan to a different website, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) from your account address and we'll adjust the subscription for you.

Paid Ads covers up to 3 workspaces; for more, upgrade to Agency.

## Upgrade SEO Starter to Traffic Printer

On **Organization Settings → Billing**, click **Unlock Mentions** on the Traffic Printer card. It adds 50+ high-quality backlinks (DR 30+), 3 editorial guest posts and 100+ PR placements every month. The difference is $470/month or $5,640/year for each website on your SEO plan. You're charged today for the rest of the current period; then it renews with your SEO plan and covers all your websites. The amount is shown on the button before you pay.

On the SEO Starter free trial, unlocking Mentions ends the trial and starts your subscription today.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing |

| **Agency** | 3,000 / month | 24,000 / year |
| **SEO Starter** | 750 / month | 9,000 / year |
| **Traffic Printer** | 1,000 / month | 12,000 / year |

Annual plans get the whole year's credits at the start of the year. Credits never expire and carry over. See [Credits & Usage](/agent/credits).
