Ryze has five plans. Paid Ads and SEO are separate products — you can run one or both. On top of them, three [channel packages](#channel-packages) — **Agentic**, **Local** and **Social** — open one section of the app each.

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid Ads** | $89/month | Running Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn and other ad accounts with the AI agent |
| **Agency** | $279/month | Agencies running Paid Ads for many clients |
| **SEO Starter** | $129/month | SEO and AI-search content for one website |
| **Traffic Printer** | $599/month | SEO plus backlinks and off-page authority |
| **Shopify CRO** | $1,499/month | Done-for-you A/B tests on your store, plus SEO and Paid Ads |

Subscribe, switch, or cancel on **Organization Settings → Billing** — see [Manage your subscription](/billing/manage-subscription). Prices are in USD.

## Trials and annual billing

- **Paid Ads** and **Agency**: 7-day free trial.
- **SEO Starter**: 3-day free trial.
- **Traffic Printer** and **Shopify CRO**: no trial.
- **Annual billing** saves 30% in the first year. After that, the plan renews at 12× the monthly price: Paid Ads $1,068/year, Agency $3,348/year, SEO Starter $1,548/year, Traffic Printer $7,188/year.

The exact trial length and price are shown before you confirm. What happens when the trial ends, and how to cancel, is explained in the [Billing FAQ](/billing/faq).

## Channel packages

Three sections of the app are sold as packages, each for one website:

| Package | Price | What it opens | Also included in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agentic** | $49/month | **Agentic** — [AI Visibility](/seo/ai-visibility): AI visibility in ChatGPT and other AI engines, tracked prompts with the answers and cited sources, and the AI readiness check of your website | SEO Starter and Traffic Printer |

- Packages are billed monthly, and you can cancel anytime. They don't add credits — the agent's work in these sections uses your plan's credits or credits you buy.
- Subscribe on **Organization Settings → Billing**, under **Channels**, or click a locked section in the sidebar: it shows **Unlock Agentic**, **Unlock Local** or **Unlock Social** with the package and **Subscribe**. When a plan includes the package, its card says **Included in** and the plan's name.
- On SEO Starter, the **Unlock Local** window also offers **Upgrade** to Traffic Printer, which adds Agentic, Local and Mentions to your SEO plan and is charged pro-rata today.
- To use a package on another website, click **Add a website** on its card on the Billing page and pick the website — each extra website is charged at the package price and renews and cancels together with your subscription.

## More than one website

An SEO plan covers one website. When you subscribe with several workspaces, Ryze asks **Which website is this plan for?** — the plan runs on the website you pick.

To run SEO on another site, open its workspace: it shows *This workspace isn't on your plan*. Click **Add this website** (the button shows the price) — it's charged at your plan's price, prorated for the current period, and renews and cancels together with your subscription. Extra websites can be added after the trial converts to a paid plan.

Each extra website also adds your plan's credits to your balance (SEO Starter: 750 a month or 9,000 a year; Traffic Printer: 1,000 a month or 12,000 a year). They're plan credits, so they reset on each renewal like the rest of your plan's credits.

**Cancel plan** on an SEO subscription ends SEO for every website on it. To stop SEO for one website only, or to move your SEO plan to a different website, email [support@get-ryze.ai](mailto:support@get-ryze.ai) from your account address and we'll adjust the subscription for you.

Paid Ads covers up to 3 workspaces; for more, upgrade to Agency.

## Upgrade SEO Starter to Traffic Printer

On **Organization Settings → Billing**, click **Unlock Mentions** on the Traffic Printer card. It adds 50 high-quality backlinks with a domain rating of 30+, 3 editorial guest posts and 100+ PR placements every month, and Local comes with Traffic Printer too. The difference is $470/month or $5,640/year for each website on your SEO plan. You're charged today for the rest of the current period; then it renews with your SEO plan and covers all your websites. The amount is shown on the button before you pay.

On the SEO Starter free trial, unlocking Mentions ends the trial and starts your subscription today.

## Credits

The AI agent runs on credits. Your plan adds a credit allowance every billing period, and you can buy more anytime on the **Billing** page.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid Ads** | 750 / month | 9,000 / year |
| **Agency** | 3,000 / month | 36,000 / year |
| **SEO Starter** | 750 / month | 9,000 / year |
| **Traffic Printer** | 1,000 / month | 12,000 / year |

Annual plans get the whole year's credits at the start of the year. Plan credits reset on your billing date: every month on monthly plans, once a year on annual plans. Credits you buy on top never expire. See [Credits & Usage](/agent/credits).
